Three-Layer Rhubarb Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

1st layer (crust)

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup margarine (cold)

2nd layer (filling)

  • 5 cups rhubarb, chopped
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup cream
  • ¼ tsp salt

3rd layer (meringue)

  • 6 egg whites
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • Dash of salt
  • Slivered almonds (for sprinkling)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
  2. 1st layer: Mix flour, sugar, and margarine until combined. Press the mixture evenly into the bottom of a baking pan. Bake at 300°F for 10 minutes. Remove from oven.
  3. 2nd layer: In a bowl, combine chopped rhubarb, egg yolks, sugar, flour, cream, and salt. Pour this mixture over the partially baked first layer. Return to oven and bake at 300°F for 40–45 minutes, or until the filling is set and firm. Remove from oven and let cool slightly.
  4. 3rd layer: Whip egg whites with a dash of salt until soft peaks form. Gradually add ¾ cup sugar and continue whipping until stiff peaks form. Fold in vanilla. Spread the meringue over the warm second layer, sprinkle with slivered almonds.
  5. Return to the oven and bake at 300°F until the meringue is nicely browned, about 8–12 minutes. Watch closely to avoid over-browning.
  6. Cool completely before slicing to allow layers to set. Serve at room temperature.
