Fluffy Pancakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 3½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, beat the egg and mix with milk and melted butter.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Pour ¼ cup of batter for each pancake onto the griddle.
  6. Cook until bubbles form and edges are dry, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  7. Serve warm with syrup, fruit, or toppings of your choice.
