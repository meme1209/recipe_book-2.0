Orange Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 7 cups margarine
  • 7 cups sugar
  • 14 eggs
  • 7 cups buttermilk
  • 1 ¾ cups orange peel
  • 14 cups flour
  • 7 tsp baking soda

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and sugar. Beat in eggs, buttermilk and orange peel. Combine flour and baking soda; add to creamed mixture just until blended. Fill muffin cups 2/3 full. Bake @ 350° for 18-20 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean.
  2. Notes
  3. This recipe is big enough for breakfast.
