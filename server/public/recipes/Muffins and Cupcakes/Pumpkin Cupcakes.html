Pumpkin Cupcakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cups sugar
  • ¾ cup vegetable oil
  • 15 oz solid pack pumpkin
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Filling
  • 1 TBSP cornstarch
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup shortening
  • 1/8 cup butter (no substitutes)
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • ¼ tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Combine the eggs, sugar, oil and pumpkin. Combine the flour, baking soda, salt, baking powder and cinnamon; add to pumpkin mixture and beat until well mixed. Fill muffin cups 2/3 full. Bake @ 350° for 18-22 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely.
  2. For filling, combine cornstarch and milk in small saucepan until smooth. Bring to boil, stirring constantly. Remove from heat; cool to room temperature. Cream shortening, butter and icing sugar. Beat in vanilla. Gradually add cornstarch mixture, beating until light and fluffy. Using a sharp knife, cut a 1” circle 1” deep in the top of each cupcake. Carefully remove tops and set aside. Spoon filling into cupcakes. Replace tops.
  3. Yield: 20 cupcakes
