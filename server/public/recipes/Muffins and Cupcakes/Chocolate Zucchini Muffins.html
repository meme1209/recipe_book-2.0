Chocolate Zucchini Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups 15 cups 30 cups flour
  • 3 cups 15 cups 30 cups sugar
  • ½ cup 2 ½ cups 5 cups baking cocoa
  • 1 ½ tsp 2 ½ TBSP 5 TBSP baking powder
  • 1 ½ tsp 2 ½ TBSP 5 TBSP baking soda
  • 1 tsp 5 tsp 10 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp 1 ¼ tsp 2 ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 4 20 40 eggs
  • 1 ½ cups 7 ½ cups 15 cups vegetable oil
  • 2 TBSP 10 TBSP 1 ¼ cup margarine, melted
  • 1 ½ tsp 2 ½ TBSP 5 TBSP vanilla
  • 1 ½ tsp 2 ½ TBSP 5 TBSP almond extract
  • 3 cups 15 cups 30 cups grated zucchini

Instructions

  1. Combine first 7 ingredients. Combine the eggs, oil, margarine and extracts; mix well. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened. Fold in zucchini. Pour into 3 loaf pans. Bake @ 350° for 55-60 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean.
  2. Notes
  3. Middle recipe is x5. Use for Tuesday.
  4. Large recipe is x10. Use for Thursday.
