Peach Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 20 cups flour (+1 or 7 TBSP) divided
  • 1 10 TBSP baking powder
  • 1 ½ 15 tsp cinnamon (or 5 TBSP)
  • ¾ 7 ½ cups brown sugar, packed
  • ½ 5 tsp salt
  • 2 20 eggs
  • 1 10 cups plain yogurt or sour cream
  • ¼ 2 ½ cups butter, melted
  • 2 20 tsp vanilla
  • 1 10 cups peaches, cubed
  • Topping
  • ¼ 2 ½ cup brown sugar
  • 3 30 TBSP flour
  • ½ 5 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 20 TBSP cold butter
  • Glaze
  • ½ 5 cups icing sugar
  • ½ 5 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ 5 tsp vanilla
  • 2-3 20-30 TBSP heavy cream
  • Combine 2 cups flour, baking powder, cinnamon, brown sugar and salt. Beat eggs, yogurt, melted butter and vanilla. Combine peaches and remaining TBSP flour; tossing to coat. Stir wet ingredients into dry just until moistened. Fold in peaches. Fill muffin cups.
  • For topping, stir together dry ingredients. Mix in butter until crumbly. Sprinkle over muffins. Bake @ 350° for 18-20 minutes or until muffins test done in the center. Cool.
  • For glaze mix together all ingredients, adding cream until desired consistency is reached. Drizzle over muffins.
