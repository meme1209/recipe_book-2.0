Orange Applesauce Cupcakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 TBSP butter or margarine, softened
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp grated orange peel
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • Frosting
  • ¼ cup margarine, softened
  • 2 cups icing sugar
  • 1 ½ tsp grated orange peel
  • 2-4 tsp orange juice

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and brown sugar. Add egg and beat well. Beat in applesauce, vanilla and orange peel. Combine the flour, baking powder, salt and baking soda. Add to creamed mixture. Fill muffin cups half full. Bake @ 350° for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Cool for 10 minutes before removing from pan to wire rack to cool completely.
  2. For frosting, cream margarine and icing sugar. Add icing sugar and enough orange juice to achieve spreading consistency. Frost cupcakes.
  3. Yield: 1 dozen
