Cream Cheese Raspberry Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 3-oz package cream cheese
  • 2 TBSP butter, softened
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 egg white
  • 3 TBSP buttermilk
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • ¾ cup flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1/8 tsp baking soda
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • ¾ cup fresh raspberries
  • ¼ cup icing sugar
  • 1 tsp milk

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, cream the cream cheese, butter and sugar until smooth. Add eggs and egg whites; mix well. Beat in the buttermilk and vanilla. Combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt; add to creamed mixture just until moistened. Fold in the raspberries.
  2. Fill paper-lined muffin cups ¾ full. Bake @ 350° for 25-28 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Cool for 5 minutes before removing from pan.
  3. Combine icing sugar and milk; drizzle over muffins.
  4. Yield: 8 muffins
