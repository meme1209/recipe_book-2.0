Black-Bottom Banana Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup margarine, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 ½ cups mashed ripe bananas (about 3 medium)
  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ cup baking cocoa

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in egg and vanilla. Stir in bananas.
  2. Combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt; add to creamed mixture and mix well.
  3. Divide batter in half. Add cocoa to half. Using the smallest ice-cream scoop, put one scoop into baking cups, and one scoop remaining batter on top of this one. Swirl with a knife.
  4. Bake @ 350° for 18-20 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Cool.
  5. Yield: about 18 muffins
