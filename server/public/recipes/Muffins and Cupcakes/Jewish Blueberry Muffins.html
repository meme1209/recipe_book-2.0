Jewish Blueberry Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 ½ cups vegetable oil
  • 12 cups white sugar
  • 2 ½ cups orange juice
  • 22 eggs
  • ¼ cup vanilla
  • 18 cups flour
  • 5 TBSP baking powder
  • 1 TBSP salt
  • 1 ½ gallons fruit

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs and sugar for 10 minutes.
  2. Mix in oil, juice and vanilla and beat another 5 minutes.
  3. Add dry ingredients in the morning.
  4. Sprinkle brown sugar and cinnamon on top of each muffin before baking.
  5. Bake @ 350° for 18- 20 minutes.
