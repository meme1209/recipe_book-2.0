Pistachio Cupcakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 box white cake mix
  • 1 box pistachio pudding
  • 4 eggs ( can use only egg whites if you want it lighter)
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • ¼ cup water
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 ½ cups white sugar
  • 1 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp cinnamon
  • 3 cups chopped walnuts
  • Notes
  • Topping recipe is already enlarged.

Instructions

  1. Fill muffin cups half full, then sprinkle with topping mixture. Add some more batter on top. Sprinkle on a little more topping mixture. Bake @ 350° for 18-20 minutes.
