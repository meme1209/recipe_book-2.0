Shoofly Cupcakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 cups boiling water
  • 1 cup molasses

Instructions

  1. Combine flour, brown sugar and salt. Cut in margarine until crumbly. Set aside ½ cup for topping. Add baking soda to remaining crumb mixture. Stir in water and molasses; fill muffins. Sprinkle with reserved crumb mixture. Bake @ 300° for 20-25 minutes.
  2. Yield: 30 cupcakes
