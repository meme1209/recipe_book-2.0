Banana-Pecan Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • 1 ½ cups cake flour
  • 1 ½ cups sugar
  • 1 ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 ½ cups mashed ripe bananas (2 to 3 medium)
  • 1 ½ cups (12 ounces) sour cream
  • 3 eggs
  • 6 TBSP butter, melted
  • Streusel
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 3 TBSP brown sugar
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • 3 TBSP cold butter
  • ½ cup chopped pecans

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the first 7 ingredients. In another bowl, combine the bananas, sour cream, eggs and butter. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened.
  2. Fill muffin cups 2/3 full. For streusel, combine the flour, sugars and cinnamon. Cut in butter until crumbly; stir in pecans. Sprinkle over tops.
  3. Bake @ 350° for 18- 20 minutes. Cool for 5 minutes before removing from pans.
