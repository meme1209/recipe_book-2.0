Zucchini Cupcakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 1/3 cups sugar
  • ½ cup canola oil
  • ½ cup orange juice
  • 1 tsp almond extract
  • 2 ½ cups flour
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1½ cups shredded zucchini
  • Caramel Frosting
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 ½ - 2 cups icing sugar

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs, sugar, oil, orange juice and extract. Combine dry ingredients; gradually add to egg mixture and mix well. Stir in zucchini. Bake @ 350° for 20-25 minutes. Cool
  2. For frosting, combine brown sugar, butter and milk in a saucepan. Bring to a boil over medium heat cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened. Remove from heat stir in vanilla. Cool to lukewarm. Gradually beat in icing sugar until frosting reaches spreading consistency. Frost cupcakes
  3. Yield: 1 ½-2 dozen
