Very Berry Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 TBSP baking soda
  • 18 cups flour
  • 4 ½ TBSP baking powder
  • 2 ¼ tsp salt
  • 4 ½ cups butter
  • 6 ¾ cups sugar
  • 18 eggs, unbeaten
  • 4 ½ TBSP vanilla
  • 9 cups buttermilk
  • 4 ½ cups each strawberries, raspberries and blueberries

Instructions

  1. Sift first 4 ingredients together; set aside. In a large bowl, cream butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla until light and fluffy. Begin adding ¼ of the flour, mixing well. Add ¼ of the milk, mixing well. Repeat until all ingredients are used. Mix in fruit and bake.
