Raspberry Streusel Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • ½ sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup butter, melted
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup frozen raspberries
  • Streusel Topping
  • ¼ cup chopped pecans
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 TBSP flour
  • 2 TBSP rolled oats
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • 2 TBSP butter, melted

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar and baking powder. In a small bowl, blend milk, butter and egg. Stir milk mixture into flour mixture just until moistened. Fold in frozen raspberries just before filling muffin cups. Fill muffin cups about ¾ full.
  2. For topping, combine ingredients until mixture resembles moist crumbs; sprinkle over muffins.
  3. Bake @ 350° for 20-25 minutes or until golden. Let stand 5 minutes; carefully remove from pans.
  4. Notes
  5. Stir together muffins just before baking.
  6. Make x10 for breakfast.
