Rhubarb Cupcakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 7 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 2 ½ cups shortening
  • 5 cups sour cream
  • 5 eggs, unbeaten
  • 10 cups flour
  • 10 cups raw rhubarb
  • 2 ½ tsp baking soda
  • 2 ½ tsp baking powder
  • 5 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Beat shortening, all brown sugar and eggs. Beat until light and fluffy, add sour cream and dry ingredients alternatively. Do not beat in rhubarb, fold in by hand. Sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon. Bake @ 300 for 30-35 min
