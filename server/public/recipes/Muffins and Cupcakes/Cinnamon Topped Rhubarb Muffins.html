Cinnamon Topped Rhubarb Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups flour
  • 1 ½ cups packed brown sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups finely chopped rhubarb
  • Topping
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mix together first 5 ingredients. Combine egg, buttermilk, vegetable oil and vanilla; stir into dry ingredients just until moistened. Fold in rhubarb. Fill paper-lined muffin cups ½ full. Combine topping ingredients; sprinkle over top. Bake @ 325° for 15-18 minutes or until muffins test done.
  2. Yield: 2 dozen
  3. Notes
  4. Made x 6 for breakfast. Don’t stir together until morning.
