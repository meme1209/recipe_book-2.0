Banana Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2 cups mashed bananas
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp hot water
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 cups flour

Instructions

  1. Cream sugar and margarine.
  2. Add bananas and eggs.
  3. Dissolve soda in water.
  4. Add to mixture.
  5. Add vanilla and flour.
  6. Bake @ 350° for 20-25 minutes.
  7. Yield: 18-20 muffins
  8. Notes
  9. Make x 5 for Heartland
  10. Needs about 20 bananas.
