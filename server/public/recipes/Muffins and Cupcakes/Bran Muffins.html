Bran Muffins

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 9 cups flour
  • 9 cups bran
  • 4 cups sugar
  • 2 ¼ TBSP baking powder
  • 2 ¼ TBSB baking soda
  • 4 ½ tsp salt
  • Mix together well. Make a hole in the middle and add:
  • ¾ cup molasses
  • 4 ½ cups water
  • 1/8 cup instant coffee granules
  • 4 ½ cups milk
  • 4 ½ cups vegetable oil
  • 4 ½ tsp vanilla
  • 9 eggs

Instructions

  1. Mix everything together by hand.
  2. Allow to stand overnight.
  3. Batter will be thin.
  4. Bake @ 350° for 18 minutes.
