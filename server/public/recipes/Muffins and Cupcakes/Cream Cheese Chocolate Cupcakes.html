Cream Cheese Chocolate Cupcakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 cup peanut butter chips
  • Cupcakes
  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ¼ cup baking cocoa
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 TBSP vinegar
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Beat cream cheese until smooth. Add the sugar, egg and salt; mix well. Fold in the chocolate and peanut butter chips; set aside.
  2. For cupcakes, combine the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda and salt. Add the water, oil, vinegar and vanilla; mix well. Fill muffin cups half full with batter. Top each with about 2 TBSP of the cream cheese mixture. Bake @ 350° for 20-25 minutes or until toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Cool completely.
  3. Yield: 1 ½ dozen
  4. Notes: Made x8 for butchering. Too big
