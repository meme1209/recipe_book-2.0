Lemon Square

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 cups flour
  • 2 ½ cups butter
  • 1 ¼ cups icing sugar
  • 1 ½ tsp salt
  • 16 eggs
  • 8 cups sugar
  • 1 cup flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • 2 cups lemon juice (or more)

Instructions

  1. Mix together and press into pan. Bake 20 minutes @ 350°.
  2. Filling
  3. Mix all together and pour onto crust. Bake for 20 minutes. While still warm, sprinkle with icing sugar.
  4. Notes
  5. Made recipe x3
