Peanut Butter Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • ½ cup coconut, optional
  • 1/3 cup margarine, melted
  • 4 cups Rice Krispies
  • Chocolate Dip
  • 3 cups chocolate chips
  • ¼ cup margarine
  • ¼ cup hot water

Instructions

  1. Line baking sheet with wax paper. Mix icing sugar, peanut butter, coconut and margarine together until well blended. Gently fold in Rice Krispies until well coated. Form into 1” balls. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  2. To make dip, melt chocolate chips, margarine and hot water over low heat. If mixture is too thick for dipping, add 1 or 2 TBSP of hot water.
  3. Yield: 4 dozen
