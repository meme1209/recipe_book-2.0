Ingredients

  • 2 cups corn syrup
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 ½ cups peanut butter
  • 8 cups marshmallows
  • 4 cups chocolate chips
  • 2 TBSP vanilla
  • 4 cups corn flakes, crushed
  • 4 cups peanuts

Instructions

  1. Bring first 3 ingredients to a boil. Boil for 3-5 minutes. Reduce heat and add peanut butter, marshmallows, chocolate chips and vanilla. Stir together until smooth and then pour over corn flakes and peanuts. Stir until coated well and press into a greased pan. Cut into squares while still warm.
