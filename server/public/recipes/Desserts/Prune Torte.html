Prune Torte

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 11 ¼ cups margarine
  • 23 eggs
  • 45 cups flour
  • ¾ cup almond extract
  • 16 ½ cups icing sugar
  • 11 ¼ TBSP baking powder
  • 1 ½ cups cream

Instructions

  1. Beat margarine and sugar until fluffy; add eggs and beat well. Add extract and cream. Add dry ingredients until completely blended. Roll out on underside of pi plate with wax paper. Bake @ 300° until golden brown.
  2. Prune filling
  3. 20-24 lbs prunes
  4. 3 cups sugar
  5. ½ cup vanilla
  6. ½ cup cinnamon
  7. ¾ cup real lemon
  8. Cook pitted prunes. When soft add sugar, vanilla and cinnamon. Put through grinder.
  9. Icing
  10. 2 cups whipping cream
  11. 1 TBSP almond extract
  12. 4-6 cups icing sugar
  13. Mix all together until desired consistency is reached. Spread on top of torte.
  14. Notes
  15. Makes 22-5 layer tortes
