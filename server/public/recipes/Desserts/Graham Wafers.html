Graham Wafers

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup margarine
  • ¼ cup milk
  • ¾ cup honey

Instructions

  1. Stir together flour, baking powder, and salt. Cut in margarine and mix milk and honey together. Add flour mixture. Mix well and knead. Put in pans stirring often till light brown. When cold and hard put through grinder. Bake @ 325
