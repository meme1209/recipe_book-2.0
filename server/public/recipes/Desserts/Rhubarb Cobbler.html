Rhubarb Cobbler

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 15 cups rhubarb, chopped per tray
  • 3 cups white sugar per tray
  • 1 ½ cups cornstarch per tray
  • Topping:
  • 7 cups flour
  • 10 ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 ¾ tsp salt
  • 2 1/3 cups margarine
  • 3 ½ cups oatmeal
  • 4 2/3 cups milk

Instructions

  1. Put rhubarb into tray, stir in sugar and cornstarch.
  2. Combine flour, baking powder, sugar and salt. Cut in margarine until mixture is crumbly, add rolled oats and stir. Add milk, stir well to moisten, drop here and there onto rhubarb mixture. Sprinkle with sugar. Bake uncovered @ 375 for 55 minutes until rhubarb is cooked and top is golden brown.
