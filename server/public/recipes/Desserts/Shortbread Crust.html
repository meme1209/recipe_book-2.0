Shortbread Crust

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb butter or margarine
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • ½ cup corn starch
  • 3 cups flour

Instructions

  1. Beat margarine until fluffy. Add rest of ingredients. Press into pie plate. Bake @ 350° for 15-20 minutes.
  2. Notes
  3. Make x5 for Heartland
