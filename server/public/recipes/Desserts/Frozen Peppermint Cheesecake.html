Frozen Peppermint Cheesecake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ¼ Oreo crumbs
  • ¼ cups margarine, melted
  • 1 8oz. package cream cheese
  • 1 14oz. can condensed milk
  • 1 cup peppermint candy, crushed
  • 2 cup whipping cream, whipped
  • Red or Green food coloring

Instructions

  1. Combine crumbs and margarine, press into 9x13 pan. Bake. In a large bowl beat cream cheese till fluffy. Gradually beat in condensed milk. Stir in crushed candy and food coloring. Fold in whipped cream. Pour over crust layer. Freeze.
