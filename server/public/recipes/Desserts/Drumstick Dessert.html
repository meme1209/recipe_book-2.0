Drumstick Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 21 cups oreo crumbs
  • 21 cups chopped peanuts
  • 3 ½ cups margarine, melted
  • 2 ½ cups peanut butter
  • 21 cups cream cheese
  • 7 ½ cups icing sugar
  • 7 ½ cups peanut butter
  • ½ cup vanilla
  • 10 cups whipping cream

Instructions

  1. Mix everything together and press into pie plates. Bake @ 350° for about 12 minutes. Cool.
  2. Filling
  3. Beat cream cheese until smooth and fluffy. Add icing sugar, peanut butter and vanilla. Whip cream until stiff and fold into cream cheese mixture. Let it whip a while until it gets thicker. Spread onto crust and drizzle with caramel. Freeze.
  4. Notes
  5. Makes about 25 pie plates.
