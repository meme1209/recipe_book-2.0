Homemade Peanut Butter Cups

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter, divided
  • 4 ½ tsp butter, softened
  • ½ cup icing sugar
  • 1 ½ cups semisweet chocolate
  • 1 ½ cups milk chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, combine ½ cup peanut butter, butter and icing sugar until smooth.
  2. In a microwave, melt the chocolate chips with remaining peanut butter; stir until smooth.
  3. Drop teaspoonfuls of chocolate mixture into paper-lined miniature muffin cups. Top each with a scant teaspoonful of peanut butter mixture; top with another teaspoonful of chocolate mixture. Refrigerate until set. Store in an airtight container.
  4. Yield: 3 dozen
  5. Notes
  6. I doubled filling recipe.
