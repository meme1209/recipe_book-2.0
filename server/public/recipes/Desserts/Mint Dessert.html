Mint Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 20 cups whipped cream (not whipped)
  • 26 2/3 cups condensed milk
  • ½ cup peppermint extract
  • Green food coloring
  • Oreo crumb crust (no butter, not baked)

Instructions

  1. Place oreo crumbs on bottom of pie plate.
  2. Whip cream until nice and stiff.
  3. Add condensed milk; whip another few minutes.
  4. Then add the peppermint and food coloring. (We mixed it by hand).
  5. Pour onto crumbs; freeze.
  6. Notes
  7. We used homemade condensed milk- if using store bought, it needs 20-14oz cans.
  8. We used this recipe x2 for wedding.
  9. Empire Spice brand peppermint is the best- can buy it at Sobeys.
