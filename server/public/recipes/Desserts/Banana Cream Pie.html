Banana Cream Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup flour
  • 1 TBSP vanilla
  • 2 egg yolks

Instructions

  1. Bring 1 ½ cups milk to a boil.
  2. Mix together sugar, salt and flour.
  3. Make a smooth paste with remaining ½ cup milk.
  4. Add to boiling milk.
  5. Bring to a boil.
  6. Add egg yolks and boil until thickened.
  7. Remove from heat and stir in salt and vanilla.
