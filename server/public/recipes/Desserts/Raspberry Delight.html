Raspberry Delight

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1s layer:
  • 1 cup graham crumbs
  • 2 tbsp. melted butter
  • 2nd layer:
  • 1 3oz package raspberry Jell-O
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 1 tbsp. lemon juice
  • 10 oz. raspberries
  • 3rd layer:
  • 8 oz. cream cheese
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Dash salt
  • 1 cup whipped cream

Instructions

  1. 1st layer: Mix graham crumbs and melted butter, press into 9” square pan. Bake.
  2. 2nd layer: Make filling, including raspberries, let set until wobbly
  3. 3rd layer: Cream together cream cheese, icing sugar, vanilla, and salt. Beat whip cream and add to cream cheese mixture. Put half of this layer over crust, next Jell-O mixture, Top with remaining cream cheese mixture. Sprinkle with graham crumbs. Refrigerate.
