Raspberry Cheesecake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 bricks cream cheese
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 TBSP flour
  • 1 cup whipping cream
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 ½ tsp vanilla
  • Jello
  • 4 TBSP raspberry jello powder
  • 1 cup juice (raspberry)
  • 1 TBSP cornstarch (needs a little bit more)

Instructions

  1. Whip cream cheese until smooth; add sugar and flour, mixing well. Stir in whipping cream, eggs and vanilla. Put an oreo cookie in the bottom of a muffin cup and top with cream cheese mixture, about half full.
  2. Cook jello ingredients until thick. Spoon about 1 TBSP onto cheesecakes and swirl.
  3. Bake @ 300° for 15 minutes.
  4. Notes
  5. Do not bake longer; it might look like it’s not enough, but it’s perfect. Leave in oven to cool.
  6. Use whole oreo cookies for crust.
  7. We made for hulba x6, twice.
