Pumpkin Scones

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups flour
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 ½ tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ½ cup cold butter
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup pumpkin
  • 1/3 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Mix together dry ingredients; add cold butter until mixture is crumbly
  2. Stir in wet ingredients just until moistened.
  3. Divide into 2 portions.
  4. Form into a circle about ½ inch thick.
  5. Cut into 8 wedges, separate and place on greased baking sheet.
  6. Bake @350° for about 20 minutes or until golden brown. Drizzle with glaze.
  7. Notes
  8. X10 for Heartland breakfast
