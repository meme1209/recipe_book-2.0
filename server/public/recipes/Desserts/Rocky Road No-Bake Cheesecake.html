Rocky Road No-Bake Cheesecake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ¼ 12 ½ cups Oreo crumbs
  • ¼ 2 ½ cup butter
  • 3 30 squares chocolate, divided
  • 2 20 8-oz packages cream cheese, divided
  • 1/3 3 1/3 cup sugar
  • ¼ 2 ½ cups milk
  • 2 20 cups Cool Whip
  • ¾ 7 ½ cups marshmallows
  • 1/3 3 1/3 cup peanuts

Instructions

  1. Make Oreo crumb crust and bake.
  2. Melt 1 square chocolate in microwave until smooth.
  3. Beat cream cheese, sugar and milk until fluffy.
  4. Stir in chocolate.
  5. Fold in Cool Whip, marshmallows and peanuts.
  6. Spoon into crust.
  7. Refrigerate 4 hours or until firm.
  8. Notes
  9. Large recipe makes 20 plates.
