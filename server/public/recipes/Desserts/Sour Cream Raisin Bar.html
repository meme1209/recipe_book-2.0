Sour Cream Raisin Bar

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Crust:
  • 16 cups raisin, covered with water, bring to a boil
  • 9 cups brown sugar
  • 9 cups margarine
  • 15 ¾ cups flour
  • 15 ¾ oatmeal
  • Middle:
  • 27 egg yolks
  • 13 ½ cups sour cream
  • 7 ½ tbsp. cornstarch
  • 9 cups white sugar
  • 9 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Cream together Crust. Press half of the crust into 4 steamer trays. Bake 7 min.
  2. Bring first four Middle ingredients to a boil to thicken. Stir in raisins and add vanilla. Pour over baked crumbs and put on remaining crust. Bake @ 300 for 30 min.
  3. Note: When using freezer Rhubarb, drain. When using fresh Rhubarb need about double the rhubarb. Steam them.
