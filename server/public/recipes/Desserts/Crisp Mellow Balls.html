Crisp Mellow Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 bags mini marshmallows
  • 6 cans condensed milk
  • 1 ¾ cup graham crumbs
  • 6 cups chopped almonds
  • 6 cups Rice Krispies

Instructions

  1. Pour condensed milk over marshmallows, almonds and Rice Krispies. Mix well. Add crumbs and stir well. If too soft, add more Rice Krispies. Roll into balls and roll in coconut. Freeze until ready to serve.
  2. Yield: 135 balls
