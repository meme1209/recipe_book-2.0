Chocolate Chip Carrot Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 3 eggs
  • ¾ cup orange juice
  • ¾ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups grated carrots
  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Combine first 8 ingredients. Beat the eggs, orange juice, oil and vanilla. Stir into dry ingredients just until moistened. Fold in carrots and chocolate chips. Pour into 2- 8”x4”x2” loaf pans. Bake @ 350° for 55-60 minutes or until toothpick comes out clean.
