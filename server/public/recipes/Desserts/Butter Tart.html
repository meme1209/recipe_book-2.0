Butter Tart

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 ¼ pounds margarine
  • 130 eggs
  • 72 cups brown sugar
  • 15 cups corn syrup
  • ¾ cup vanilla
  • ¼ cup salt
  • Crust:
  • 27 pounds flour, sifted
  • 13 pounds shortening
  • 4 pounds icing sugar
  • 10 cups water
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 ½ pounds margarine
  • 90 eggs
  • 60 cups brown sugar
  • 10 cups corn syrup
  • ½ cup vanilla
  • ¼ cup salt

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine 20 minutes on high speed. Add sugar and cream mix another 15 minutes. Add syrup and eggs slowly. Add rest of ingredient, beat 20 minutes on high speed. Crust: Mix 1minute on medium speed. Let stand overnight.
