Frozen Mocha Cheesecake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup margarine
  • 1 ½ cups graham crumbs
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup cocoa
  • 1 8oz cream cheese
  • 1 1/3 cup condensed milk (1 can)
  • 2/3 cup chocolate syrup
  • 1 TBSP instant coffee
  • 1 tsp hot water
  • 1 cup whipping cream

Instructions

  1. Melt butter. Mix all ingredients together. Reserve a little bit to sprinkle over top. Press into 9-inch pan. Bake for 10-15 minutes.
  2. 2nd layer
  3. Beat cream cheese until fluffy. Beat in milk and syrup. Dissolve coffee in water. Add to cream cheese mixture. Beat cream until stiff and fold into batter. Pour over crust and scatter crumbs over top. Freeze.
