Peaches and Cream

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1st layer
  • 2 ½ cups margarine
  • 10 cups graham crumbs
  • 2nd layer:
  • 5 8oz. packages cream cheese
  • 5 cups sugar
  • 10 cups whipped cream
  • 3rd layer:
  • 9 lbs peaches
  • 1 ¼ cups white sugar
  • 1 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 10 tbsp. cornstarch

Instructions

  1. 1st layer: mix and put into pans. Reserve half for top.
  2. 2nd layer: Beat cream cheese and sugar. Whip cream and fold into cream cheese mixture. Spread on crust, reserve half.
  3. 3rd layer: Drain juice and pour into 10 cup measuring cup, fill with water to make it 10 cups. Add sugar and cornstarch and cool till thickened, then add peaches and cool. Layer onto cream cheese mixture top with remaining cream cheese mixture. Sprinkle with last of crumbs.
