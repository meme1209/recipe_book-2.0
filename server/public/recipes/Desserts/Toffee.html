Ingredients

  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 2 cups corn syrup
  • ½ cup butter
  • 2 cups cream
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Bring first 3 ingredients to a boil until ball forms in water. Add butter and cream. Bring to a boil. Remove from heat and add vanilla.
