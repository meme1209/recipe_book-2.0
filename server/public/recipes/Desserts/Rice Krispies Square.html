Rice Krispies Square

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ 3 7 cups cream
  • 1 ½ 2 5 cups corn syrup
  • 2 ½ 3 7 cups brown sugar
  • 1/8 ¼ 1/3 tsp salt
  • 1 1 ½ 3 lbs butter (use less)
  • 2 2 ½ 4 bags marshmallows

Instructions

  1. Mix together first 5 ingredients. Bring to boil, stirring constantly, until ball forms.
  2. Stir in marshmallows until melted and then mix in Rice Krispies.
  3. Mix well and press into pans.
