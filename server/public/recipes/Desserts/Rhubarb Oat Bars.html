Rhubarb Oat Bars

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups 24 cups chopped rhubarb
  • 1 cup 16 cups brown sugar, divided
  • 4 TBSP 4 cups water, divided
  • 1 tsp 6 TBSP lemon juice
  • 4 tsp 1 ¼ cups cornstarch
  • 1 cup 16 cups rolled oats
  • ¾ cup 12 cups flour
  • ½ cup 8 cups flaked coconut
  • ¼ tsp 4 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup 5 1/3 cups margarine, melted

Instructions

  1. Combine rhubarb, ½ cup brown sugar, 3 TBSP water and lemon juice. Bring to boil. Reduce heat to medium; cook and stir for 4-5 minutes or until rhubarb is tender. Combine cornstarch and remaining water until smooth; gradually stir into rhubarb mixture. Bring to a boil; cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thick. Remove from heat; set aside.
  2. Combine oats, flour, coconut, salt and remaining brown sugar. Stir in margarine until mixture is crumbly. Press half into a greased 8” cake pan. Spread with rhubarb. Sprinkle with remaining oats mixture and press down lightly. Bake @ 350° for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown.
  3. Notes
  4. If using frozen rhubarb, measure rhubarb while still frozen, then thaw completely. Drain in colander, but don’t press any liquid out.
  5. Very good with strawberries mixed into the rhubarb
  6. Also good with a little bit of strawberry or raspberry gelatine mixed in.
  7. Needs 3 ice cream pails frozen rhubarb.
  8. Try making crust x 12.
