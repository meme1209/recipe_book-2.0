Dutch Apple Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 40 cups flour
  • 1 20 cup packed brown sugar
  • ½ 10 cup quick-cooking oats
  • ¾ 15 cup butter, melted
  • 2/3 13 1/3 cups sugar
  • 3 60 TBSP cornstarch ( 1 ¾ cup)
  • 1 ¼ 25 cups cold water
  • 3 60 cups diced apples
  • 1 20 tsp cinnamon (6 TBSP + 2 tsp)

Instructions

  1. Combine the flour, brown sugar, oats and butter; set aside 1 (or 20 for large recipe) cup for topping. Press remaining crumb mixture into an ungreased pie plate.
  2. For filling, combine the sugar, cornstarch and water in a large saucepan until smooth; bring to a boil.
  3. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened. Remove from heat; stir in apples.
  4. Pour into crust; top with reserved crumb mixture. Bake @ 350° for 40-45 minutes or until crust is golden brown. Cool on a wire rack
