Raspberry Cream Cheese Bars

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ¾ 4 ½ cups butter, softened
  • 1 6 cups brown sugar, packed
  • 1 ½ 9 cups oats
  • 1 ½ 9 cups flour
  • ½ 3 tsp baking soda
  • ½ 3 tsp salt
  • 11 66 oz cream cheese
  • ½ 3 cups sugar
  • 2 12 eggs
  • 1 6 tsp vanilla
  • 1/3 2 cups slivered almonds
  • Raspberry filling

Instructions

  1. Cream butter and sugar. Combine rest of ingredients, mix into creamed mixture and mix well. Press ¾ of mixture into a 9”x13” pan. Bake @ 350° for 11-13 minutes or until edges begin to brown.
  2. Filling
  3. Beat cream cheese and sugar. Add eggs and vanilla; mix well. Spread over crust. Cover with raspberry filling. Mix 1/3 cup almonds into remaining oat mixture; sprinkle over filling. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until set and edges are golden brown.
