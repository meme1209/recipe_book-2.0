Crisp Marshmallow Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 bags small marshmallows
  • 6 cans condensed milk
  • 1 ¾ cups graham crumbs
  • 6 cups chopped almonds
  • 6 cups rice krispies

Instructions

  1. Pour milk over almonds, marshmallows and rice krispies. Mix well. Add crumbs. Stir well. If too soft, add more rice krispies. Roll into balls and roll in coconut. Freeze until ready to serve.
  2. Yield: 135 balls
