Zucchini Chocolate Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 cups margarine
  • 5 cups oil
  • 20 eggs
  • 10 tsp vanilla
  • 5 cups buttermilk
  • 17 ½ cups sugar
  • 10 tsp cinnamon
  • 25 cups flour
  • 2 ½ cups cocoa
  • 5 tsp baking powder
  • 10 tsp baking soda
  • 16 tsp salt
  • 20 cups ground zucchini

Instructions

  1. Mix margarine, oil, sugar and eggs. Add vanilla and buttermilk. Sift dry ingredients together then add them to the mixture. Add zucchini and bake @ 350.
