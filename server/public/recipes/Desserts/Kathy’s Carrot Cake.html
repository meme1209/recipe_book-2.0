Kathy’s Carrot Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 30 cups flour
  • 30 cups sugar
  • 7 tsp baking soda
  • 30 cups carrots, grated
  • 10 tbsp. baking powder
  • 5 tbsp. vanilla
  • 60 eggs
  • 7 ½ tsp salt
  • 13 cups Mazola oil
  • 5 tbsp. coconut
  • 4 cups coconut
  • 4 14oz. cans pineapples, crushed
  • Icing:
  • 1 bag icing sugar
  • 7 8oz bricks cream cheese
  • 1 whipping cream
  • Splash vanilla

Instructions

  1. Sift together all dry ingredients except sugar. Whip oil, eggs, sugar and vanilla until well blended. Then fold in carrots. Slowly add sifted dry ingredients and beat 1 minute. Grease and flour pans. Bake @ 350 for 35 minutes or until cake springs back. This recipe make eight 9x13” cakes.
