Rhubarb Layer Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1st layer
  • 2 cups graham crumbs
  • ¼ cup margarine, melted
  • 2nd layer:
  • 4 cups rhubarb
  • 1 ¼ cups sugar
  • 3 tbsp. cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp. butter
  • Red food coloring
  • 3rd layer:
  • 1 cup whipping cream
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • 4th layer
  • 1 4 serving size instant vanilla pudding
  • 2 cups milk

Instructions

  1. 1st layer: Mix together and reserve half cup crumbs Press the rest into bottom of pan. Bake @ 325 for 5 minutes.
  2. 2nd layer: Stir first two ingredients to create moisture, then cook them. Add cornstarch, butter and coloring. Cool to room temperature and pour over crust.
  3. 3rd layer: Beat whipping cream and fold in marshmallows. Over 2nd layer with it.
  4. 4th layer: beat together and pour over whipped cream layer. Sprinkle with left over crumbs.
