Watergate Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 box white cake mix
  • 1 4 serving package pistachio pudding mix
  • 3 eggs
  • ¾ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup 7-up
  • 1/8 cup walnuts, coarsely shopped
  • Frosting:
  • 1 4 serving package pistachio pudding mix
  • ¾ cup heavy cream whipped

Instructions

  1. Mix first 5 ingredients together, then add nuts, mix well. Bake in greased and floured pan @ 300 for 30-35 minutes.
  2. Frosting: Prepare pudding as directed on box. Fold pudding and cream together. Spread on cooled cake and sprinkle with walnuts.
