Lemon Roll Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 120 large eggs
  • 8 tbsp. lemon extract
  • 40 cups flour
  • 3 tbsp. salt
  • 13 tbsp. baking powder
  • 40 cups sugar
  • 2 2/3 cups cold water
  • Icing:
  • 5 cups sugar
  • 30 cups milk
  • 12 cups flour
  • 15 cups Crisco
  • 15 cups margarine
  • 10 tbsp. vanilla
  • 30 cups sugar

Instructions

  1. Separate eggs. Beat egg yolks until thick. Gradually add sugar, then lemon extract, then stir in stiffly beaten egg whites.
  2. Sift dry ingredients 5 times. Fold in dry ingredient alternately with cold water.
  3. Line a shallow pan with wax paper, pour in batter. Bake 12 minutes.
  4. Fill with Lemon and roll.
  5. Icing: Cook first 3 ingredients, till turns into a paste. Cool. Cream last 4 ingredient together, add to first mixture. Then beat till light and fluffy.
