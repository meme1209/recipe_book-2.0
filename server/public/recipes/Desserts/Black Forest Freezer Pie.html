Black Forest Freezer Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 oreo crumb crust
  • 2 cups chocolate ice cream, softened
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 8 oz frozen whipped topping, thawed
  • 1 can (21 oz) cherry pie filling
  • 3 TBSP chocolate syrup, optional

Instructions

  1. Spoon ice cream over crust. Cover and freeze for 15 minutes. In a mixing bowl, beat cream cheese and icing sugar until smooth. Fold in whipped topping. Spread over ice cream. Using back of spoon, make an 8 inch diameter well in the center of pie for pie filling. Cover and freeze for 3-4 hours. Just before serving, spoon pie filling into the well. Drizzle with chocolate syrup and serve immediately
