Strawberry Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 tray sliced strawberries, place in baked pie shell
  • 26 cups water
  • 3 cups cornstarch
  • 12 cups white sugar
  • 3 ¼ strawberry Jell-O

Instructions

  1. Cook first three ingredients over medium heat, till clear, then add Jell-O. Cool and pour over strawberries. To with sweetened whipped cream.
