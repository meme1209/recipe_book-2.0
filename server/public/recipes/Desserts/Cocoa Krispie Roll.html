Cocoa Krispie Roll

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter or margarine
  • 2 TBSP milk
  • 1 ½ cups icing sugar
  • 2/3 cup cocoa
  • ¾ cup corn syrup
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup peanut butter
  • 2 TBSP butter
  • 4 ½ cups Rice Krispies

Instructions

  1. In saucepan, let the butter and milk come to a boil; then add icing sugar and cocoa. Keep warm in oven.
  2. Roll
  3. Heat syrup and sugar over medium heat until mixture bubbles. Remove from heat and add peanut butter and butter. Stir until smooth. Add Rice Krispies and mix well. Press into a well greased 11”x15” cookie sheet or line sheet with well-greased wax paper, making sure you remove paper immediately afterwards so it doesn’t stick. Spread icing on Rice Krispies and roll up tightly. Chill in fridge.
