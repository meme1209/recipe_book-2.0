Zucchini Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 2 cups sugar
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 ½ cups cold butter or margarine
  • Filling
  • 10 cups peeled, seeded and cubed zucchini
  • 2/3 cup lemon juice
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Combine flour, sugar, cinnamon and salt. Cut in butter until crumbly. Reserve 3 cups and pat remaining crumbs into 9”x13” pan. Bake @ 375° for 15 minutes.
  2. Bring zucchini and lemon juice to boil. Reduce heat; cover and simmer for 8 minutes or until crisp tender. Stir in sugar, cinnamon and nutmeg. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes (mixture will be thin). Spoon over crust. Sprinkle with crumbs. Bake @ 375° for 40-45 minutes or until golden.
  3. Notes
  4. Made crust x4
  5. Made filling x5
  6. Makes 2 steamer trays
