Tiramisu

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 box cake mix
  • 1 8oz brick cream cheese
  • 1 can eagle brand condensed milk
  • 1 tub cool whip (no dream whip)
  • 3-4 tsp rum or rye
  • 1 cup strong coffee

Instructions

  1. Make cake as directed on box. Break apart in 1x3 inch pieces.
  2. Beat cream cheese until fluffy and gradually add condensed milk, fold in whipped topping. Layer bottom of pan with half cake, sprinkle cake with strong coffee. Spread half of condensed mixture over cake, layer the other half of cake. Sprinkle coffee over and spread rest of condensed milk over cake. Garnish with chocolate shavings. Cover and chill overnight
