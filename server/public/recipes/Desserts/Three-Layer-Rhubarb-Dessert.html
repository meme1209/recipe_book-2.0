Three Layer Rhubarb Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1st Layer:
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 2nd Layer:
  • 5 cups rhubarb, chopped
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 1 cup cream
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 3rd Layer:
  • 6 egg whites
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • Dash of salt
  • Slivered almonds (for topping)

Instructions

  1. 1st Layer: Mix together flour, sugar, and margarine. Press into pan. Bake at 300°F for 10 minutes.
  2. 2nd Layer: Mix rhubarb, egg yolks, sugar, flour, cream, and salt. Pour over first layer. Bake at 300°F for 40–45 minutes, or until firm.
  3. 3rd Layer: Whip egg whites, sugar, vanilla, and salt until stiff peaks form (like meringue). Spread over second layer. Sprinkle with slivered almonds. Bake at 300°F for about 10 minutes, or until lightly browned.
