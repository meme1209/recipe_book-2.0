Cream Cheese Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 8 6-oz package chocolate chips
  • 1 8 6-oz package butterscotch chips
  • 1 8 8-oz package cream cheese
  • 1 8 8-oz jar maraschino cherries
  • 2 16 cups mini marshmallows
  • 2 16 cups coconut

Instructions

  1. Mix together both packages chips in double boiler and melt.
  2. Stir in cream cheese, fruit and marshmallows.
  3. Mix well.
  4. Shape into balls.
  5. Roll in coconut.
  6. Chill until firm.
  7. Notes
  8. Large recipe is enough for Heartland.
