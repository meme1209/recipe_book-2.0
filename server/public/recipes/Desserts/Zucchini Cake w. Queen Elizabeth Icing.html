Zucchini Cake w. Queen Elizabeth Icing

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 45 68 eggs
  • 30 45 cups sugar
  • 15 22 ½ cups oil
  • 30 45 cups shredded zucchini
  • 30 45 cups flour
  • 14 21 TBSP vanilla
  • 4 ½ 6 ¾ TBSP baking powder
  • 9 ½ 14 ¼ TBSP baking soda
  • 4 ½ 6 ¾ TBSP salt
  • 14 21 TBSP cinnamon
  • 10 ¾ 16 cups chopped dates
  • 24 36 cups nuts
  • 1 ½ 22 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 1 15 cups margarine
  • ¾ 11 ¼ cups cream
  • 2 ½ 37 ½ cups coconut

Instructions

  1. Combine eggs, oil, sugar and vanilla. Beat until smooth; add zucchini and stir well.
  2. Sift flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and cinnamon. Add dates and nuts.
  3. Let mixture stand overnight in cooler. Pour into greased and floured cake pans and bake @ 300° for 50-60 minutes.
  4. Icing
  5. Mix together all ingredients; bring to boil and boil for 3 minutes.
  6. Spread over cake and bake until golden brown.
  7. Notes
  8. 1.5 lbs in large cake pans. Makes 45 cakes, large recipe.
