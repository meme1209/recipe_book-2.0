Coconut Cream Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 ¾ 9 cups sugar
  • 3 1/3 4 ½ tsp salt
  • 30 1/3 40 ½ cups milk
  • 4 ½ 6 cups cornstarch
  • 41 55 egg yolks, slightly beaten
  • 1 1/8 1 ½ cups margarine
  • 4 5 TBSP vanilla
  • 1 ½ 2 TBSP coconut extract
  • 6 ¾ 9 cups coconut noodle, toasted (add next day)

Instructions

  1. Mix sugar and salt in saucepan. Stir in milk and bring to boil. Add cornstarch (which has been dissolved in some cold milk), stirring constantly. Boil for 1 minute. Stir half of this mixture into egg yolks. Return to saucepan and bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Boil for 1 ½ minutes. Remove from heat. Add margarine and vanilla. Refrigerate overnight. Whip custard very well the next day. Mix toasted coconut into custard. Pour into baked pie shells. Top with whipped cream and garnish with toasted coconut if desired.
  2. Yield: 24 pies
