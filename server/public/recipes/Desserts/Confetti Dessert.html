Confetti Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup margarine
  • 1 ½ cups peanut butter
  • 3 cups chocolate chips
  • 1 24 oz bag mini marshmallows

Instructions

  1. Mix together first 3 ingredients and melt.
  2. When melted, add marshmallows and stir until covered.
  3. Notes
  4. X5 for Heartland.
