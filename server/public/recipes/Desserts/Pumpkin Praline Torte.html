Instructions

  1. Beat sugar, eggs and oil; add pumpkin and vanilla. Combine dry ingredients and add to pumpkin mixture and mix until just blended. Bake @ 325° for 35 minutes.
  2. Topping
  3. Ingredients x 2 for Sunday
  4. 5 15 cups brown sugar
  5. 2 6 cups butter
  6. 2 6 cups cream
  7. 4 12 cups pecans
  8. Boil like regular toffee, leaving a bit thick so it doesn’t run off cake. Cool and spread on cake. Reserve some pecans for top of whipped cream.
  9. Whipped Cream
  10. 7 21 cups whipped cream
  11. 1 3 cups icing sugar
  12. 1 1/3 4 tsp vanilla
  13. Whip cream; add icing sugar and vanilla when thickened. Spread on cake.
