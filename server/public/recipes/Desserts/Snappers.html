Snappers

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 1/3 cups condensed milk (1` can)
  • 1 1/8 cup corn syrup
  • Pretzels
  • Milk chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Boil first 4 ingredients gently for 15 minutes or until almost-frim ball stage.
  2. Lay pretzels on wax paper.
  3. Top with 1 tsp of the toffee, using a plastic spoon or squeeze bottle.
  4. Place a toasted pecan on top. (optional)
  5. Melt the chocolate.
  6. Drop ½ TBSP on wax paper.
  7. Lay pretzel candy on top of chocolate; don’t press down.
  8. Drizzle top of candy with chocolate.
  9. Don’t refrigerate.
