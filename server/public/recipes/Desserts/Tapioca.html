Tapioca

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 bags tapioca (Safeway brand)
  • 6 cups jello powder
  • 24 cups water (use juice from fruit)
  • 5 cups whipping cream
  • 6 cups fruit of your choice

Instructions

  1. Soak tapioca overnight in water 1 inch above tapioca. Drain in the morning.
  2. Boil tapioca, jello powder and water until the balls are soft and chewy or until transparent.
  3. Put in freezer to gel, stirring often.
  4. Add whipped cream and fruit.
  5. Stir together and serve.
  6. Notes
  7. Made with raspberries. Was a bit too gelled. Try more water or less jello powder.
