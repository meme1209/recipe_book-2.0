Italian Nugget

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups sugar
  • 2 cups clear syrup or corn syrup
  • 1 cup honey
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup water
  • 4 egg whites
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups butter
  • 4 oz jujubes

Instructions

  1. Put sugar, syrup, honey, salt and water in saucepan. Over low heat stir until sugar is dissolved.
  2. Bring to boil; cook until temperature reaches 252°F -122°C.
  3. Beat egg whites until firm peaks form. Pour ¼ of the hot syrup in thin stream over egg whites, beating constantly.
  4. Continue beating until mixture is thick enough to hold its shape.
  5. Cook remaining syrup until a small amount of syrup forms brittle threads when dropped in cold water. (this will take over an hour). 315°F- 157°C.
  6. Pour remaining hot syrup over meringue in thin stream, beating constantly until mixture is very thick.
  7. Add vanilla and roughly chopped butter. Beat until thick again for about 5 minutes.
  8. With wooden spoon, mix in jujubes, and spread on greased cookie sheet, smoothing top with spatula.
  9. Refrigerate until firm.
