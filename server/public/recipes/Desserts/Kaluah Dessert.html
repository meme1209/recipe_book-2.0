Kaluah Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 graham crumb crust
  • 2 tsp unflavored gelatin
  • ¼ cup cold water
  • 12 ounces cream cheese
  • 2 TBSP sugar
  • 2/3 cup condensed milk
  • ¼ cup Kaluah
  • 2 cups Cool Whip

Instructions

  1. In a small pan, sprinkle gelatin over cold water; let stand for 1 minute. Heat over low heat, stirring until gelatin is completely dissolved. Remove from heat; set aside.
  2. In a large bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth. Beat in condensed milk, Kaluah and gelatin mixture until blended. Gently fold in Cool Whip; pour over crust. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or until firm. Can also be frozen.
