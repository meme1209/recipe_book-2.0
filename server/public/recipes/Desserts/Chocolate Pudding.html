Chocolate Pudding

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 4 cups sugar
  • 1 ½ 3 cups flour
  • 2 4 quarts milk
  • 8 16 egg yolks
  • 1 2 tsp salt
  • 2 4 tsp vanilla
  • 4 8 tsp butter
  • Add
  • 12 24 TBSP cocoa
  • ¼ ½ cup butter
  • 1 2 cups sugar

Instructions

  1. Stir together flour and sugar; mix with milk until smooth.
  2. Mix in egg yolks and salt. Boil until thickened.
  3. Remove from heat and add butter and vanilla.
  4. Mix in last 3 ingredients.
  5. Cover with saran wrap and refrigerate until completely cool.
