Poppyseed Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cup milk
  • 15 cups cream
  • 25 cups sugar
  • 15 cups cake flour
  • 11 cups flour
  • 12 tbsp. baking powder
  • 25 eggs
  • 10 tbsp. vanilla
  • 8 cups poppyseed
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs, add sugar, till slightly stiff. Add cream, milk, and flour. Add salt, vanilla and poppyseed. Bake @ 350 for 12-15 minutes.
