Poppy Seed square

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Bottom:
  • 5 cups flour
  • 2 ½ tsp baking powder
  • 3 ¾ cups sugar
  • 2 ½ cups milk
  • 3 1/3 cups poppy seed
  • 15 eggs, separated
  • 2 ½ cups oil
  • Middle:
  • 5 cups margarine
  • 15 egg yolks
  • 2 ½ cups icing sugar
  • Top:
  • 20 squares semisweet chocolate
  • 10 tbsp. margarine

Instructions

  1. Bottom: Beat egg yolks with sugar, add oil and milk. Add remaining ingredients, except egg whites. Beat egg whites and fold into preceding. Bake @ 350 for about 25 min.
  2. Middle: Beat until fluffy and spread over cooled cake. Refrigerate to set.
  3. Top: Melt chocolate and margarine, pour over top and chill.
