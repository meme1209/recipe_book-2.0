Turtles

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ - 1 lb pecans
  • 1 ½ cups corn syrup
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • Dip
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • ¼ cup butter
  • ½ bar parowax

Instructions

  1. Spread pecans on bottom of greased 9”x13” pan. Combine syrup, butter, sugar and condensed milk in saucepan. Bring to a boil, stirring. Continue to boil over low heat, stirring often, about 25 minutes, until mixture reaches medium-hard ball stage (250°). A small amount dropped in ice water will form a firm ball that will hold its shape but can be easily flattened. Pour toffee over pecans and cool completely. Cover a large tray with wax paper. Spoon out mixture in small clumps. Freeze until firm for easy dipping.
  2. Melt chocolate, butter and wax in a small saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly. Place saucepan in a bowl of hot water. Dip pecan clumps. Place on wax paper to set.
  3. Yield: 5-6 dozen
