Rhubarb Sour Cream Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups rhubarb
  • 1 ¼ cups sugar
  • ¼ cup flour
  • ¾ cup sour cream
  • 9 cups flour
  • 9 cups brown sugar
  • 2 2/3 cups margarine

Instructions

  1. Mix and divide between 2 pie crusts.
  2. Crumble topping for 25 pies
  3. Mix until crumbly and sprinkle over pies.
  4. Bake @ 325° for 55-60 minutes.
  5. Notes
  6. Do not enlarge filling recipe.
  7. For 25 pies you’ll need: 13 cups sour cream
  8. 60 cups rhubarb
  9. 16 cups sugar
