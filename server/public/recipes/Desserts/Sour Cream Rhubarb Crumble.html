Sour Cream Rhubarb Crumble

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 cups oats
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 2 cups sour cream
  • 5 cups rhubarb
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 TBSP corn starch

Instructions

  1. Crumble all ingredients together, and spread ½ over bottom of a 9x13 inch pan.
  2. Filling
  3. Boil all ingredients for 5 minutes. Pour over crust and top with remaining crust.
  4. Bake @ 325° for 20-30 minutes
