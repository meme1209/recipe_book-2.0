Butter Tarts

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 ¼ lbs margarine
  • 130 eggs
  • 72 cups brown sugar
  • 15 cups corn syrup
  • ¾ cup vanilla
  • ¼ cup salt

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine 20 minutes at high speed.
  2. Add sugar and cream another 15 minutes.
  3. Add syrup and eggs slowly.
  4. Add rest of ingredients.
  5. Beat 20 minutes at high speed.
  6. Notes
  7. Use this recipe as is for Heartland. Did not use correct recipe October 2015.
