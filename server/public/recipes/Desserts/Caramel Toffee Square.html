Caramel Toffee Square

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup cold butter
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 TBSP sugar
  • 1/8 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 ½ cups corn syrup
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 squares (about 2 oz or 56 grams) semi sweet chocolate
  • ¼ cup butter

Instructions

  1. Mix together, press into greased pan and bake until lightly browned.
  2. Filling
  3. Boil until mixture reached medium-hard ball stage. Let cool and pour over base. Refrigerate 2 ½ hours before applying topping.
  4. Topping
  5. Melt and pour over filling. Refrigerate until hardened.
  6. Base x4, filling x3, makes 1 ½ steamer trays.
