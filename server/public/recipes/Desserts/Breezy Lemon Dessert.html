Breezy Lemon Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 7 ½ cups flour
  • 2 ¼ cups butter, melted
  • 6 TBSP sugar
  • 3 cups almonds, chopped
  • 6 8 –oz packages cream cheese
  • 5 cups icing sugar
  • Lemon Filling
  • 8 cups sugar
  • 1 ¾ cups cornstarch
  • 1 cup lemon juice
  • 24 egg yolks, slightly beaten
  • 8 cups milk
  • 2 cups butter
  • 8 cups sour cream

Instructions

  1. Bake first four ingredients until light brown.
  2. Whip cream cheese and icing sugar until smooth.
  3. Add enough whipped cream to make it spreadable.
  4. Spread onto cooled crust.
  5. For filling, combine sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, egg yolks and milk in heavy sauce pan.
  6. Cook over medium heat until boiling and thickened, stirring constantly.
  7. Then stir in the butter. Refrigerate overnight.
  8. Whip until smooth and then add sour cream.
  9. Spoon onto cream cheese. Top with whipped cream and garnish with slivered almonds.
  10. Notes
  11. Crust recipe x2. Cream cheese layer x2. Lemon filling x 1 ½.
