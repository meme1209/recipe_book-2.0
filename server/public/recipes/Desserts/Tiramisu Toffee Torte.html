Tiramisu Toffee Torte

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 package white cake mix
  • 1 cup strong brewed coffee
  • 4 egg whites
  • Skor chips
  • Frosting
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup chocolate syrup
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 6 TBSP strong brewed coffee, room temperature
  • Skor chips

Instructions

  1. Combine cake mix, coffee and egg whites; beat on low speed for 30 seconds. Beat on medium for 2 minutes. Fold in Skor chips
  2. Pour into greased cake pans. Bake @ 350° for 25-30 minutes or until tests done. Cool.
  3. For frosting, in a large bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth. Beat in chocolate syrup and vanilla. Add the whipping cream.
  4. Beat on high speed until light and fluffy, about 5 minutes.
  5. Cut cake into cubes; pile into plate and top with frosting. Sprinkle with Skor chips
  6. Notes
  7. Jan 2015 I used 12 boxes cake mix; 7 pounds mix to 1 bun tray.
  8. Got 3 trays and 1 small.
