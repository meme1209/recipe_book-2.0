Vanilla Ice Cream

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp pure vanilla extract
  • Optional: pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together cream, milk, sugar, vanilla, and salt until sugar is dissolved.
  2. Cover and chill the mixture in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours.
  3. Pour into an ice cream maker and churn according to manufacturer’s instructions (usually 20–25 minutes).
  4. Transfer to a container and freeze for 2–4 hours until firm.
  5. Serve scoops plain or with toppings like chocolate syrup, fruit, or sprinkles.
