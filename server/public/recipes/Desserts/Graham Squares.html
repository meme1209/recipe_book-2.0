Graham Squares

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 4 lbs butter
  • 3 cups 12 cups corn syrup
  • 2 cups 8 cups cream
  • 2 cups 8 cups brown sugar
  • ½ tsp 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp 2 tsp vinegar
  • 1 4 bags marshmallows
  • 1 4 boxes graham cookies, broken into pieces
  • 6 cups 24 cups almonds
  • 2 cups 8 cups coconut

Instructions

  1. Mix together first 5 ingredients.
  2. Cook until mixture reaches firm ball stage.
  3. Add vinegar and marshmallows to toffee; stir until melted.
  4. Remove from heat.
  5. Mix together graham cookie pieces, almonds and coconut.
  6. Pour toffee over mixture and stir until completely covered.
  7. Pour into tray covered with greased wax paper.
  8. Cut into squares while still warm.
