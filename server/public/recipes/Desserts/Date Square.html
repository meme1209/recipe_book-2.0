Date Square

Ingredients

  • Bottom:
  • 7 ½ cups oatmeal
  • 7 ½ cups flour
  • 5 tsp baking soda, dissolved in:
  • 10 tbsp. hot water
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ¼ cup vanilla
  • 4 cups brown sugar
  • 2 pounds margarine
  • Filling:
  • 2 ½ pounds dates
  • 2 ½ cups water
  • 2 cups brown sugar

Instructions

  1. Bottom: Mix all dry ingredients together and rub in margarine. Spread half in bottom of greased pan.
  2. Filling: Mix together and boil. Spread on bottom and top with remaining bottom mixture.
  3. Notes:
  4. Filling: x2 Crust: x 1 ½
