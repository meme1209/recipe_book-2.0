Raisin Delight

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups flour, sifted
  • Dash salt
  • 16 tsp baking powder
  • ¾ cup margarine
  • 4 cups sugar
  • 4 cups milk
  • 3 cups raisins
  • Sauce:
  • 8 cups brown sugar
  • ¾ cups margarine
  • 10 cups boiling water
  • 8 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix together flour, salt, and baking powder. Cream butter and sugar until well blended. Stir in flour mixture and milk. Place in buttered pan.
  2. Sauce: Place brown sugar, margarine, and water into a pan. Bring to a boil, let cool, add vanilla.
