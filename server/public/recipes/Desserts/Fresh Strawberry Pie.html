Fresh Strawberry Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ¾ 18 ¾ cups sugar
  • 1/8 4 cups cornstarch
  • 1 25 cups water
  • 1/8 3 cups corn syrup
  • 1/8 4 cups strawberry gelatine
  • 1 26 quart strawberries

Instructions

  1. Combine first 5 ingredients. Bring to boil. Cool to room temperature. Layer strawberries on prebaked pie shell. Pour glaze over strawberries and refrigerate until ready to eat.
  2. Notes
  3. Use more cornstarch and gelatine when making more than 2 pies.
  4. July 2015 made 26 pies---large recipe
  5. Needs about 13 pails strawberries for 26 pies.
