Chocolate Roll Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 75 eggs
  • 14 ¼ cups sugar, divided ( 4 ¼ cups in flour, 10 cups in eggs)
  • 2 ½ cups cake flour
  • 7 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 5 cups cocoa
  • 5 TBSP baking powder
  • 1 ½ TBSP salt
  • 6 ½ TBSP vanilla
  • 2 ½ cups water

Instructions

  1. Beat egg whites until stiff. Slowly beat in ¾ of the sugar. (The rest of the sugar goes in flour). Beat egg yolks until thick and light. Slowly stir in water and vanilla. Carefully fold egg yolks into egg white mixture until blended. With hands, NOT with whipper, fold in flour, cocoa, salt, baking powder and sugar- which has been sifted 7 times.
  2. Put wax paper (parchment paper works better) into large bun trays. Spray with PAM, and pour 2 ½-2 ¾ lbs of batter into each tray. Bake @ 350° for 10-12 minutes. While still hot, wrap in towel until cooled. When cooled, unwrap and spread with icing.
  3. Notes
  4. Do not double recipe. Make as many separate batches as you need.
  5. Made 3 batches April 2009.
  6. 3 batches makes about 44 rolls.
  7. Made 4 batches June 2011. Got about 60 rolls.
