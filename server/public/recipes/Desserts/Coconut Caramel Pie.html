Coconut Caramel Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup 2 ½ cups margarine
  • 1 1/3 cups 13 1/3 cups flaked coconut
  • ½ cup 5 cups chopped pecans
  • 1-8oz 10-8oz packages cream cheese
  • 1-14oz 10-14oz cans sweetened condensed milk
  • 1-16oz 10-16oz cartons whipped topping
  • 1-12oz 10-12oz container caramel ice cream topping

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, melt margarine. Stir in coconut and pecans. Cook and stir over medium heat until coconut is golden. Cool.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, beat cream cheese with an electric mixer until smooth. Gradually beat in sweetened condensed milk on low speed. Fold in whipped topping. Divide mixture in half. Spoon one part into pastry shells and drizzle with caramel topping. Sprinkle half of coconut mixture on cream cheese layer. Repeat layers. Cover and freeze for 5 hours or until firm.
  3. Notes
  4. Made with graham crumb crust.
  5. Used 4-1 litre cartons whipping cream with about 2 cups sugar added.
  6. Makes 23 pie plates.
