Strawberry Passion

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1st layer:
  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup chopped nuts
  • 2nd layer:
  • 8 oz. cream cheese
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 cup whipping cream, whipped
  • 3rd layer
  • 2 3.4oz package vanilla instant pudding
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries
  • 4th layer:
  • Whipped cream
  • Strawberries

Instructions

  1. 1st layer: mix and press into 9x13 pan. Bake @ 350 for 15 minutes. Cool
  2. 2nd later: Beat cream cheese and sugar. Fold in whipped cream. Spread over crust.
  3. 3rd layer: Beat pudding and milk slowly. Fold in strawberries. Spread on cream cheese layer.
  4. 4th layer: Decorate with whipped cream and strawberries.
