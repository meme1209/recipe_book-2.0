Raspberry Shortcake Cheesecake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 12 inch round prepared white cake
  • 2 TBSP raspberry jam, melted
  • 2 8 oz packages cream cheese
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 TBSP vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 ½ cups Cool Whip
  • 1 ½ cups raspberries

Instructions

  1. Place cake in springform pan.
  2. Brush with raspberry jam.
  3. Beat cream cheese, sugar and vanilla until well blended. Add eggs, one at a time, just until blended; pour over cake.
  4. Bake @325° for 35-40 minutes or until center is almost set. Run knife around pan to loosen cake. Cool before removing rim. Refrigerate 4 hours.
  5. Spread cheesecake with Cool Whip and top with raspberry sauce.
  6. Notes
  7. I baked in combi at 320°. No fan, no moisture.
  8. Made 9 cakes for Katrina’s farewell party.
  9. Cupcakes
  10. 4 boxes cake mix makes 188 miniature cupcakes.
  11. Remove papers and dip in raspberry jam.
  12. Needs 20 bricks cream cheese.
  13. Bake for 9 minutes.
  14. Used 1 pail raspberries.
  15. Made 165 cupcakes.
