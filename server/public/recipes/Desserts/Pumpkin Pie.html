Pumpkin Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 40 60 cups pumpkin (very well drained)
  • 40 60 eggs
  • 16 24 cups sugar
  • 6 ½ 10 tsp nutmeg
  • 8 12 tsp ginger
  • 5 ½ 8 TBSP cinnamon
  • 3 1/3 4 ¾ TBSP salt
  • 1 ½ 2 ¼ cups flour
  • 20 30 cups milk
  • 8 cups pumpkin
  • 14 eggs
  • 4 cups brown sugar
  • 3 cups cream
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • Notes
  • Makes 7 pies

Instructions

  1. Mix everything together and pour into pie crust.
  2. Bake @ 250° to 300° until firm.
  3. Notes
  4. Made large recipe for Heartland April 2015. Makes 45 pies.
  5. Pie crust recipe x 2 is enough.
  6. Needs 5 ½ litres whipping cream.
  7. Plain Pumpkin Pie
