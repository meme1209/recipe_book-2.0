Chocolate Marble Brownie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup cocoa
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • ¾ tsp vanilla
  • Filling
  • 1 8oz package cream cheese
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

  1. Stir together flour, baking powder, and salt. Melt butter in medium saucepan and remove from heat. Stir in cocoa, beat in sugar, eggs and vanilla. Blend in dry ingredients. Pour half the batter in a 9” square baking pan.
  2. Beat cream cheese, gradually beat in sugar, egg and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Carefully spread cream cheese filling on top of chocolate batter. Spoon remaining chocolate batter on filling. Pull a knife through both batters to create a marble effect. Bake @ 350 for 40-45 minutes. Cool.
