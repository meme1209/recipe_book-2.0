Strawberry Pecan Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 20 cups pretzels, crushed
  • 10 cups chopped pecans
  • 15 cups brown sugar
  • 10 cups melted butter
  • 40 cups diced strawberries
  • 20 cups cream cheese, softened
  • 5 cups sugar
  • 7 TBSP vanilla
  • 20 cups whipped topping
  • 20 drops almond extract

Instructions

  1. First roast pecans. Mix pretzels, pecans, brown sugar and butter together and bake @ 350 º for 7 minutes. Allow to cool and break into small pieces. Cover bottom of plate with crust.
  2. Beat cream cheese, sugar and vanilla until smooth. Fold in whipped topping.
  3. Spread cream cheese over crust. Top with strawberries.
  4. Notes
  5. This recipe makes 24 pie plates.
