Marshmallow Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ bags marshmallows
  • 9 cups icing sugar
  • 4 ½ cups graham crumbs
  • 4 ½ cup walnuts
  • 2 ½ cups butter
  • 1 ½ cups cocoa
  • 9 eggs
  • coconut

Instructions

  1. Mix together first four ingredients. Beat together last three ingredients. Beat well. Make balls and roll in coconut.
