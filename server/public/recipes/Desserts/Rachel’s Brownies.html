Rachel’s Brownies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups margarine
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 cups oil
  • 2 cups cocoa
  • Stir in:
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 4 tsp baking soda
  • 8 cups white sugar
  • 8 cups flour
  • Icing:
  • 2 cups margarine
  • 1 cup cocoa
  • 1 ½ cups cream
  • 4 tsp vanilla
  • 8 cups icing sugar

Instructions

  1. Boil first 3 ingredients. Add vanilla and icing sugar. Stir until smooth.
  2. Notes
  3. Make icing when brownies have cooled
  4. Can only make 1 recipe at a time.
  5. 1 recipe makes 3 steamer trays.
  6. For buttermilk, put 1 TBSP lemon or vinegar in a 1-cup, and fill with milk.
