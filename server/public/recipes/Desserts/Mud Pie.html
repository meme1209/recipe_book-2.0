Mud Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup margarine
  • 2 cups oreo crumbs
  • 4 cups coffee ice cream, softened
  • 1/3 cup cocoa
  • 3 TBSP margarine
  • 2/3 cup white sugar
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream or evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Melt first amount of margarine and stir into oreo crumbs. Press into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie plate. Bake @ 350° for 10 minutes. Cool.
  2. Spoon ice cream into shell, spreading carefully. Freeze until firm.
  3. Put cocoa, margarine, sugar and cream into saucepan. Heat and stir over medium heat until boiling. Remove from heat and add vanilla. Cool for about 10 minutes, then smooth over ice cream. Return to freezer.
  4. Notes
  5. Topping spreads better on ice cream if it’s still a little warm.
