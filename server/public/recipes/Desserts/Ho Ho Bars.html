Ho Ho Bars

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 chocolate cake mix, or from scratch
  • 1 ½ cups milk
  • 5 TBSP flour
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 cup Crisco shortening
  • ½ cup margarine
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup margarine
  • 1 ½ cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Bake cake in pans, half the thickness of regular amount. Cool.
  2. Cook 1 ½ cups milk, 5 TBSP flour and 1 TBSP sugar until thick. Cool.
  3. Whip Crisco and margarine until fluffy. Add icing sugar and vanilla and beat until creamy.
  4. Add to flour mixture and beat another 4-5 minutes.
  5. Spread on cake and cool 1 hour in freezer until firm.
  6. Boil sugar, ½ cup milk and ½ cup margarine.
  7. Remove from heat and stir in chocolate chips until melted.
  8. Let cool before spreading on cake.
  9. Notes
  10. X 6 makes 5 steamer trays.
