Pineapple Filled Bar

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Bottom:
  • 12 cups brown sugar
  • 15 cups flour
  • 7 ½ cups margarine
  • 1 ½ tsp salt
  • 22 ½ cups coconut
  • Middle:
  • 12 cups sugar
  • 1 ¼ cups lemon juice
  • 20 cups crushed pineapple in juice
  • 7 tbsp. margarine
  • 3 ¾ cups cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Bottom: Mix together till crumble. Spread half into greased combi tray. Set remaining mixture aside.
  2. Middle: Stir ingredients into sauce pan. Bring to a boil while stirring over heat. Cool slightly and spread over bottom layer. Cover with remaining half of crumb mixture. Bake @ 350 for 25 min or until brown.
  3. Yield: 4 combi trays
