Jelly Roll Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 12 180 egg yolks (room temperature)
  • 1 15 cups white sugar
  • 1 15 cups cake flour
  • 2/3 10 TBSP baking powder
  • ½ 7 ½ cups milk
  • ½ 7 ½ tsp lemon extract

Instructions

  1. Beat egg yolks for 10 minutes; add sugar and beat 15 minutes more. Eggs should be room temperature. Mix together cake flour and baking powder. Add to egg mixture, alternating with milk and lemon extract, beginning and ending with flour. Line sheets with wax paper and spray with Pam. Pour 10 cups (not more) batter onto tray. Bake @310° for 12-15 minutes. Roll up in towel while still hot and wait until completely cooled. Unroll and spread with lemon pie filling and white cake icing.
