Pumpkin Patch Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups 15 cups sugar
  • 1 cup 10 cups pumpkin
  • ½ cup 5 cups vegetable oil
  • ½ cup 5 cups water
  • 2 20 eggs
  • 1 2/3 cup 16 2/3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp 10 tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp 7 ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp 5 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp 5 tsp nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp 2 ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup 5 cups chopped pecans

Instructions

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine first 5 ingredients. Combine the flour, baking soda, salt, spices and baking powder; gradually add to pumpkin mixture and mix well. Fold in pecans. Pour into loaf pans lined with waxed paper and greased. Bake @ 350° for 70-80 minutes or until a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Cool for 10 minutes before removing from pans.
  2. Yield: 1 loaf
  3. Notes
  4. Made x12 Feb. 2011.
  5. Made x30 Sept. 2014. Got 50 loaves.
