Ingredients

  • 4 cups oatmeal
  • 1 ¼ cups flax seed (grind half)
  • 1 ¼ cups sunflower seeds
  • ½ cup millet
  • 1 ¼ cup sesame seeds
  • Poppy seeds (optional)
  • 1 ¾ cup margarine
  • 1 ¾ cup corn syrup
  • 3 ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 3 ¼ tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Combine margarine, sugar and syrup. Bring to boil; boil about 5 minutes. Remove from heat; add vanilla and seeds, stirring well. Pack firmly into greased pan and cool, or roll into balls.
  2. Notes
  3. Very hard to cut in pan if refrigerated.
