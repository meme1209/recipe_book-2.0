Oatmeal Chocolate Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 15 cups Mazola oil
  • 45 cups brown sugar
  • 60 eggs
  • 30 cups cake flour
  • 5 cups cocoa
  • 10 tbsp. baking soda
  • 10 tbsp. baking powder
  • 5 tsp salt
  • 10 tbsp. vanilla
  • Chocolate Icing:
  • 10 ¾ cups white sugar
  • 30 tbsp. margarine (2 cups minus 2 tbsp.)
  • 30 cups cocoa (2 cups minus 2 tbsp.)
  • 30 tbsp. cornstarch (2 cups minus 2 tbsp.)
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 5 tbsp. vanilla
  • 15 cups boiling water

Instructions

  1. Mix Mazola oil and brown sugar, add eggs, mix well, and add dry ingredients. Then add oatmeal and bake @ 350 for 20 min.
