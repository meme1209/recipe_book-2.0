Chocolate Cream Cheese Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups cream cheese
  • 8 bags miniature marshmallows
  • 8 jars cherries, cut into pieces
  • 8 cups chocolate chips
  • 8 cups butterscotch chips
  • 16 cups coconut noodle

Instructions

  1. Whip cream cheese. Melt chocolate and butterscotch chips in double boiler. Mix together cream cheese, cherries, marshmallows, and melted chips. Make small balls and roll into coconut noodle.
