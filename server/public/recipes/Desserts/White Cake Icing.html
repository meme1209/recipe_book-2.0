White Cake Icing

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 30 cups milk
  • 12 cups flour
  • 20 cups sugar
  • 15 lbs Crisco
  • 8 lbs margarine
  • 10 lbs icing sugar
  • 1 cup skim milk powder
  • 3 oz salt
  • 10 TBSP vanilla
  • 7 TBSP almond extract
  • 2 lbs plus 8 oz boiling water
  • Additional 10 cups icing sugar (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook first 3 ingredients the day before. It will make a thick paste. Cook thoroughly. It should not taste raw. Whip while still hot for easily gets lumpy. Very important--- cool overnight.
  2. Whip 10 lbs icing sugar, lard, margarine, milk powder and salt together for 20-30 minutes, then add above cooled mixture. Whip for another 10 minutes. Add vanilla and almond extract. If desired, add rest of icing sugar according to taste. Then very gradually, add boiling water while whipping on high. Whip until nice and fluffy for 1 hour.
  3. Notes
  4. Made recipe ½ for roll cake. Not enough.
