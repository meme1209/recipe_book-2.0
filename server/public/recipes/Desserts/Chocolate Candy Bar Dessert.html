Chocolate Candy Bar Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cup Oreo crumbs
  • ½ cup margarine, melted
  • 1-8oz package cream cheese
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1-1 litre tub Cool Whip
  • 1 cup skor chips
  • 1-4 serving size package instant chocolate pudding
  • 1 ½ cup cold milk
  • 1 jar nutella

Instructions

  1. Combine crumbs with margarine. Press firmly into bottom of 9x13 inch baking pan and bake.
  2. Mix cream cheese and sugar with electric mixer until smooth. Gently stir in ½ of whipped topping and half of nutella. Spread evenly over crust. Sprinkle skor pieces over cream cheese layer.
  3. Pour milk into large bowl. Add pudding. Beat with wire whisk on low speed in electric mixer for 2 minutes. Stir in other half of nutella. Pour over skor pieces. Let stand 5 minutes to thicken. Spread remaining cream over pudding layer. Refrigerate. Garnish with skor bits.
  4. Notes
  5. Recipe makes 2 pie plates.
