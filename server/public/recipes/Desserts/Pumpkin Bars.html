Pumpkin Bars

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 16 cups flour
  • 16 cups white sugar
  • 8 cups oil
  • 4 tsp salt
  • 16 eggs
  • 16 cups pumpkin
  • 16 tsp baking powder
  • 16 tsp cinnamon
  • 16 tsp baking soda
  • 3 tbsp. ginger
  • 3 tbsp. cloves
  • 3 tbsp. nutmeg
  • Frosting:
  • 6 tbsp. creamy milk
  • 6 8oz cream cheese
  • 6 tbsp. butter
  • Splash vanilla
  • Icing sugar

Instructions

  1. Mix eggs, oil, sugar, and pumpkin. Sift all dry ingredients and add to egg mixture. Mix well and pour into greased floured pans. Bake at 300 for 25-30 minutes.
  2. Frosting: Beat cream cheese, butter, vanilla and milk until soft. Add icing sugar till you can spread. About 8 cups.
