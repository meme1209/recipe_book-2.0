Oatmeal Fruit Chews

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 18 cups brown sugar
  • 7 cups white sugar
  • 24 cups margarine
  • 48 eggs, beaten
  • ½ cup vanilla
  • 24 cups flour
  • 4 tbsp. salt
  • 8 tbsp. baking soda
  • 72 cups oatmeal
  • 24 cups craisins
  • 24 cups dates
  • 24 cups chopped nuts
  • 24 cups coconut

Instructions

  1. Cream together sugar, margarine, eggs, and vanilla until fluffy. Grind craisins and dates (use no water). Sift flour, salt, and soda then add oatmeal and mix well. Bake @ 350 for 10-15 minutes. Do not overbake.
