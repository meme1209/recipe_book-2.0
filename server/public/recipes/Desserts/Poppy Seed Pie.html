Poppy Seed Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ 10 cups poppy seeds
  • 10 40 egg yolks
  • 7 ½ 30 cups milk
  • 2 ½ 10 cups sugar
  • 1 TBSP 1 cup vanilla
  • ¼ 3 ½ cups corn starch
  • ¼ 3 ½ flour

Instructions

  1. Boil until mixture thickens, then cool thoroughly.
  2. Pour into baked pie shells.
  3. Top with whipped cream.
