Rhubarb Cream Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 TBSP margarine
  • 1 1/8 cups sugar
  • 2-3 eggs, beaten
  • 2-3 TBSP flour
  • 4 cups rhubarb, sliced

Instructions

  1. Cream butter and sugar together.
  2. Add eggs.
  3. Mix in flour.
  4. Add rhubarb.
  5. Fill unbaked pie shells.
  6. Bake @ 400° for 10 minutes, then bake @ 350° for 35-40 minutes.
  7. Filling will form its own crust.
  8. Notes
  9. Needs 10 bags rhubarb for 20 pies.
