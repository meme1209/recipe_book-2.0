Caramel Pecan Cheesecake Bars

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pecan pieces
  • 1 ½ cup graham crumbs
  • ¼ cup butter, melted
  • 4 8-oz packages cream cheese
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 3 TBSP flour
  • 1 TBSP vanilla
  • 4 eggs
  • 24 Kraft caramels
  • 1 TBSP water
  • 3 squares semi-sweet chocolate, melted

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Line 13x9 inch baking pan with foil. Remove ½ cup pecans. Finely chop remaining pecans. Mix wafer crumbs, pecans and butter. Press onto bottom of pan. Bake for 10 minutes. Cool.
  2. Beat cream cheese and sugar on medium speed until well blended. Add sour cream, flour and vanilla; mix well. Add eggs, one at a time. Mix on low speed just until blended. Pour over crust.
  3. Bake 45 minutes or until center is almost set. Cool completely.
  4. Melt caramels with water. Pour over cheesecake. Top with reserved pecans. Drizzle chocolate over cheesecake. Refrigerate 4 hours.
