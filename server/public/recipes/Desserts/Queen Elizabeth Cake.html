Queen Elizabeth Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 25 cups water
  • 25 cups dates
  • 7 ½ TBSP baking soda
  • 6 ¼ cups butter or margarine
  • 25 cups brown sugar
  • 25 eggs
  • 37 ½ cups flour
  • 6 ¼ tsp salt
  • 7 ½ TBSP vanilla
  • 12 ½ cups walnuts
  • 7 ½ TBSP baking powder
  • 17 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 9 ½ cups margarine
  • 6 ¼ cups cream
  • 30 cups coconut
  • Melt all together and boil for 3 minutes. Pour over cake and put back into oven for 6 minutes @400°.
  • Notes
  • I made cakes 1 ¼ lbs.
  • Got 38 cakes April 2014
  • Stir together in the morning.

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle baking soda over dates. Pour boiling water over dates and soda, and leave to cool.
  2. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs and vanilla.
  3. Stir together flour, salt and baking powder.
  4. Mix dates into butter mixture and then add dry ingredients and nuts.
  5. Bake @ 325° for 20-30 minutes.
  6. Topping
