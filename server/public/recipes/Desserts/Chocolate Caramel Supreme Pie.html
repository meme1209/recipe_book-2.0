Chocolate Caramel Supreme Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared chocolate crumb crust
  • 30 caramels
  • 3 TBSP margarine
  • 2 TBSP water
  • ½ cup chopped, toasted pecans
  • 1 8-oz package cream cheese, softened
  • 1/3 cup icing sugar
  • ¾ cup chocolate chips
  • 3 TBSP hot water
  • 2 cups whipped topping

Instructions

  1. Melt caramels with water and margarine; spread over crust. Sprinkle with pecans.
  2. Refrigerate for 1 hour.
  3. Beat cream cheese and sugar; spread over caramel layer. Refrigerate.
  4. Melt chocolate chips in 3 TBSP hot water over low heat.
  5. Stir until smooth; cool slightly, and fold into 1 cup whipped topping.
  6. Spread over cream cheese layer. Top with remaining whipped topping.
  7. Garnish with chocolate curls.
