Angel Food Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sifted cake flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 ¼ cup egg whites
  • 1 ¼ tsp cream of tartar
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • ¼ tsp almond extract
  • 1 cup white sugar

Instructions

  1. Sift cake flour. Sift flour and ½ cup sugar together 3 times.
  2. Beat egg whites until frothy. Add cream of tartar, salt, vanilla and almond extract. Beat until soft peaks form. Add 1 cup sugar, 1 TBSP at a time, continuing to beat until all is added and whites stand in stiff peaks. Fold in ¼ flour mixture at a time.
  3. Turn into ungreased 10 inch tube pan (with wax paper on the bottom). Gently cut through batter with knife to remove air pockets.
  4. Bake @ 350° for about 35 minutes or until cake springs back when lightly pressed. Invert pan to cool, rest tube over neck of beer bottle filled with water.
  5. Notes
  6. Don’t enlarge recipe more than 5x at a time.
