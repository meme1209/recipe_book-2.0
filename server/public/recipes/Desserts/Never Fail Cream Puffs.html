Never Fail Cream Puffs

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 16 cups flour
  • 8 16 cups water
  • 8 16 tsp baking powder
  • 24 48 eggs
  • 2 4 tsp salt
  • 4 8 cups butter or margarine

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425° (hot). Grease a large cookie sheet. Sift flour, baking powder and salt together several times. Bring water and butter just to boiling point. Remove from heat and mix in flour all at once. Replace on heat and beat hard until mixture forms a mass, leaving sides of pan clean. Remove from heat and beat in eggs one at a time, stirring vigorously after each until thoroughly combined, making a smooth egg mixture. Drop by spoonfuls into mounds about the size of an egg, well apart. Bake @ 425° for about 15 minutes, then turn oven down to 300°, and bake for 25 minutes or longer. When cream puffs are nice and risen, prick them on top with a knife. Put back in oven to dry inside; cool.
  2. Fill with jam, and top with whipped cream.
