Pumpkin Torte

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup butter or margarine
  • 2 cups graham crumbs
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 TBSP unflavoured gelatine
  • ¼ oz water
  • 14 oz pumpkin 1
  • ½ cup sugar 2
  • 3 egg yolk 3
  • 1 ½ tsp cinnamon 4
  • ½ tsp salt 5
  • ½ cup milk 6
  • 1 tsp nutmeg 7
  • 3 egg whites
  • ¼ cup sugar

Instructions

  1. Melt butter and mix together with crumbs and sugar. Press into an ungreased 9”x13” pan. Bake until set.
  2. Filling
  3. Beat cream cheese and ¾-cup sugar together well. Beat in one egg at a time. Pour over crust and bake @ 325° for 20 minutes. Sprinkle gelatine over water and let stand at least 5 minutes. Measure ingredients 1-7 into saucepan. Heat over medium heat until boiling and thickened. Stir in gelatine and chill until syrupy. Beat egg whites until soft peaks form. Add sugar gradually, beating until stiff. Fold into pumpkin mixture. Pour over cheese layer. Chill. Serve with sweetened whipped cream.
  4. Notes
  5. X4 makes 1 ½ steamer trays. Enough for Heartland.
