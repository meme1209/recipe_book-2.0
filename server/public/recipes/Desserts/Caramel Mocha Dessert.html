Caramel Mocha Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 cups oreo crumbs
  • 1 ½ cups margarine, melted
  • Caramel
  • 1 ½ cups corn syrup
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 12 cups cream cheese
  • 4 TBSP instant coffee
  • 3 cups whipping cream
  • 3 cups icing sugar

Instructions

  1. Add melted margarine to crumbs. Press firmly into dish. Drizzle with caramel.
  2. Caramel
  3. Boil and bring to soft ball stage.
  4. Filling
  5. Beat cream cheese until smooth. Dissolve coffee in cream; add to cream cheese. Add icing sugar. Spead over caramel. Garnish with sweetened whipped cream and drizzle with caramel.
