Frozen Mocha Marbled Loaf

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups oreo crumbs
  • 3 TBSP margarine
  • 1 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 can condensed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups whipping cream, whipped
  • 2 TBSP instant coffee
  • 1 TBSP hot water
  • ½ cup chocolate syrup

Instructions

  1. Combine crumbs and margarine. Press firmly into a 9”x13” pan. Bake until set. In a mixing bowl, beat cream cheese until light and fluffy. Add milk and vanilla; mix well. Fold in whipped cream. Spoon half of mixture in another bowl and set aside. Dissolve coffee in hot water; fold into remaining cream cheese mixture. Stir in chocolate syrup. Spoon half of chocolate mixture over crust. Top with half cream cheese mixture. Repeat layers. Marble. Cover and freeze for 6 hours or overnight.
