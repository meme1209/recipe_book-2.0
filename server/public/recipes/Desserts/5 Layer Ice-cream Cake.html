5 Layer Ice-cream Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups cocoa
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 TBSP butter
  • ¾ cup cream
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Layer 1---place a layer of moistened oreo crumbs on bottom of 10-inch spring form pan.
  2. Layer 2--- take 1/3 ice cream from a 4-litre pail of ice cream and whip until creamy. Break up oreo cookies until you have 2 cups. Mix into ice cream and spread on crust. Freeze for a couple of hours.
  3. Layer 3--- spread about ¼ inch of hot fudge topping on ice cream. Freeze.
  4. Layer 4--- take another 1/3 of ice cream and whip until creamy. Add any chocolate sauce until ice cream is a nice brown color. Add Nutella until it has a nice flavour. Freeze for a couple of hours.
  5. Layer 5--- take the last 1/3 of ice cream and whip until creamy. Add 1 ½ cups skor bar chips and enough very, very strong coffee to make a good taste. Spread over fourth layer. Can sprinkle top with oreo crumbs or skor chips.
  6. Fudge
  7. Bring first 5 ingredients to a boil. Remove from heat and add vanilla. Cool before spreading over ice cream.
  8. Notes
  9. For Father’s Day, 2011, we needed 9 pails ice-cream for 12 sprinform pans and one small steamer tray.
  10. We had 6 vanilla and 3 chocolate pails.
