Kalooah Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 22 cups Oreo crumbs
  • 3 cups margarine
  • 64 egg yolks
  • 4 cups water
  • 4 cups sugar
  • ½ cup instant coffee
  • 32 cups heavy cream
  • 4 cups coffee liqueur
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 4 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs at high speed until tick. Over low heat cook water, sugar, and instant coffee till sugar is dissolved. While beating eggs on medium slowly add coffee mixture. Continue beating eggs until creamy and cool. Beat cream until thick. Fold whipped cream and coffee liqueur into egg mixture.
