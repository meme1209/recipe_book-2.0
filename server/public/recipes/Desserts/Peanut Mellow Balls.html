Peanut Mellow Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • 1 1-lb bag marshmallows
  • 2 TBSP butter
  • 6 cups popped popcorn
  • 6 cups cereal, any kind, mixed

Instructions

  1. Melt together chocolate chips and peanut butter until smooth.
  2. Melt butter and pour over marshmallows. Microwave until they begin to lift, take out and stir. Microwave some more until melted. Mix marshmallow and peanut butter mixture together and pour over popcorn and cereal. Stir to coat and form into balls or press into greased pan.
