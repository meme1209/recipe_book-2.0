Rhubarb Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cups margarine
  • 20 cups brown sugar
  • 10 cups white sugar
  • 20 eggs
  • 20 tsp vanilla
  • 40 cups flour
  • 20 tsp baking soda
  • 10 tsp salt
  • 20 cups sour milk
  • 30 cups rhubarb

Instructions

  1. Mix first 4 ingredients and then add the rest of the ingredients. Mix alternately, on top sprinkle mixture of brown sugar, cinnamon, and walnuts. Bake @ 325 for 25-30 minutes.
