Glazed Raspberry Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 cups 90 cups fresh raspberries, divided
  • 1 cup 20 cups water, divided
  • 1 cup 20 cups sugar
  • 3 TBSP 4 cups cornstarch
  • 2 TBSP 2 ¼ cups lemon juice
  • 1-3oz 7-8oz packages cream cheese
  • 1 TBSP 1 ¼ cup butter, softened
  • 1 TBSP 1 ¼ cup milk
  • 1 22 pastry shells, prebaked

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, combine 1 cup raspberries and 2/3 cup water. Simmer, uncovered, for 3 minutes. Strain raspberries and discard seeds; set juice aside. In another saucepan, combine sugar, cornstarch and remaining water until smooth. Add raspberry juice. Bring to boil over medium heat; cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened. Remove from heat; stir in lemon juice. Cool. Beat cream cheese, butter and milk until smooth. Spread onto crust. Top with raspberries. Slowly pour glaze over raspberries. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
