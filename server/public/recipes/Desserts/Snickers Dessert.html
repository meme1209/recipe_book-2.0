Snickers Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 TBSP milk
  • 2 ½ cups whipped cream (measure after whipping)
  • 2 snicker bars
  • ¾ cup chocolate pudding
  • Oreo crumb crust

Instructions

  1. Make crust and bake for 10 minutes.
  2. Mix cream cheese and 1 TBSP milk until smooth. Gently stir in 2 cups whipped topping and chopped candy bars. Spread over cooled crust.
  3. Stir ½ cup whipped cream into chocolate pudding and spread over cream cheese layer.
  4. Garnish with whipped cream.
  5. Refrigerate until set.
  6. Notes
  7. Sweeten whipped cream before using.
