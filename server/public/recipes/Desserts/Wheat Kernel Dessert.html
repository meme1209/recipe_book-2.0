Wheat Kernel Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 quart vanilla pudding
  • 3 quarts wheat kernels
  • 12 quarts diced fruit
  • 3 quarts whipped cream
  • 3 cups cream cheese

Instructions

  1. Simmer wheat until cracked and soft.
  2. Drain well.
  3. Whip cream cheese until smooth.
  4. Mix well with whipped cream cheese and pudding.
  5. Fold in wheat and fruit.
