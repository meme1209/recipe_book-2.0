Frozen Lemon Dessert

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Graham crumbs
  • 5 cups sugar
  • 30 eggs, separated
  • 5 pints whipping cream
  • 5 tsp lemon flavoring

Instructions

  1. Beat sugar and egg yolks thoroughly, add lemon flavoring. Beat egg whites real stiff and fold into stiffly beaten whipped cream. Mix everything together by hand. Line bottom of pan with graham crumbs, top with lemon mixture. Frreze.
