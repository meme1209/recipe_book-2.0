Chocolate Crunch Brownies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter or margarine, softened
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 6 TBSP cocoa
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 7 oz jar marshmallow crème
  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3 cups rice krispies
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. In a mixing bowl, cream butter and sugar. Add eggs
  2. Stir in cocoa, flour, vanilla and salt.
  3. Spread into a 9x13” pan. Bake @ 350° for 25 minutes or until brownies test ready.
  4. Cool and spread marshmallow crème over brownies.
  5. Melt peanut and chocolate chips over low heat, stirring constantly.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in rice krispies. Spread over marshmallow crème.
  7. Chill before cutting. Makes 3 dozen.
