Caramel Nut Roll

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 28 caramels
  • ¼ cup butter or margarine
  • 2 TBSP cream
  • 1 ½ cups icing sugar
  • 1 cup salted peanuts
  • 2 cups miniature marshmallows
  • 1-2 cups flaked coconut

Instructions

  1. Place caramels, margarine and cream in a 2-quart microwave dish. Microwave 1-3 minutes, stirring every minute until smooth. Stir in sugar until smooth. Add peanuts and gently fold in marshmallows. Sprinkle coconuts on a 10”x15” piece of waxed paper. Spoon caramel mixture down the center of coconut. Using the wax paper, coat caramel with coconut and roll into 2 - 10” logs. Discard wax paper. Wrap logs in plastic wrap, and chill for 4 hours. Remove plastic wrap. Cut logs into slices and serve.
  2. Notes
  3. For Good Friday, I made 19 batches. I used 20 chocolate caramels and 8 brown caramels for each batch.
