Chocolate Turtle Cheesecake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups 24 cups oreo crumbs
  • 6 Tbsp 4 ½ cups margarine, melted
  • 14 ounces 168 ounces caramels
  • 1 cup 12 cups pecans, chopped and toasted
  • ½ cup 6 cups sugar
  • 5 ounces 60 ounces evaporated milk
  • 2 8-oz 24 8-oz bricks cream cheese
  • 1 tsp 4 TBSP vanilla
  • ½ cup 6 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Melt chocolate chips.
  2. Combine crumbs and margarine and press into pans.
  3. Bake @ 350° for 10 minutes.
  4. In a heavy saucepan, melt caramels with evaporated milk over low heat, stirring frequently, until smooth.
  5. Pour over crust and top with pecans.
  6. Beat cream cheese until smooth, and then add sugar and vanilla, beating until fluffy.
  7. Blend in melted chocolate chips and spread over pecans.
  8. Chill.
  9. Garnish with whipped cream.
