Lemon Bar

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Bottom:
  • 5 cups flour
  • 2 ½ cups margarine
  • 1 ¼ cups icing sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Filling:
  • 10 eggs, beaten
  • 5 cups sugar
  • 10 tbsp. flour
  • 2 ½ baking powder
  • 20 tbsp. lemon juice.

Instructions

  1. Bottom: Mix with beater. Pat into the bottom of long stainless tray. Bake @ 350 or until slightly brown.
  2. Filling: Mix together and pour over crust. Bake @ 350 for 20 min. While still hot sprinkle with icing sugar.
