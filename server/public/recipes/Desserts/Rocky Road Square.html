Rocky Road Square

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups miniature marshmallows
  • 2 ½ cups rice krispies
  • ½ cup corn syrup
  • 1 cup chocolate chip
  • ½ cup peanut butter

Instructions

  1. Mix together marshmallows and rice krispies. Melt together chocolate chips and corn syrup over low heat, stir in peanut butter and pour over marshmallow and rice krispies.
