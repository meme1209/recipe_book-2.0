Fruit Pizza

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Crust:
  • 6 ½ lbs butter
  • 19 ½ cups flour
  • 6 ½ cups icing sugar
  • 3 ¼ cups cornstarch
  • Spread:
  • 385 oz. cream cheese
  • 15 cups sugar
  • 8 tbsp. vanilla
  • Glaze:
  • 36 cups pineapple juice (use orange instead or half an half)
  • 10 cups sugar
  • 1 cup lemon juice
  • Cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Crust: Beat butter until fluffy. Add rest of ingredients. Press into pie plate. Bake @ 350° for 15-20 minutes.
  2. Spread: Beat until light and fluffy. Spread over baked crust. Top with fruit.
  3. Glaze: Heat to boiling and thickened.
  4. Notes
  5. Made as is, Mother’s day 2016, had around 40 pie plates. Was good.
