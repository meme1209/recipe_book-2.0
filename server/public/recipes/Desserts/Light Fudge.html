Light Fudge

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • 2 cups corn syrup
  • 2 cups cream
  • ½ cups butter
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix first 5 ingredients together and cook until soft ball forms in cold water. Remove from heat and add vanilla and soda. Stir well.
