Black Forest Cheesecake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Oreo crumbs
  • ½ cup melted margarine
  • 8 oz. cream cheese
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 8 oz. whipped cream
  • ½ bag miniature marshmallows
  • Cherry pie filling

Instructions

  1. Mix crumb and margarine and press into 9x13 pan. Bake 8 minutes and cool.
  2. Beat together cream cheese and icing sugar. Fold in cream and marshmallows. Spread over baked crust. Spread cherry pie filling. Top with sweetened whipped cream. Sprinkle with Oreo crumbs.
