Rum Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 6oz. package chocolate chips
  • 1 6oz. package butterscotch chips
  • 1 8oz. package cream cheese
  • 1 cup walnuts, chopped
  • 2 cups mini marshmallows
  • 1 ½ graham crumbs
  • 1/8 cup rum extract
  • 3 tbsp. water

Instructions

  1. Beat cream cheese in mixer. Heat chocolate and butterscotch chips in double boiler. Beat in cream cheese; mix in walnuts, rum, marshmallows and crumbs. Make balls with smallest scoop, roll in coconut.
