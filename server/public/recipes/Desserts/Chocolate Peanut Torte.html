Chocolate Peanut Torte

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter or margarine, melted
  • ½ cup chopped nuts
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 TBSP sugar
  • Filling x 10
  • 1 8 oz cream cheese
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • 4 cups whipped topping
  • 3 cups cold milk
  • 2 oz instant chocolate pudding

Instructions

  1. Mix crust ingredients together and press into plate. Bake until edges are brown.
  2. In mixing bowl, beat cream cheese, sugar and peanut butter until smooth. Fold in half of whipped topping. Spread over crust. In another mixing bowl, beat milk and pudding on low for 2 minutes. Carefully spread over cream cheese layer. Then spread remaining topping over pudding layer.
