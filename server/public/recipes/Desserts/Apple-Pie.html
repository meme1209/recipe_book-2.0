Classic Apple Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cubed
  • 6–8 tbsp ice water
  • 6 cups thinly sliced apples (Granny Smith or Honeycrisp)
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 egg (for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix flour and salt. Cut in butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs. Stir in ice water, 1 tbsp at a time, until dough forms. Divide in half, shape into disks, and chill for 30 minutes.
  3. In another bowl, toss sliced apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice.
  4. Roll out one dough disk and place in a 9-inch pie dish. Fill with apple mixture.
  5. Roll out second dough disk and place over filling. Trim and crimp edges. Cut slits in top to vent.
  6. Beat egg with 1 tbsp water and brush over crust.
  7. Bake for 45–50 minutes or until crust is golden and filling is bubbly. Cover edges with foil if browning too quickly.
  8. Cool for at least 2 hours before serving.
