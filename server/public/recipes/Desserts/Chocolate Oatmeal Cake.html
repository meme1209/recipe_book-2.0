Chocolate Oatmeal Cake

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 12 cups oatmeal
  • 24 cups boiling water
  • 12 cups vegetable oil
  • 6 cups cocoa
  • 36 cups brown sugar
  • 48 eggs
  • 24 cups flour, sift 3 times
  • 8 tbsp. baking soda
  • 8 tbsp. baking powder
  • 4 tbsp. salt
  • 8 tbsp. vanilla

Instructions

  1. 1 ½ lbs batter per pan. Bake @ 300 for 300 30-35 minutes. Yields: 33 cakes
