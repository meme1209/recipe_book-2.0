Puffed Wheat Squares

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ¾ 4 ½ cups butter
  • 1 6 cups corn syrup
  • 1 ½ 9 cups brown sugar
  • 3 18 TBSP (or 1 1/8 cup) cocoa
  • Dash salt
  • Splash vanilla
  • Puffed wheat

Instructions

  1. Bring first 6 ingredients to a boil.
  2. Boil until it reaches soft ball stage.
  3. Remove from heat and add vanilla.
  4. Mix in the puffed wheat.
  5. Notes
  6. Large recipe makes 5 steamer trays.
