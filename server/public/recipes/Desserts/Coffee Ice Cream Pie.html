Coffee Ice Cream Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ounces unsweetened chocolate
  • ¼ cup butter, cubed
  • 1 can (5 ounces) evaporated milk
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 2 cups coffee ice cream, softened
  • 1 chocolate crumb crust (8 inches)
  • 1 carton (8 ounces) frozen whipped topping, thawed
  • ¼ cup chopped pecans

Instructions

  1. In a heavy saucepan, melt chocolate and butter over low heat. Stir in milk and sugar. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly. Cook and stir for 3-4 minutes or until thickened. Remove from heat; cool completely.
  2. Spoon the ice cream into crust. Stir sauce; spread over ice cream. Sprinkle with pecans. Top with the whipped topping. Freeze until firm. Remove from the freezer 15 minutes before serving.
