Lemon Poppy Seed Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 package lemon cake mix
  • ¾ cup vanilla instant pudding
  • 1 cup warm water
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 TBSP lemon juice or grated lemon peel
  • 4 eggs
  • ¼ cup poppy seed
  • 1 heaping spoon Miracle Whip

Instructions

  1. Place everything in mixing bowl and beat on medium for 3-4 minutes. Put into loaf pans and bake @ 300° for 25-30 minutes.
  2. Yield: 2 loaves
