Best Broccoli Casserole

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water + 1 cup instant rice + ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ cup margarine
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • ¼ cup celery
  • 1 can (10 3/4oz) cream of mushroom soup, undiluted
  • 1 can (10 3/4oz) cream of celery soup, undiluted
  • 10 oz broccoli
  • ½ cup cheese

Instructions

  1. Make rice. Melt margarine in saucepan. Sautee onions and celery until tender. In large mixing bowl, combine rice, celery, onions with remaining ingredients. Pour into greased baking dish. Bake @ 350° for 1 hour
  2. Notes
  3. I made x3 for 1 steamer tray
