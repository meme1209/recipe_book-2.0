Loaded Biscuits

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cups flour
  • 10 TBSP sugar
  • 6 ½ TBSP baking powder
  • 5 tsp salt
  • 12 ½ cups grated cheese
  • ½ cup green peppers, finely chopped
  • ½ cup onions, finely chopped
  • 1 ¼ cups oil
  • 4 ¼ cups milk

Instructions

  1. Mix together first 4 ingredients.
  2. Add cheese, peppers and onions.
  3. Add oil and milk, just until moistened.
  4. Scoop with ice-cream scoop onto greased tray.
  5. Bake @ 325° for 15 minutes.
