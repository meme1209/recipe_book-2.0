Fried Mushrooms

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • Seasoning salt
  • Onion salt
  • Garlic
  • 1 cup cornflakes, crushed

Instructions

  1. Beat together first three ingredients.
  2. Stir seasonings into flour.
  3. Roll whole mushrooms in flour.
  4. Dip in egg mixture.
  5. Roll in crumbs.
  6. Fry
  7. Notes
  8. This recipe makes 20 mushrooms.
  9. One bun tray is enough.
