Scalloped Carrots

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 cups water
  • 4 cups carrots, sliced ¼ inch thick
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • ½ cup margarine, divided
  • ¼ cup flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp mustard
  • ¼ tsp celery salt
  • Dash pepper
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil carrots in water. Cover and cook for about 4 minutes. Drain and immediately place carrots in ice water; drain and pat dry. In saucepan, sauté onions in ¼ cup margarine. Stir in flour, salt, mustard, celery salt and pepper until blended. Gradually add milk; bring to a boil. Cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened. Pour cheese into sauce and pour over carrots. Bake @ 350° for 35-40 minutes.
