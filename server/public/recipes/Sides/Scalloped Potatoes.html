Scalloped Potatoes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 (10 3/4oz) cans cream of celery soup, undiluted
  • 2 (10 3/4oz) cans cream of chicken soup, undiluted
  • 1 lb shredded cheese
  • 2 cups sour cream
  • ½ cup margarine
  • 2 tsp seasoning salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 10 lbs potatoes, peeled and cooked

Instructions

  1. Combine soups, cheese, sour cream, margarine, seasoned salt, garlic powder, and pepper. Cook and stir until cheese is melted and mixture is smooth. Add potatoes and mix well.
