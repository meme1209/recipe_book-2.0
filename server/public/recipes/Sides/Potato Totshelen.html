Potato Totshelen

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups fine grated raw potatoes
  • 1 cup hot milk
  • 1 TBSP salt
  • 8-10 eggs
  • 1 big serving spoon flour

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients. Drop by serving spoonfuls into fryer and fry till half done. Then fry till golden brown.
