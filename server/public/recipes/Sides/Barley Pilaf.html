Barley Pilaf

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups pearl barley
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 3 large carrots, shredded
  • 4 cups sliced mushrooms
  • 1 ½ tsp pepper
  • 7 cups small broccoli pieces
  • Cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook barley with soup, salt and chicken base to taste. Drain. Sautee onions, carrots, mushrooms on skillet. Cook broccoli with 3 cups soup; add pepper and drain when ready. Mix everything into barley. Mix in cheese. Keep warm.
