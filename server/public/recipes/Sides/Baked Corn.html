Baked Corn

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups corn
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 TBSP flour
  • 1 TBSP sugar (optional)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Dash of pepper
  • 2 TBSP melted margarine
  • Dry bread crumbs

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs and milk. Combine corn, eggs, milk, flour, seasonings and margarine. Mix well. Pour into greased pan. Bake @300° for 20 minutes. Remove from heat and. Mix bread crumbs and margarine together and sprinkle over top. Bake another 20 minutes or until mixture is set.
  2. Notes
  3. Make x 8
  4. 2 jars corn
