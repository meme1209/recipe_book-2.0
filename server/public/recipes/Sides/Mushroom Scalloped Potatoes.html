Mushroom Scalloped Potatoes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Mushroom soup
  • Dash paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Whip together all ingredients and pour over sliced potatoes. Should be thick and does not have to cover all potatoes. Bake @ 350° for ½ hour. Layer potatoes, onions and then mozzarella cheese.
