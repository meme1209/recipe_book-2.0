Creamy Vegetable Casserole

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Broccoli
  • Carrots
  • Cauliflower
  • 3 cans mushroom soup
  • Cream cheese

Instructions

  1. Blanch vegetables. Make a sauce with a bit of cream or milk.
  2. Add seasonings to taste.
  3. Sprinkle with croutons.
  4. Bake uncovered until bubbly.
