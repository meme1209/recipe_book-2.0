French Onion Casserole

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 large onions, sliced
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 3 TBSP flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 ½ cups milk
  • 2 cans (10 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • Croutons
  • Shredded mozzarella

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, sauté onions in butter until tender. Add flour, salt and pepper to onions; stir until smooth. Gradually stir in milk until blended; bring to boil; cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened. Add mushroom soup. Pour into greased steamer tray. Top with croutons until nicely covered. Spread with cheese.
  2. Bake until cheese is melted and casserole is heated through.
