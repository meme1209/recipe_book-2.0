Zucchini Fries

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 medium zucchini
  • ½ cup flour
  • 1 tsp onion salt
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 tsp canola oil
  • 4 cups Corn Flakes (very finely crushed)

Instructions

  1. Cut zucchini in half widthwise, then cut each half lengthwise into 8 wedges; set aside. In a large bowl, combine the flour, onion salt, oregano and garlic powder. Combine the egg, milk and oil; stir into dry ingredients just until blended. Dip zucchini wedges in batter, the roll in finely crushed cereal.
  2. Fry in 350° oil, a few at a time, for 3-4 minutes or until golden brown. Drain and keep warm.
  3. Notes
  4. Batter mixture x 10.
  5. 2 fairly large zucchini is enough.
  6. 3 bags cornflakes
  7. Shake off batter well before coating with cornflakes
