Baked Spinach Dip in Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 packages (8 oz each) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 10 oz frozen spinach, thawed and chopped and squeezed dry
  • 1 cup (4 oz) shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 pound sliced bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • 1 TBSP dill
  • ½ -1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 round loaf (1 pound) unsliced sourdough bread
  • Assorted fresh vegetables

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, beat cream cheese and mayo until blended.
  2. Stir in spinach, cheese, bacon, onion, dill and garlic; set aside.
  3. Cut a 1 ½ inch slice off top of bread; set aside. Carefully hollow out bottom, leaving a ½ inch shell. Cube removed bread and place on a baking sheet. Broil 3-4 inches from the heat for 1-2 minutes or until golden brown; set aside.
  4. Fill bread shell with spinach dip; replace top. Place any dip that doesn’t fit in the shell in a greased baking dish. Wrap bread in heavy duty foil; place on a baking sheet. Bake @ 350º for 1 hour or until dip is heated through. Cover and bake additional dip for 40-45 minutes or until heated through. Open foil carefully. Serve warm dip with vegetables and reserved bread cubes.
