Zucchini Pizza

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8-10 zucchini, diced and fried in margarine
  • 5 onions
  • 1 jar mushrooms
  • 1 cup tomato paste
  • Bacon
  • Cheese

Instructions

  1. Stir tomato sauce and a bit of cheese together with zucchini.
  2. Spread on bottom of pan.
  3. Top with mushrooms, onions and bacon.
  4. Bake @ 325° until heated through, then top with cheese.
