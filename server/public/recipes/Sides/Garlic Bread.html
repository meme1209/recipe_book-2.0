Garlic Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 quarts water (take from here for yeast)
  • 6 TBSP yeast
  • ¾ cup minced garlic
  • 10 TBSP sugar
  • 10 TBSP salt
  • Flour

Instructions

  1. Knead and let rise as you would regular bread. Weigh out 1 lb loaves – 3 per bun tray. Take scissors and cut slits across bread when half risen. Bake and then brush with garlic butter.
