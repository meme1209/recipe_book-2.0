Bacon Wrapped Water Chestnuts

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 TBSP honey
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ cup oyster sauce
  • 1 can water chestnuts

Instructions

  1. Heat marinade until warm. Soak water chestnuts for ½ to 1 day. Cut bacon into 4 pieces and wrap chestnuts. Bake until bacon is crisp.
