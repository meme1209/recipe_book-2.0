Zucchini Tomato Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Pie crust
  • 2 medium zucchini, cubed
  • ½ cup onion, chopped
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 2 tsp parsley
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp basil
  • ¼ tsp oregano
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • ¾ cup ham, cubed
  • 1 tomato, sliced

Instructions

  1. Prebake piecrust. Sauté zucchini and onion in butter until tender; stir in seasonings. Spoon into crust. Combine eggs, cheese and ham; pour over zucchini mixture. Top with tomato slices. Bake @ 375° for 20-25 minutes or until knife inserted in center comes clean.
