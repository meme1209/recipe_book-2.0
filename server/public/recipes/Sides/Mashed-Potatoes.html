Mashed Potatoes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet), peeled and cubed
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: black pepper, garlic powder, sour cream, or cream cheese

Instructions

  1. Place potatoes in a large pot and cover with cold water. Add a pinch of salt.
  2. Bring to a boil and cook until potatoes are fork-tender, about 15–20 minutes.
  3. Drain the potatoes and return them to the pot.
  4. Add butter and mash until mostly smooth.
  5. Gradually stir in milk until desired consistency is reached.
  6. Season with salt and optional ingredients to taste.
  7. Serve warm as a comforting side dish.
