Sweet and Sour

Ingredients

  • 3 cups white sugar
  • 1 cup vinegar
  • 4 cups cold water
  • ¾ cup corn starch
  • ¾ cup ketchup
  • ½ cup oil
  • 4 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and bring to boil. Boil until clear.
