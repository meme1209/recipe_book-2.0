Special Squash

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs spaghetti squash
  • ¾ cup milk
  • 6 TBSP melted margarine
  • 3 eggs
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 3 TBSP flour
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1/8 tsp cloves
  • 1/8 tsp nutmeg
  • Topping
  • ½ cup graham crumbs
  • ¼ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 TBSP melted margarine

Instructions

  1. Cook squash until soft. Blend with hand blender until smooth. Mix all ingredients together and bake for 30-40 minutes @ 325°.
  2. Top with topping mixture and bake for another 10-15 minutes.
