Italian Cheese Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cups flour
  • 4 tsp salt
  • 4 tsp sugar
  • ¼ cup yeast
  • 4 cups warm water
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup Italian dressing
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp thyme, optional
  • Dash of pepper

Instructions

  1. Make dough and let rise for 20-30 minutes. Press into greased bun trays. Press quite thin.
  2. Topping
  3. Combine all ingredients. Sprinkle over top of dough. Sprinkle with mozzarella. Bake @ 400° for 20 minutes or until golden brown. Serve warm.
  4. Notes
  5. Make this recipe x2.
  6. Make x5 for pizza.
