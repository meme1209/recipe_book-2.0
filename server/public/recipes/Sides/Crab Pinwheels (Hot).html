Crab Pinwheels (Hot)

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 package (8oz.) cream cheese
  • 1 can (16 oz.) crabmeat, drained and flaked
  • 3/4 cup finely chopped sweet red pepper
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 green onions, finely chopped
  • 3 TBSP minced parsley
  • 1/4 to 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 6 flour tortillas

Instructions

  1. In a small mixing bowl, beat cream cheese until smooth. Stir in
  2. the crab, red pepper, cheese, onions, parsley, and cayenne. Spread
  3. 1/3 cupful over one side of each tortilla; roll up tightly. Wrap
  4. in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least two hours.
  5. Cut and discard ends of roll-ups. Cut each into six slices. Place
  6. on baking sheets coated with non-stick cooking spray. Bake at 350°
  7. for 10 minutes or until bubbly. Serve warm.
  8. Yield: 3 dozen
