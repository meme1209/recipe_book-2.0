Challah Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ¼ 2 ½ cups warm water
  • ½ 1 cup sugar
  • 2 4 TBSP yeast
  • 4 ½ 9 cups warm milk
  • 2/3 1 1/3 cups lard
  • 6 12 eggs
  • 2 4 tsp salt
  • 1 ½ 2-3 gallons flour

Instructions

  1. Dissolve yeast in warm water. Stir in warm milk, sugar and salt. Add eggs and 1 ¼ cups of flour. Beat until smooth. Add enough flour to make dough manageable. Let rise until doubled, about 1 ½ - 2 hours. Divide into 3 parts and braid loosely. Brush with shortening. Let rise 40-50 minutes. Bake @ 375° for 25-30 minutes. Beat egg yolk and cold water. Brush over braid. Brush with garlic butter.
