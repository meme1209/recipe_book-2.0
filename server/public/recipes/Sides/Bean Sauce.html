Bean Sauce

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 litres water
  • 1 cup chicken soup base
  • ½ cup honey
  • 6 TBSP real lemon
  • 2 ½ TBSP oregano
  • 1/8 cup basil
  • ½ TBSP garlic powder
  • ½ cup cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Bring all ingredients to a boil and boil until thickened.
  2. Heat up beans.
  3. Drain.
  4. Right before serving, pour sauce over beans. Sprinkle with seasoning salt.
