Waffles

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb butter or margarine
  • 2 cups eggs
  • 4 cups milk
  • 4 cups flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix together milk and flour until smooth to make a thick paste.
  2. Separate egg whites from yolks.
  3. Beat egg whites until stiff.
  4. Mix egg yolks and butter into flour mixture.
  5. Then add egg whites.
