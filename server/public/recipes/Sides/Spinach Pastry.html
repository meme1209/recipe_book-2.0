Spinach Pastry

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 ¼ cups butter
  • 3 ¼ cups cream cheese
  • 6 ½ cups flour
  • Combine pastry ingredients and press into tart shells. Prebake until golden brown. Shells need to be poked about halfway through baking as they get bubbles.
  • Filling
  • 9 eggs
  • 5 ¼ cups shredded cheese
  • 4 ½ cups cooked spinach
  • 2 ¼ cups sour cream
  • 2 ¼ cups milk
  • 2 ¼ tsp salt
  • 2 ¼ tsp onion salt
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • Combine above ingredients and fill prebaked tart shells. Bake @ 300° for about 15 minutes.
