Creamed Onions

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 large onions, sliced
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 2 TBSP flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 2 cups milk

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, sauté onions in butter until tender and golden brown. Add flour, salt and pepper to onions; stir until smooth. Gradually stir in milk until blended, bring to boil; cook and stir for 2 minutes or until thickened. Reduce heat
