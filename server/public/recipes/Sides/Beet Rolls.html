Beet Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 large onions, chopped
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 2 ½ cups cream
  • ¼ cup dried dill
  • ¼ cup flour
  • Rice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Beet leaves

Instructions

  1. Fry onions in margarine until soft.
  2. Add cream, dill, flour, salt and pepper.
  3. Simmer. Do not boil.
  4. When sauce is ready pour over cooked rice, just until you can shape rice into a ball.
  5. Form balls and wrap in beet leaves.
  6. Arrange tightly in pan, and pour rest of sauce over rolls.
  7. Bake until heated through.
