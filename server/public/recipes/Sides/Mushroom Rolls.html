Mushroom Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 jar mushrooms
  • 2 cups onions, chopped
  • 4 cups cream cheese
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Melt butter; add mushrooms and onions.
  2. Cook 5-10 minutes or until onions are soft.
  3. Add next 5 ingredients and stir until cream cheese is melted; cool.
  4. Spread thinly on wraps.
  5. Roll up. Cut into ½” slices.
  6. Bake @ 350º for 10-15 minutes or until toasted.
