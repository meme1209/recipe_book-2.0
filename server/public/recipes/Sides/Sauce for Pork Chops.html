Sauce for Pork Chops

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ¼ cup chopped onions
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 ¼ cup vinegar
  • 2 ½ cups tomato juice
  • 2 ½ cups ketchup
  • 5 tsp soya sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 ½ tsp dry mustard
  • 2 ½ tsp salt
  • 2 ½ tsp paprika

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, melt margarine. Add onions and fry until soft.
  2. Mix together all rest of ingredients, adding onions and butter.
  3. Bring to a boil.
  4. Serve.
