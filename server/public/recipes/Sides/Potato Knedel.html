Potato Knedel

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Finely chop 1 full pail of potatoes.
  2. Extract as much water as possible in washing machine.
  3. Add ¾ stainless steel soup bowl of soup
  4. 1 cup lard
  5. ½ cup salt
  6. With medium ice cream scoop, make balls with potatoes and place into steamer trays at about 9: 30.
  7. Steam in combi until dinner.
