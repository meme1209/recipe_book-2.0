Carrot Casserole

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 lbs carrots, crushed
  • 10 eggs
  • 2 ½ chopped onions
  • 5 cups celery
  • 5 TBSP chopped parsley
  • 3 ¾ cup melted margarine
  • 5 tsp salt
  • 25 cups day old bread – or less

Instructions

  1. Cook carrots, drain and mash. Beat eggs; add onions, celery, parsley, margarine and salt. Stir in carrots. Add breadcrumbs. Pour into greased trays. Bake @ 350° for 30 – 40 minutes.
  2. Notes
  3. Make recipe x 2
  4. Makes 2 steamer trays.
  5. Needs 3 steamer trays for Heartland.
