Garlic Bread

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French bread or Italian bread
  • ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley (optional)
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Slice the bread in half lengthwise.
  3. In a bowl, mix softened butter, minced garlic, parsley, and salt.
  4. Spread the garlic butter mixture evenly over both halves of the bread.
  5. Place bread halves on a baking sheet, cut side up.
  6. Bake for 10–12 minutes, or until the edges are golden and crisp.
  7. Optional: Sprinkle with Parmesan and broil for 1–2 minutes for extra crispiness.
  8. Slice and serve warm.
