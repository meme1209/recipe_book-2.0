Pepper Poppers

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 package (8 oz) cream cheese
  • 1 cup (4oz) shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup (4oz) shredded Monterey Jack cheese
  • 6 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp chilli powder
  • 1 lb peppers, cut into quarters and seeded
  • ½ cup dry bread crumbs
  • Sour cream, onion dip, or Ranch salad dressing

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl combine the cheeses, bacon and seasonings; mix well.
  2. Spoon about 2 tablespoonfuls into each pepper section.
  3. Roll in bread crumbs.
  4. Place in baking pan.
  5. Bake, uncovered @325° for about 15 minutes.
  6. Serve with sour cream, dip or dressing
  7. Yield: 2 dozen
