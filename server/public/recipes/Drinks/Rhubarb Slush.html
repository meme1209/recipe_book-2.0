Rhubarb Slush

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cups water
  • 8 cups diced rhubarb (unpeeled)
  • 4 oz jello powder, any flavour
  • ½ cup real lemon
  • 2 cups sugar

Instructions

  1. Boil rhubarb in water until broken down. Strain for juice only. Add rest of ingredients.
  2. Notes
  3. Makes 1 ice-cream pail.
