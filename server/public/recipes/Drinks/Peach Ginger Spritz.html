Peach Ginger Spritz

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 gallons of freezer peaches
  • 8 cups sugar
  • 1 gallon shredded ginger fresh or frozen
  • Two bottles of real lemon
  • 12 cups of water
  • 1/4 cup vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Put the ginger in a mesh sack or cheese cloth.
  2. Combine all the ingredients in a pot and cook until the peaches are super soft.
  3. Take out the bag of ginger and blend thoroughly so you have a smooth puree.
  4. Add the ginger back in and cook the puree to reduce it slightly.
  5. Add peach food dye to enhance the color if desired.

Rimmer Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp citric acid
  • Half a cup of peach jello powder

Assembly

  1. Dip the rim of a cup into the peach ginger puree and then into the rimmer.
  2. Add 1/3 cup of the puree.
  3. Add ice and sparkling water or Sprite.
