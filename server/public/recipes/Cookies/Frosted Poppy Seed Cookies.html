Frosted Poppy Seed Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 7 ½ cups butter
  • 7 ½ cups shortening
  • 15 cups sugar
  • 15 eggs
  • 5 TBSP vanilla
  • 45 cups flour
  • 5 TBSP baking soda
  • 5 TBSP salt
  • 15 cups sour cream
  • 3 ¾ cups poppy seeds
  • 3 cups butter
  • 22 cups icing sugar
  • 8 tsp vanilla
  • 3 cups milk

Instructions

  1. Cream butter, shortening and sugar until fluffy. Beat in eggs and vanilla. Combine dry ingredients; add to creamed mixture alternately with sour cream. Stir in poppy seeds. Refrigerate overnight. Bake @ 350° for 8-10 minutes or until set and edges are lightly browned.
  2. Frosting
  3. Beat all ingredients together with just enough milk to achieve the desired consistency. Frost cookies.
  4. Notes
  5. Use smallest scoop to get 600 cookies
  6. Don’t grease cookie sheets
