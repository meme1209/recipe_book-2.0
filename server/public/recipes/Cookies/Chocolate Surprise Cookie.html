Chocolate Surprise Cookie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • X15 x20
  • 1 ½ 22.5 cups flour 30 cups
  • ½ 7 ½ cups unsweetened cocoa 10 cups
  • ½ 7 ½ tsp baking soda 10 tsp
  • ¼ 3 ¾ tsp salt 5 tsp
  • ½ 7 ½ cups sugar (plus more for dipping) 10 cups
  • ½ 7 ½ cups packed brown sugar 10 cups
  • ½ 7 ½ cups margarine 10 cups
  • ¼ 3 ¾ cups creamy peanut butter 5 cups
  • 1 tsp 5 TBSP vanilla 6 ½ TBSP
  • 1 15 eggs 20
  • Inside
  • ½ 7 ½ cups creamy peanut butter 10 cups
  • ½ 7 ½ cups icing sugar 10 cups

Instructions

  1. Whisk together flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt. Set aside.
  2. Beat together margarine, both sugars and peanut butter until fluffy. Add vanilla and eggs, beating to combine. Stir in flour mixture and stir to combine, blending well. Set aside.
  3. Mix together peanut butter and icing sugar until smooth.
  4. Pull off heaping tablespoon of cookie dough and flatten with your hands. Take about a teaspoon of peanut butter mixture and place it in the center of the cookie dough. Wrap the chocolate dough around the peanut butter center, pressing to seal. Dip the top of the cookie in sugar and place on greased baking sheet. Flatten to about ½ inch thickness.
  5. Bake in preheated oven for 7-9 minutes @300º. Let stand on baking sheet for 2 minutes, then move to wire rack to cool.
  6. Notes
  7. Made x15 May 2013. Not enough.
  8. Do not use peanut butter that is too soft for centers.
  9. Make centers night before and freeze for better handling.
  10. Stir together cookies just before baking. If too dry add a bit of water to cookie dough.
