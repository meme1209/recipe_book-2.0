Strawberry Glaze Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 20 28 cups sugar
  • 2 40 56 cups flour
  • 1 10 14 cups white cake mix
  • ¾ 7 ½ 10 ½ cups canola oil
  • 1 10 14 cups cream
  • 2 20 28 tsp baking powder
  • 2 20 28 tsp baking soda
  • 1 10 14 tsp salt
  • 1 10 14 tsp vanilla
  • 3 30 42 eggs
  • 24 cups strawberry juice
  • 12 cups sugar
  • 1 ½ lbs strawberry jello powder
  • 2 cups cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Sift together flour, cake mix, baking soda, baking powder and salt. Mix together oil, sugar and eggs. Then alternately, add flour, cream and vanilla. Can stand overnight. Bake approximately 10 minutes @ 325°. Use smallest scoop. When cooled, spread glaze and Cool Whip between cookies.
  2. Strawberry Glaze
  3. Cook first 3 ingredients until boiling. Then add cornstarch. Boil until thickened.
  4. Notes
  5. Needs 6 containers Cool Whip.
  6. Needs 2 dairy queen containers strawberries- just the juice.
  7. Add a bit of homemade cake icing to Cool Whip so cookies don’t get as soggy.
