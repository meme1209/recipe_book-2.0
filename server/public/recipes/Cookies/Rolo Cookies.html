Rolo Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 12 20 boxes chocolate cake mix
  • 1 12 20 eggs
  • ¼ 3 5 cups water
  • ¼ 3 5 cups vegetable oil
  • Rolo candy

Instructions

  1. Combine first 4 ingredients and mix well.
  2. Shape cookies into balls and press the candy into the center.
  3. Do not expose candy.
  4. Place onto ungreased cookie sheet.
  5. Bake @ 300° for 8-10 minutes.
  6. Cool before removing from pan.
