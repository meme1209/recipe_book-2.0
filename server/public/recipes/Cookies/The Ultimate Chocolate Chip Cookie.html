The Ultimate Chocolate Chip Cookie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup butter flavoured Crisco
  • 1 ¼ cups brown sugar, packed
  • 2 tbsp. milk
  • 1 tbsp. vanilla
  • 1 egg
  • 1 ¾ cup flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup pecan pieces, optional

Instructions

  1. Combine Crisco, brown sugar, milk, and vanilla in a large bowl. Beat at medium until creamy. Beat egg into creamed mixture. Combine flour, salt and baking soda and mix into creamed mixture until blended. Stir in chocolate chips and pecans. Bake @ 275 for 15-20 minutes for crisp cookie or 375 for 8-10 minutes for chewy cookie. Cookies will appear moist, do not overbake.
  2. Yields: 3 dozen
