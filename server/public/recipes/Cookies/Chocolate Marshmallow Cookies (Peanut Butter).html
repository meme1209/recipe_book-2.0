Chocolate Marshmallow Cookies (Peanut Butter)

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ¼ cup unsweetened cocoa
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ cup margarine
  • 10 TBSP sugar
  • ¼ cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1 egg
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • 7 tsp milk
  • Pinch of salt
  • Large marshmallows (halved crosswise)
  • 2 ¼ cups icing sugar
  • 4 ½ TBSP margarine (room temperature)
  • ¼ cup unsweetened cocoa
  • ¼ cup hot water
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Mix together all ingredients and drizzle over cookies.

Instructions

  1. Whisk flour, cocoa and baking powder. Cream butter, sugar and peanut butter until fluffy. Add flour mixture alternately with milk, egg and vanilla mixture. Refrigerate dough for 1 hour. Scoop onto parchment paper- lined cookie sheets. Bake @ 350° for 8-10 minutes. Remove from oven. Place half a marshmallow on each cookie. Return to oven for 2 minutes. Cool.
