Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and sugar together until fluffy. Add eggs and whip until well mixed. Stir together dry ingredients and add to mixture with cream.
  2. Icing
  3. Mix together and boil until thickened. Spread between cookies.
  4. Notes
  5. Large recipe makes 1000 cookies with medium scoop.
