Turtle Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 20 boxes chocolate cake mix
  • 1 20 eggs
  • ¼ 5 cups water
  • ¼ 5 cups vegetable oil
  • ¾ 15 cups chopped pecans
  • ½ 10 cups skor chips
  • ¼ 5 cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Combine first 4 ingredients and mix well.
  2. Fold in last 3 ingredients until well blended.
  3. Place cookies on ungreased cookie sheet.
  4. Bake @ 325° for 10-12 minutes.
  5. Notes
  6. Used smallest ice cream scoop
