Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ 40 cup flour
  • 1 16 tsp baking soda
  • 1 16 cups margarine
  • ¼ 4 cup white sugar
  • ¾ 12 cups brown sugar
  • 1 16 tsp vanilla
  • ½ 8 cups vanilla instant pudding
  • 2 32 eggs
  • 1 16 cups milk chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Combine margarine, sugar, and pudding mix. Beat till smooth and creamy. Beat in eggs and vanilla. Gradually add flour and baking soda. Stir in chips. Batter will be stiff. Bake @ 275 for 12 minutes.
