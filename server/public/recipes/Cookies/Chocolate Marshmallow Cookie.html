Chocolate Marshmallow Cookie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 12 15 cups margarine, softened
  • 2 24 30 cups sugar
  • 2 24 30 eggs
  • ½ 6 7 ½ cups milk
  • 2tsp 8tbsp 10tbsp vanilla
  • 3 ½ 42 52 ½ cups flour
  • ½ 6 7 ½ cups cocoa
  • 1tsp ¼cup 5tbsp baking powder
  • 1tsp ¼cup 5tbsp salt
  • Large marshmallows
  • Icing:
  • 4tbsp 3 ¾ cups margarine
  • ½tbsp 1 ¾ cups cocoa
  • 3tbsp 2 ¾ cups milk
  • 1 15 cups icing sugar
  • ½ 7 ½ tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Cream butter and sugar; add milk, eggs and vanilla and mix well. Combine flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt; beat into creamed mixture. Drop onto ungreased baking sheet. Bake @ 350 for 8 minutes. Meanwhile cut marshmallows in half, place cut side down onto each cookie. Return to oven for 2 minutes.
  2. Icing: Combine butter, cocoa, milk and bring to a boil, boil for 1 minute stirring constantly. Cool slightly; transfer to mixing bowl and add icing sugar and vanilla and beat well. Spread over cooled cookies.
