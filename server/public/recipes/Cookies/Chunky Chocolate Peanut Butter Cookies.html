Chunky Chocolate Peanut Butter Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup margarine
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup peanut butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 ½ cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¾ cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Beat margarine, white sugar, brown sugar and peanut butter until fluffy.
  2. Blend in eggs and vanilla.
  3. Add dry ingredients and mix well. Mix in chocolate chips.
  4. Bake @ 350° for 10 to 12 minutes or until lightly brown.
