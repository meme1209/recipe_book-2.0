Chewy Oatmeal Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 7 ½ lbs margarine
  • 10 15 cups brown sugar
  • 10 15 cups white sugar
  • 20 30 eggs
  • 6 ½ 9 ¾ tsp vanilla
  • 19 28 ½ oatmeal
  • 7 ½ 10 cups nuts (optional)
  • 7 ½ 10 chocolate chips (optional)
  • 6 ½ 9 ¾ TBSP baking soda
  • 21 31 ½ cups flour
  • 1 1 ½ TBSP salt

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and both sugars together until fluffy.
  2. Add eggs and vanilla and beat until smooth.
  3. Stir together dry ingredients and mix into above mixture.
  4. Bake @ 300° for 10-12 minutes.
