Oatmeal Pumpkin Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 27 cups flour
  • 13 ½ cups rolled oats
  • 10 cups margarine
  • 6 ¾ cups white sugar
  • 13 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 6 ¾ cups chocolate chips
  • 7 eggs
  • 2 ¼ TBSP vanilla
  • 2 ¼ TBSP salt
  • 4 ½ TBSP cinnamon
  • 4 ½ TBSP baking soda
  • 13 ½ cups pumpkin

Instructions

  1. Cream together margarine and sugar until fluffy.
  2. Add eggs, vanilla and pumpkin.
  3. Sift together dry ingredients.
  4. Fold into creamed mixture.
  5. Add chocolate chips.
  6. Bake @ 325° for 12 minutes.
