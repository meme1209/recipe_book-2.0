Pinwheel Refrigerator Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ¾ cups 42 cups flour
  • 2 tsp 1 cup baking powder
  • ¼ tsp 2 TBSP salt
  • ½ cup 12 cups margarine
  • 1 cup 24 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 24 eggs
  • 1 tsp ½ cup vanilla

Instructions

  1. Combine flour, baking powder and salt. Cream margarine, sugar, egg and vanilla thoroughly. Add dry ingredients, mixing well. Work with hands to a smooth dough. Roll dough between 2 sheets of waxed paper. Spread filling over dough. Roll up tightly. Wrap in wax paper and chill. Cut into slices. Place on greased cookie sheets. Bake @ 375° for 10-15 minutes, or until golden.
  2. Notes
  3. I froze rolls overnight, and allowed to thaw in fridge a little before cutting.
  4. I cooked apricots for filling, can also try dried apples.
