Watermelon Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ½ tsp almond extract
  • 2 ¼ cups flour
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • Red and green food coloring
  • Mini chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Beat in egg and extract. Combine the flour, salt and baking powder; gradually add to creamed mixture and mix well.
  2. Remove 1 cup of dough; set aside. Add enough red food coloring to tint remaining dough deep red, roll into a 3 ½ inch long log; wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate until firm, about 2 hours.
  3. Divide the 1 cup of reserved dough into 2 pieces. To one piece, add enough green food coloring to tint the dough a deep green. Leave remaining dough plain. Wrap each piece separately in plastic wrap; chill until firm, about an hour.
  4. On a floured sheet of wax paper, roll white dough into an 8 ½ inch x 3 ½ inch rectangle. Place red dough along short end of rectangle. Roll up and encircle red dough with white dough; set aside
  5. On floured waxed paper, roll the green dough into a 10 inch x 3 ½ inch rectangle. Place log of red/white dough along the short end of the green dough. Roll up and encircle log with green dough. Cover tightly with plastic wrap; refrigerate at least 8 hours or overnight.
  6. Unwrap dough and cut into 1/8 inch slices. Place 1 inch apart on ungreased baking sheets. Lightly press chocolate chips into each slice to resemble seeds.
  7. Bake @ 350º for 6-8 minutes or until cookies are firm but not brown. While still warm, cut each cookie in half. Remove to wire racks to cool.
  8. Yield: 3 dozen
