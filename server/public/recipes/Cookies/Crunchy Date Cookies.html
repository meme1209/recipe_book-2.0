Crunchy Date Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cups butter or margarine
  • 10 cups white sugar
  • 10 cups packed brown sugar
  • 20 eggs
  • 20 cups flour
  • 10 tsp vanilla
  • 7 TBSP baking powder
  • 5 tsp salt
  • 10 tsp cinnamon
  • 5 tsp ginger
  • 5 tsp nutmeg
  • 30 cups cornflakes
  • 20 cups chopped dates

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and both sugars.
  2. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
  3. Mix all dry ingredients together.
  4. Add to mixture.
  5. Fold in cornflakes and dates.
  6. Drop by spoonfuls on a greased cookie sheet.
  7. Bake @ 350° for 10-12 minutes.
  8. Notes
  9. Mix together just before baking.
