Chocolate Sandwiches

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 16 cups margarine
  • 32 cups sugar
  • 32 eggs
  • 10 TBSP vanilla
  • 32 cups milk
  • 64 cups flour
  • 5 1/3 Tbsp baking powder
  • 5 1/3 TBSP baking soda
  • 8 tsp salt
  • 16 cups cocoa
  • 1 ¼ cups gelatine
  • 5 cups cold water
  • 18 cups white sugar
  • 7 ½ cups water
  • 2 TBSP salt
  • 10 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together in order mentioned, beating until smooth. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto greased cookie sheet. Bake @ 400° for 7 minutes. Cool and make sandwiches.
  2. Marshmallow Filling
  3. Soak gelatine in 5 cups cold water. Heat sugar in 7 ½ cups water until dissolved. Add gelatine and boil until it pulls strings ( ½ hour is just right). Let stand overnight. Add salt and vanilla. Beat with mixture until fluffy.
  4. Notes
  5. Did not grease pans. Recipe big enough. Use small scoop.
