Chocolate Marshmallow Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 12 15 cups margarine, softened
  • 2 24 30 cups sugar
  • 2 24 30 eggs
  • ½ 6 7 ½ cups milk
  • 2 tsp 8 TBSP 10 TBSP vanilla
  • 3 ½ 42 52 ½ cups flour
  • ½ 6 7 ½ cups cocoa
  • 1 tsp ¼ cup 5 TBSP baking powder
  • 1 tsp ¼ cup 5 TBSP salt
  • Large marshmallows

Instructions

  1. Cream butter and sugar; add milk, eggs and vanilla and mix well.
  2. Combine flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt; beat into creamed mixture.
  3. Drop by rounded teaspoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets.
  4. Bake @350º for 8 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile cut marshmallows in half, cut side down, onto each cookie. Return to oven for 2 minutes. Cool completely. Spread with icing.
  6. Icing
  7. 4 TBSP 3 ¾ 4 ¾ cups margarine
  8. ½ TBSP 1 ¾ 2 ¼ cups cocoa
  9. 3 TBSP 2 ¾ 3 ½ cups milk
  10. 1 15 18 ¾ cups icing sugar
  11. ½ tsp 4 5 tsp vanilla
  12. Combine butter, cocoa and milk and bring to a boil.
  13. Boil for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  14. Cool slightly; transfer to a small mixing bowl and add icing sugar and vanilla and beat well. Spread over cooled cookies.
