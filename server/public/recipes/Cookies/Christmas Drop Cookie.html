Christmas Drop Cookie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 cups margarine
  • 5 cups Lard
  • 11 cups sugar
  • 30 eggs
  • 2 ½ tsp nutmeg
  • 25 cups flour
  • 10 tsp baking soda
  • 15 cups raisins
  • 10 cups walnuts
  • 10 cups cherries
  • 15 cups dates
  • 20 cups candied fruit

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and lard. Add sugar and beat, add eggs. Then add flour, nutmeg, baking soda. Mix in raisins, walnuts, cherries, dates, and candied fruit.
