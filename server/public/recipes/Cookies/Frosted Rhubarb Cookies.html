Frosted Rhubarb Cookies

Instructions

  1. Cream shortening and brown sugar. Beat in eggs. Combine flour, baking soda and salt; gradually add to creamed mixture. Stir in coconut and rhubarb. Drop by tablespoonfuls 2 inches apart on greased cookie sheet. Bake @ 350° for 12-15 minutes or until lightly browned. Cool.
  2. In a mixing bowl, beat cream cheese, margarine and vanilla. Gradually beat in icing sugar until smooth. Spread over cooled cookies.
  3. Yield: 5 dozen
