Sour Cream Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cups margarine, melted
  • 9 cups white sugar
  • 6 tbsp. baking powder
  • 6 tbsp. baking soda
  • 6 cups sour cream
  • 3 cups corn syrup
  • 3 tsp salt
  • 9 tsp vanilla
  • 33 cups flour
  • 18 eggs
  • Topping:
  • 8 egg whites
  • 4 cups water
  • 8 cups sugar
  • 8 tbsp. unflavored gelatin
  • 2 cups cold water
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Sift flour soda, salt, and baking powder. Mix in sugar.
  2. Mix butter, eggs, sour cream, vanilla and corn syrup, stir in flour mixture. Don’t grease pans, use a lot of flour when you roll out dough. Bake @ 350.
  3. Topping: Beat egg whites until stiff. Boil the first water and sugar.
  4. Mix gelatin in the second water. Add to hot mixture, stir well, and pour slowly into egg whites, while beating on high speed, add vanilla. Beat till cool and tops stand in stiff peaks.
  5. Notes
  6. Don’t add more flour. Let dough stand overnight or half an hour in cooler.
  7. Do not enlarge recipe, make twice if more is needed.
