Peanut Butter Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups margarine
  • 8 cups peanut butter
  • 8 cups white sugar
  • 8 cups brown sugar
  • 16 eggs
  • 8 tsp vanilla
  • 8 tsp salt
  • 8 tsp baking soda
  • 16 cups flour

Instructions

  1. Cream butter, beat in sugar. Add other ingredients, add more flour if necessary to make mixture pliable. Form balls and flatten with fork. Bake @ 350for 10-12 min.
