Russian Tea Biscuits

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 12 lbs butter (no substitutes)
  • 2 3 TBSP salt
  • 8 12 cups icing sugar
  • 16 24 cups pecans, roasted and chopped
  • 32 48 cups flour
  • 5 1/3 8 TBSP cream

Instructions

  1. Cream butter until fluffy. Add rest of ingredients. Bake @ 325° for 20-25 minutes or till slightly brown. Cool and roll in icing sugar.
  2. Notes
  3. Press the cookies flat with your hand before baking
  4. No need to grease pan.
  5. Made recipe x1 ½ December 2003
