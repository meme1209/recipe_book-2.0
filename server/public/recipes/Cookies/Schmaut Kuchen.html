Schmaut Kuchen

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 8 16 cups sugar
  • 2 4 8 cups margarine
  • 8 16 32 tsp baking powder
  • 2 4 8 tsp vanilla
  • 1 2 4 tsp salt
  • 4 8 16 cups buttermilk
  • 4 8 16 eggs
  • 4 8 16 tsp baking soda
  • 11 22 44 cups flour
  • 2 cups 6 cups margarine
  • 8 cups 24 cups icing sugar
  • 2 tsp 2 TBSP coconut extract
  • 8 TBSP 1 ½ cup cream
  • 8 cups 24 cups icing sugar
  • 2 cups 6 cups milk

Instructions

  1. Cream together margarine and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs and beat well. Add rest of ingredients and let the mixer run for 2 or 3 minutes. Put dough in fridge overnight. Drop by teaspoons onto greased cookie sheets. Bake @ 400° for 10-12 minutes or until firm to touch. Cool, spread with icing and dip into coconut noodle.
  2. Icing
  3. Cream margarine until fluffy and then add 8 or 24 cups icing sugar. Blend until smooth. Stir in coconut extract and cream. Add 8 or 24 cups icing sugar and milk. Beat until nice and fluffy.
