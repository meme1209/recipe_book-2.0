Oatmeal Date Cookie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 12 18 cups margarine
  • 16 24 cups brown sugar
  • 16 24 eggs, beaten
  • 8 12 tsp baking soda
  • 8 12 tsp baking powder
  • 24 36 cups flour
  • 24 36 cups oatmeal
  • 4 6 cups milk
  • 4 6 tbsp. vanilla
  • Filling:
  • 15 22 ½ cups dates
  • 1 ½ 2 ¼ cups brown sugar
  • 6 9 cups water
  • Lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Cream together brown sugar and margarine. Add beaten eggs and beat well. Add vanilla and beat. Sift all dry ingredients into one bowl. Mix dry ingredients alternatively with milk into butter mixture. Let cool in cooler before making cookies. Roll in balls and press with fork. Bake @ 350 for 10-15 minutes. Sandwich with date filling.
