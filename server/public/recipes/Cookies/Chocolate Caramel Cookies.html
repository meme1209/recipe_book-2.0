Chocolate Caramel Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 10 cups butter, softened
  • 1 10 cups white sugar
  • 1 10 cups packed brown sugar
  • 2 20 eggs
  • 2 20 tsp vanilla (6 Tbsp + 2 tsp )
  • 2 ½ 25 cups flour
  • ¾ 7 ½ cups baking cocoa, sifted
  • 1 10 tsp baking soda (3 TBSP + 1 tsp)
  • 1 10 cups pecans, divided
  • 1 10 13 oz packages Rolo candies

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, cream butter and both sugars.
  2. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
  3. Combine flour, baking cocoa and baking soda.
  4. Gradually add to creamed mixture just until combined.
  5. Stir in ½ cup (5 cups for large recipe) pecans.
  6. Using smallest ice-cream scoop, drop cookies onto ungreased cookie sheet.
  7. Press a Rolo candy into each cookie and shape dough around it.
  8. Dip halfway into pecans mixed with sugar.
  9. Bake @ 350° for 7-10 minutes or until top is lightly cracked.
  10. Cool for 3 minutes, then move to wire racks to cool completely.
  11. Yield: small recipe about 50 cookies
  12. Notes
  13. I used cookie program in combi. 1 space after light, and short (10 minutes).
