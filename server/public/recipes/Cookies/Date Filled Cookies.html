Date Filled Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 lbs butter (or half and half with lard)
  • 10 cups white sugar
  • 10 cups brown sugar
  • 40 cups flour
  • 30 eggs
  • 10 tsp baking soda
  • 10 tsp vanilla
  • Filling:
  • 10 lbs dates
  • 5 cups sugar
  • 25 cups water

Instructions

  1. Mix butter, sugar, flour, add eggs and remaining ingredients. Roll out dough about ½ inch thick. Spread date filling and roll. Freeze overnight. Bake @ 275.
  2. Filling: Cook till soft and cool.
