Raspberry Cheesecake Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 6 white cake mix
  • 1 6 eggs
  • 5 30 TBSP water
  • 5 30 TBSP vegetable oil
  • 1 6 3-oz package cream cheese, cubed
  • 1 ½ 9 cups raspberries, freeze dried

Instructions

  1. Mix together cake mix, egg, water and oil.
  2. Beat for 2 minutes on medium.
  3. Fold in frozen raspberries and cream cheese.
  4. Drop by spoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheet.
  5. Bake for 12 minutes @ 300°.
