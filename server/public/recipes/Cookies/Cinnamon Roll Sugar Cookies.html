Cinnamon Roll Sugar Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 6 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 12 TBSP softened butter,( slice 2 TBSP 6 times totaling 12 TBSP)
  • 1 ½ cups packed brown sugar, divided into 1/4 cups
  • 1 ½ TBSP cinnamon, divided
  • Frosting
  • 8 oz cream cheese
  • ½ cup icing sugar
  • 2-4 Tbsp milk, to thin icing

Instructions

  1. In an electric or stand mixer, cream the sugar and butter. Add eggs, vanilla and sour cream until well combined. Slowly add in the flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt until well combined.
  2. In 2 batches, place in plastic wrap in a disk shape and chill for at least 2 hours for best results.
  3. Once chilled, cut each disc into thirds and roll into a 1/8 inch thick oval, about 12 inchesx5 inches. Spread each rolled piece of dough with 2 TBSP of softened butter, ¼ cup packed brown sugar and ¼ tsp cinnamon start rolling from the long end closest to you, rolling into a log shape. Using a sharp knife, cut ½ inch pieces of dough then placing into a parchment lined baking sheet`. Bake @ 350° for 9-11 minutes. Remove and let cool for 5 minutes before removing from baking sheets.
  4. To prepare frosting, beat the cream cheese until softened and smooth; slowly add icing sugar and milk until desired consistency is reached. Frost cookies, then place in refrigerator until ready to serve.
  5. Yield: 6 dozen
