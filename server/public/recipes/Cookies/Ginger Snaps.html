Ginger Snaps

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups 3 12 cups margarine
  • 2 cups 4 16 cups white sugar
  • 1 ½ 3 12 eggs
  • ¼ cup ½ 2 cups molasses
  • 3 ¾ cup 7 ½ 30 cups white flour
  • 1 ½ TBSP 3 T ¾ cup ginger
  • ¾ TBSP 1 ½ T 1/3 cup baking soda
  • ½ TBSP 1 T ¼ cup cinnamon
  • Dash 1/8 t ½ tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Cream together margarine and sugar until fluffy.
  2. Add eggs and molasses until smooth.
  3. The next morning, add rest of ingredients, and blend well.
  4. Bake @ 325° for 10-12 minutes.
  5. Notes
  6. Make x2 for Heartland
