Oatmeal Fruit-Filled Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 18 cups 19 ½ cups margarine
  • 18 cups 19 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 4 ½ cups 4 7/8 cups milk
  • 6 TBSP 6 ½ TBSP baking soda
  • ¾ cup 13 TBSP baking powder
  • 27 cups 29 ¼ cups rolled oats
  • 30 cups 32 ½ cups flour
  • 15 cups water
  • 2 lbs raisins
  • 1 lbs figs
  • 4 lbs dried apples
  • 8 lbs dates
  • 6 TBSP vanilla

Instructions

  1. Beat margarine for 15 minutes. Add sugar and beat for another 15 minutes. Then add milk with baking powder and baking soda. Stir in oatmeal and flour. Let stand in fridge overnight. Roll dough out between 2 pieces of waxed paper. Spread filling on one piece of dough, and top this with second piece. Cut out cookies with round cookie cutters. (Scraps can be rolled out and used to make marbled cookies.) bake @ 300° for 25 minutes.
  2. Filling
  3. Steam this mixture in combi for about ½ hour. Put through grinder. Cool add more hot water if not spreadable enough.
  4. Notes
  5. Filling recipe is too big. Yield: 540 cookies
