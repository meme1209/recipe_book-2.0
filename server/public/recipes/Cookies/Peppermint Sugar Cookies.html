Peppermint Sugar Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ 27 ½ cups cake flour
  • 2 22 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 ½ 16 ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ 5 ½ tsp salt
  • ½ 5 ½ cups unsalted butter
  • ¼ 2 ¾ cups vegetable shortening
  • 1 11 cups sugar
  • 1 11 large eggs
  • 1 11 large egg whites
  • 2 22 tsp vanilla
  • ½ 5 ½ tsp peppermint extract
  • Peppermint Frosting:
  • 4oz 5 bricks cream cheese
  • 4 tbsp. 2 ¾ cup unsalted butter
  • ½ 3 ½ tsp peppermint extract
  • 2 ½ 17 ½ cups powdered sugar

Instructions

  1. Sift flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt into a large bowl, then whisk it just a few times, set aside. In mixer on medium speed, whip together butter, shortening, and sugar until very pale and fluffy, about 4 minutes, scraping down sides as needed. Add in egg and mix until combined, then add egg whites, vanilla and extract. Mix till combined. With mixer running, slowly add in dry ingredients and mix till combined. Refrigerate 2 hours. Make dough ball about 3 tbsp. Place ball on lightly floured surface, sprinkle top lightly with flour, use something flat and smooth to flatten to about ½ inch thick. Transfer cookies to wax paper and bake 9-11 minutes at 375. Allow to cool 5 minutes on baking sheet before transferring to wire rack. Frost with peppermint frosting and top with candy canes
