Chocolate Chip Cookies (with pudding)

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 22 ½ cups flour
  • 10 tsp baking soda
  • 10 cups margarine
  • 2 ½ cups white sugar
  • 7 ½ cups brown sugar
  • 10 tsp vanilla
  • 4 lbs 10 oz. vanilla instant pudding
  • 20 eggs
  • 60 oz. chocolate chips
  • 2 tbsp. salt

Instructions

  1. Mix flour with baking soda. Combine sugar, margarine, and pudding in large mixing bowl, mix until smooth and creamy. Beat in eggs and salt. Gradually add flour mixture. Then stir in chocolate chips. Bake @ 300 for 10-12 minutes.
  2. Note:
  3. We also add skor chips
