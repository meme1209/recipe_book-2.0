Carrot Cookies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 16 2/3 cups margarine
  • 15 2/3 cups sugar
  • 17 eggs
  • 16 2/3 cups cooked, mashed carrots
  • 33 ¾ cups flour (sifted twice)
  • 11 ¼ TBSP baking powder
  • 3 TBSP salt
  • 5 ¼ TBSP vanilla
  • 2 ¾ cups margarine, melted
  • 2 cups orange juice
  • 5 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Cream margarine and sugar until fluffy. Add carrots, eggs and vanilla. Beat well. Sift dry ingredients and add to creamed mixture. Refrigerate overnight. Bake @ 350° for 10-12 minutes.
  2. Orange Icing
  3. Mix together. Add enough icing sugar to make a good spreading consistency. Spread on hot or cooled cookies.
  4. Notes
  5. Do not grease pans.
  6. This recipe makes about 650 cookies if made with small scoop.
  7. Icing recipe is big enough.
