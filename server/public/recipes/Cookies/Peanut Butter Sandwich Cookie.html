Peanut Butter Sandwich Cookie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup 9 cups shortening
  • 1 cup 9 cups creamy peanut butter
  • 1 cup 9 cups sugar
  • 1 cup 9 cups packed brown sugar
  • 1 tsp 3 TBSP vanilla extract
  • 3 27 eggs
  • 3 cups 27 cups flour
  • 2 tsp 6 TBSP baking soda
  • ¼ tsp 2 ¼ tsp salt
  • Filling
  • ½ cup 4 ½ cups peanut butter
  • 3 cups 27 cups icing sugar
  • 1 tsp 3 TBSP vanilla
  • 6 TBSP 3 1/3 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Cream shortening, peanut butter and sugars. Add vanilla. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Combine baking soda, flour and salt; add to creamed mixture. Shape with smallest ice-cream scoop, and flatten with a fork. Bake @ 350° for 7-8 minutes.
  2. Stir filling ingredients together, adding milk and icing sugar to make a good spreading consistency. Spread on cookies.
