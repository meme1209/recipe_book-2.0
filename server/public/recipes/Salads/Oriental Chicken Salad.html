Oriental Chicken Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Chicken strips, seasoned and breaded and fried
  • Lettuce
  • Shredded carrots
  • Onion
  • Sliced almonds
  • Chowmein noodles
  • Sunflower seeds
  • ¾ cup honey
  • 3/8 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 ½ TBSP mustard
  • ½ tsp sesame seed oil

Instructions

  1. In a pan, layer ingredients in order given except for chicken. Place the chicken on top and drizzle with dressing.
  2. Dressing
