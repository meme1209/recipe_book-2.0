Onion Cucumber Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 7 medium cucumbers, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 small green pepper, cut into thin rings
  • 2 TBSP salt
  • 1 TBSP celery seed
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup vinegar

Instructions

  1. Combine cucumbers, onions and peppers.
  2. Sprinkle with salt and celery seed.
  3. Combine sugar and vinegar; pour over vegetables.
  4. Refrigerate at least 8 hours.
  5. Notes
  6. Taste just like cooler pickles
