Dorothy Lynch Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 3/8 cup sugar
  • 2 cups ketchup
  • 2 TBSP Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ TBSP garlic salt
  • ½ TBSP onion salt
  • ½ TBSP paprika
  • 1 cup canola oil
  • 2 cups mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup vinegar
  • ¼ cup poppy seeds

Instructions

  1. Mix together sugar, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, garlic salt, onion salt and paprika.
  2. Beat on high speed for 15 minutes.
  3. While beating on high, add oil and mayo.
  4. Add vinegar last.
  5. Stir in poppy seeds.
