Oriental Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Chicken cutlets
  • Baby spinach
  • Onion greens
  • Red peppers
  • Raw peas
  • Romaine lettuce
  • Wonton strips
  • Mandarin oranges
  • Slivered almonds
  • Dressing
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • ½ cup canola oil
  • 2 TBSP lemon juice
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 1 TBSP fresh grated ginger
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 2 tsp mustard
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Blend all ingredients in blender.
