Overnight Coleslaw

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 medium head cabbage
  • 1 green pepper, chopped
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, shredded
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp dry mustard powder
  • 1 tsp celery seed
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup vinegar
  • ¾ cup vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Combine vegetables and sprinkle with sugar.
  2. Dressing
  3. Bring to a boil. Pour over salad and cover. Refrigerate overnight. Stir well before serving.
