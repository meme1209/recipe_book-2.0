Fried Noodle Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 90-gram boxes (or 2 ½ kg) spaghetti noodle
  • 8 cups celery
  • ½ cup chopped onions
  • 4 cups mayonnaise
  • ¾ cup chicken soup base
  • ½ cup soya sauce
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ¾ cup vinegar
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion salt

Instructions

  1. Cook noodle according to package directions. Cool, drain and fry. Mix all dressing ingredients together and pour over noodle, celery and onions just before serving.
