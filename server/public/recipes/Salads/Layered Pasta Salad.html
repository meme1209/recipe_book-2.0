Layered Pasta Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Shell macaroni, cooked and drained
  • Red onion, sliced
  • Frozen peas, thawed and drained
  • Smoked ham, cubed
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 cup Ranch
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Tomatoes, cubed

Instructions

  1. Layer first 4 ingredients in dish. Mix dressings; spread over salad.
  2. Top with cheese and tomatoes.
  3. Refrigerate several hours or until chilled. Toss gently before serving.
