Spaghetti Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 quart spaghetti, broken up
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • ¼ cup vinegar
  • 2 TBSP sugar
  • 2 TBSP sesame seeds
  • 1 cup diced ham
  • 4 TBSP soya sauce
  • 1 TBSP salt

Instructions

  1. Cook and cool spaghetti. Pour soya sauce over noodle. Mix together rest of ingredients and stir all together.
