Japanese Cabbage Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 small heads cabbage
  • 2 lbs spaghetti noodles
  • 2 cups sesame seeds, toasted
  • 12 cups bean sprouts
  • ½ cup onions, chopped
  • 2 cups sliced almonds, toasted
  • 2 cups sunflower seeds
  • Dressing
  • 1 cup cooking oil
  • 10 TBSP vinegar
  • 4 tsp salt
  • 1 cup soya sauce
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 tsp pepper

Instructions

  1. Almonds, sesame seeds, and sunflower seeds should be roasted before adding to salad. Cook noodles; rinse off with cold water, then fry. Mix everything together shortly before serving. Stir every few minutes so that sauce soaks in.
  2. Notes
  3. Only needs half of dressing
