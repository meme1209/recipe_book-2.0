Cornbread Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup butter or margarine
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 2/3 cup milk
  • 2 1/3 cups flour
  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 4 ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Cream butter and sugar. Combine eggs and milk. Combine flour, cornmeal, baking powder and salt; add to creamed mixture alternately with egg mixture. Pour into a greased 9x13 inch cake pan. Bake @ 325º for 25-30 minutes.
  • Toppings
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup Ranch dressing
  • 2 (15 oz) cans garbanzo or pinto beans, rinsed & drained
  • 2 (15 oz) cans kernel corn
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cup green peppers, diced
  • ¼ cup purple onions, chopped
  • 10 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled
  • 2 cups shredded cheese
  • Combine mayonnaise, sour cream and Ranch. Set aside.
  • Crumble ½ of cornbread into pan. Layer with ½ the beans, mayo mixture, corn, tomatoes, peppers, onions, bacon and cheese. Repeat layers. Dish will be very full. Cover and refrigerate 2 hours.
