Pacific Rim Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 heads romaine lettuce, finely chopped
  • 8 cups bean sprouts
  • 10 lbs chicken, bite size pieces, seasoned with seasoning salt
  • 10 red, green, orange and yellow peppers, slivered
  • 4 cups carrots, julienned
  • 2 bundles onion greens, chopped
  • 1 cup water chestnuts
  • 2 packs wonton wraps, cut into ½ inch strips and fried
  • 1 cup slivered almonds
  • 2 cups hoisin sauce
  • ½ cup oil
  • ½ cup vinegar

Instructions

  1. Toss together first 7 ingredients.
  2. Stir together last 3 ingredients.
  3. Pour dressing over vegetables.
  4. Add almonds and wonton wraps just before serving.
  5. Notes
  6. Add 1 cup Italian salad dressing and add a dash of sugar if dressing is too strong.
