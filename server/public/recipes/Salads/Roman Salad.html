Roman Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • ¾ cup vinegar
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 5 packages soup flavour mix
  • Chicken soup base

Instructions

  1. Boil above ingredients for 3 ½ minutes. Mixture should be syrupy and oil should not separate from vinegar. Cool. Finely slice to head of cabbage. Pour dressing over and toss. Just before serving, add 4 packages Roman noodle and 2 cups roasted sunflower seeds.
