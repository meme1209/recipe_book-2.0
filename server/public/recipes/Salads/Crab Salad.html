Crab Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups macaroni shells
  • 2 cups peppers
  • 12 cups celery
  • 3 cups onions
  • 6 lbs crab
  • 1 ½ lbs peas
  • 4 cups miracle whip
  • 1 cup condensed milk
  • 2/3 cup mustard

Instructions

  1. Cook shells first, drain and cool. Cut crabmeat, celery, peppers and onions into small pieces. Mix all ingredients together. Refrigerate at least 2 hours before serving
