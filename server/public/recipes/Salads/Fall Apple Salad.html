Fall Apple Salad

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 apples, cut into bit size pieces
  • 8 oz Cool Whip
  • 1 cup slivered almonds, roasted
  • 8 oz can crushed pineapple with juice
  • 1 TBSP flour
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 TBSP vinegar
  • 1 egg

Instructions

  1. Combine pineapple, flour, vinegar, sugar and egg. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly until boiling and thickened. Refrigerate to cool. When completely cooled, fold in apples, Cool Whip, and ½ cup almonds. Sprinkle other ½-cup almonds on top and refrigerate.
  2. Notes
  3. Make x4
