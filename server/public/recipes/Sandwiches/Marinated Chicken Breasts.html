Marinated Chicken Breasts

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 20 lbs chicken breasts, sliced
  • 1 gallon water
  • 1 cup tenderizer ( optional)
  • 1 cup soya sauce
  • ½ cup oyster sauce
  • 1 bottle zesty Italian salad dressing

Instructions

  1. Marinade chicken breasts 1-2 hours.
  2. Mix together 1 lb margarine and 2-3 TBSP Montreal Steak Spice.
  3. Spread mixture on both sides of hamburger buns.
  4. Grill meat until juices run clear.
  5. Grill buns until crisp.
  6. Make sandwiches.
  7. Enjoy!
