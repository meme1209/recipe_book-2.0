Instructions

  1. 1 lb loaves of whole wheat bread dough in pie plates. Let rise and bake. Slice loaves in half as you would a bun.
  2. Spread cream cheese paste on one side of the loaf.
  3. Salt, pepper, paprika, garlic salt, basil, parsley---(don’t make too strong)
  4. Spread Cheez Whiz on the other side.
  5. Then layer --- onion greens
  6. Bacon
  7. Ham
  8. Smoked turkey (optional)
  9. Lettuce (shredded)
  10. Mayonnaise (with squeeze bottle)
  11. Bean sprouts
  12. Notes
  13. I make 13 loaves. Cut them twice to make more sandwiches.
  14. Take bread recipe x1/2. 3 quarts water.
  15. Press loaves flat after shaping.
