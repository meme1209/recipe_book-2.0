Mushroom and Swiss

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Hamburger buns
  • Garlic butter
  • Roast beef (thinly sliced)
  • Onions
  • Mushrooms
  • Swiss cheese slices

Instructions

  1. Fry onions and mushrooms until onions are tender. Place beef in oven @ 250° until heated through. Spread butter on both sides of bun. Fry on frying pan until golden brown. Top with meat, onions, mushrooms and cheese.
