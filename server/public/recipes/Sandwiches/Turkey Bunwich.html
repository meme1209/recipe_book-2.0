Turkey Bunwich

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 32 cups chicken or turkey, diced
  • 8 cups celery, sliced
  • 12 cups grated cheese
  • ½ cup parsley
  • 4 tsp salt
  • 1/8 cup onion salt
  • ¼ cup soup base
  • 6 cups mayonnaise
  • Dash pepper
  • Onions (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cut meat into bite size pieces. Grill on skillet until ready. Mix everything together while still hot. Fill hamburger buns, warm in oven with wet cloth on top.
