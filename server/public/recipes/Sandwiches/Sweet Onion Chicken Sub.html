Sweet Onion Chicken Sub

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 30 lbs turkey or chicken, cut into strips
  • 5 cups soya sauce
  • 15 cups pineapple juice
  • 10 tsp garlic powder
  • 10 tsp ginger
  • 10 tsp dry mustard
  • 10 tsp pepper
  • 5 cups brown sugar
  • 7 TBSP honey

Instructions

  1. Mix everything together and bring to a boil; simmer 5 minutes. Cool completely. Pour over chicken strips and let marinade for 10 hours. Discard marinade before grilling meat. Spread bun with mayonnaise and barbeque sauce. Place chicken strips on top of mayo; add stir fried onions and peppers. Top with mozzarella.
  2. Sauce
  3. ¾ cup minced onions
  4. 13 ½ Tbsp Red wine vinegar
  5. 1 ¾ cup white vinegar
  6. 13 ½ TBSP balsamic vinegar
  7. 13 ½ TBSP dry milk powder
  8. 1 ½ cups brown sugar
  9. ¼ cup lemon juice
  10. 5 tsp poppy seed (optional)
  11. 4 tsp salt
  12. 1 TBSP onion powder
  13. ½ cup cornstarch (more if needed)
  14. Bring to a boil.
  15. Notes
  16. I made bun recipe (3 quarts) with ½ cup taco seasoning in dough. Made like sub bun, and pressed flat.
  17. I replaced red wine and balsamic vinegars with white vinegar.
  18. ½ recipe sauce is enough.
  19. Needs 100 sandwiches.
