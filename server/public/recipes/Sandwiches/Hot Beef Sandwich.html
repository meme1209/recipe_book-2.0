Hot Beef Sandwich

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Ketchup
  • Mustard
  • Onion, finely chopped

Instructions

  1. Stir together top ingredients.
  2. Spread on one side of burger bun.
  3. Top with a slice of white cheese.
  4. Top with thinly sliced beef.
  5. Warm in oven until cheese is melted.
