Broiler Sandwich

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 30 lbs broiler breasts
  • 4 cups Italian dressing
  • 1 cup soya sauce
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup seasoning salt
  • 1/8 cup tenderizer (optional
  • 5 cups mayonnaise
  • 1 TBSP mustard
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Marinade meat in first 5 ingredients for at least 6 hours. Fry on skillet until ready. Mix mayonnaise, mustard, salt and pepper. Spread on bun. Add a slice of mozzarella or Swiss cheese. Add meat and warm sandwich in oven.
