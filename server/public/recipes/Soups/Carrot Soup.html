Carrot Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 19 cups shredded carrots
  • 3 cups finely chopped onions
  • ¾ cup flour
  • ¾ cup margarine
  • 6 cups shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook carrots in soup until very soft.
  2. Melt margarine and use to sauté onions until soft.
  3. Stir in flour and seasonings.
  4. Add carrots and onions.
  5. Melt cheese in milk and add to soup.
  6. Notes
  7. For seasonings, use2 cups mushroom soup base, chicken and beef soup base, salt and pepper.
