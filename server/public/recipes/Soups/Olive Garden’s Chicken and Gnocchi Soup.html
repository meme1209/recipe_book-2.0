Olive Garden’s Chicken and Gnocchi Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 TBSP olive oil
  • ¼ cup butter
  • ¼ cup flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • ½ cup celery, finely diced
  • ½ cup onion, finely diced
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 2-3 cups broth
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 1-2 cups shredded chicken
  • 1 package gnocchi, cooked
  • Handful of spinach, ribboned
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp thyme
  • ½ tsp parsley

Instructions

  1. Heat butter and olive oil. Sautee onions and celery and garlic until tender. Stir in flour and cook for a minute, before adding milk. Add carrots and chicken; let simmer. Add chicken broth to desired consistency. Add gnocchi. Simmer until heated through.
