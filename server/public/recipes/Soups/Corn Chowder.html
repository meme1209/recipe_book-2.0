Corn Chowder

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 packages bacon
  • 1 large onions
  • 3 jars corn
  • 16 cups diced potatoes
  • 1-2 big cans mushroom soup
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Milk

Instructions

  1. Sautee bacon and onions until onions are clear.
  2. Mix everything together and bring to a boil.
  3. Boil until potatoes are soft.
  4. Notes
  5. Put everything into soup kettle at breakfast.
