Zucchini Chowder

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 20 cups zucchini, chopped
  • 4 cups onions, chopped
  • 1 jar corn
  • 2 cups tomatoes, diced
  • 2 ½ cups cheddar cheese, shredded
  • ½ cup parsley, minced
  • 2 TBSP dried basil
  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • 1 ½ cups butter
  • 15 cups soup
  • 12 cups milk
  • 2 cups cream
  • ½ cup lemon juice
  • Salt, pepper and soup base to taste

Instructions

  1. Sautee zucchini and onions in butter until tender; stir in flour.
  2. Heat soup, milk and cream in soup kettle; then add corn and vegetables.
  3. Add parsley and basil to broth.
  4. Just before serving, stir in cheese.
