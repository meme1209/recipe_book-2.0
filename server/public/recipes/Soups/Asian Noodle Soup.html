Asian Noodle Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups onions, chopped
  • 2 cups celery, diced
  • 2 cups peppers, chopped
  • 2 cups grated carrots
  • 2 cups onion greens, thinly sliced
  • Bean sprouts
  • Cooking oil
  • Minced garlic
  • Grated ginger root
  • Beef soup
  • Chicken gravy
  • Dried chili flakes
  • Beef base
  • Beef tenderloin, thinly sliced
  • Rice vermicelli noodle

Instructions

  1. Marinade beef with soya sauce, olive oil and minced garlic.
  2. Sautee onions, celery and peppers in oil with minced garlic and ginger. Mix chicken gravy into beef soup for a better, fuller flavor. Add chili flakes, soup should be a bit hot. Put meat and onions and sautéed vegetables in soup. Also add minced garlic and ginger if you wish, to taste.
  3. Add grated carrots just before serving.
  4. Cover rice noodle with boiling water, and let stand until soft; do not cook.
  5. Put hot rice noodle and bean sprouts into soup bowl and add soup.
