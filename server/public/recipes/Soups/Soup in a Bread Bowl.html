Soup in a Bread Bowl

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 18 cups potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 15 cups broccoli florets (I used parsnips)
  • 6 cups onions, chopped
  • 6 cups carrots, diced
  • 12 ribs celery, sliced (I used 2 jars canned celery)
  • ½ cup chicken soup base
  • 18 cups soup
  • 4 ½ cups margarine
  • 2 ½ cups cornstarch
  • 8 cups milk
  • 1 TBSP salt
  • 1 ½ tsp pepper
  • 6 cups ham cubed
  • 6 cups shredded cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. In a soup kettle, combine potatoes, parsnips, onions, carrots, celery, soup base and soup. Simmer for 20 minutes or until vegetables are tender. In a saucepan, melt margarine; stir in cornstarch. Cook and stir over medium heat for 2 minutes. Add to soup with the ham and milk. Simmer 10 minutes, and then stir in cheese until just melted. Put this soup into bun.
  2. Notes
  3. This recipe is just right for about 100 people
  4. When making buns use about 3 quarts liquid
