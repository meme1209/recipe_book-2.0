Thai Beef Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 14 lbs ground beef
  • 2 quarts broccoli
  • 2 quarts cauliflower
  • 2 quarts finely shredded cabbage
  • 1 quart sliced green onions
  • 1 quart julienned carrots
  • 1 quart julienned red peppers
  • 3 packages rice noodles

Instructions

  1. For meatballs:
  2. Mix together beef, garlic powder, pepper, salt and ginger to taste. Add 10 eggs, 3 cups water and 3 cups crumbs to meatballs. They should have a noticeable flavor. Drop bite size meat balls into simmering broth.
  3. For soup:
  4. Bring broth to simmer. Add lemon juice; about ¾ to 1 cup, until soup has a very light lemony taste. Add 1 cup brown sugar, ginger, soya sauce and hot sauce or crushed chilies to taste. Soup should have a strong flavor with ginger standing out slightly. Add meatballs. When ready, add cabbage. Add remaining veggies and simmer until tender; then add rice noodles. (no need to cook noodles beforehand). Don’t let overcook.
