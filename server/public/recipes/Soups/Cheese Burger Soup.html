Cheese Burger Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ 4 ½ lbs ground beef
  • 3 ¼ 6 ½ cups chopped onion
  • 3 ¼ 6 ½ cups chopped celery
  • 3 ¼ 6 ½ cups sliced carrots
  • 1 2 TBSP dried basil
  • 1 2 TBSP dried parsley
  • 1 2 cups butter
  • 12 ¾ 25 ½ cups chicken broth
  • 15 30 cups cubed potatoes
  • 1 2 cups flour
  • 8 ½ 17 cups cubed cheddar cheese
  • 6 ½ 13 cups milk
  • 1 2 cups sour cream

Instructions

  1. in a large pot, melt half the butter. Cook vegetables until tender and meat until brown. Stir in basil and parsley. Add broth and potatoes. Bring to a boil and simmer until potatoes are tender. Melt remaining butter and stir in flour and milk, stirring until smooth. Gradually add milk mixture to soup, stirring constantly. Bring to boil and reduce heat to simmer. Stir in cheese. When cheese is melted, add sour cream and heat through.
  2. DO NOT BOIL!
