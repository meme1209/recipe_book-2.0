Cream of Chicken Rice Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 medium onions, chopped
  • 10 celery ribs, chopped
  • 10 carrots, chopped
  • 2 cups margarine
  • 2 cups flour
  • 8 quarts chicken broth
  • 8 cups cooked rice
  • 10 cups light cream
  • 8 cups diced chicken
  • 5 tsp parsley flakes
  • 2 ½ tsp salt
  • 1 ¼ tsp pepper
  • Chicken base to taste

Instructions

  1. Sautee onions, celery and carrots in butter until onions are transparent; reduce heat, blend in flour and cook until bubbly. Gradually add to soup, stirring constantly. Bring to a boil and boil for 1 minute. Reduce heat and add cream, chicken, parsley, salt and pepper. Simmer for 20 minutes. Add rice.
  2. Notes
  3. This soup is also good without meat.
