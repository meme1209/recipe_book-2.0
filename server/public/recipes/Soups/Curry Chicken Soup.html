Curry Chicken Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups butter
  • 8 cups chopped onions
  • 1/3 cup curry powder
  • 1 cup water
  • ¼ cup chicken soup base
  • 4 cups mushroom soup base
  • 2 jars tomatoes, drained and broken up
  • 8 cups chicken, cubed
  • ½ amount milk and ½ amount soup

Instructions

  1. In saucepan, sauté onions in butter with curry powder.
  2. Add water and both bases.
  3. Stir into soup and milk, and add rest of ingredients.
  4. Serve with rice.
