Baked Potato Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 gallons soup
  • 1 cup cheese whiz
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 5 TBSP basil, or to taste
  • Diced potatoes

Instructions

  1. Thicken soup slightly.
  2. Boil potatoes until soft.
  3. Add spices.
  4. Serve with a dab of sour cream, bacon bits, shredded cheddar cheese and chives.
