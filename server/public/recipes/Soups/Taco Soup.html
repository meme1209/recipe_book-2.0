Taco Soup

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups pinto beans
  • 16 cups black beans or chilli beans
  • 8 cups kidney beans
  • 8 cups corn
  • 16 cups crushed tomatoes
  • 8 TBSP Ranch dressing mix
  • 4 lbs hamburger
  • 3 TBSP chilli powder
  • 3 TBSP taco seasoning
  • 8 cups salsa (use hot)
  • Sautee
  • 4 cups onions
  • 4 cups celery
  • 4 cups green peppers

Instructions

  1. Combine beans, tomatoes, corn, spices and sautéed vegetables in a large pan. Fry hamburger, drain and add to other ingredients. Simmer in combi @ 325°. Put into combi at 3: 00 to cook until supper. Serve with tortilla chips and shredded cheese.
  2. Notes
  3. Do not drain anything except hamburger.
