Dumplings

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 8 cups soft margarine
  • 1 8 cups eggs
  • 1 8 cups egg yolks
  • 3 ½ -4 25-32 cups flour
  • ½ 4 tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Whip margarine until fluffy.
  2. Add eggs and yolks together with flour, baking powder and margarine.
  3. Mix together with hands.
  4. Shape into balls.
  5. Drop into boiling soup.
  6. Cover and simmer for 12 minutes.
  7. Notes
  8. Use large recipe for Heartland.
