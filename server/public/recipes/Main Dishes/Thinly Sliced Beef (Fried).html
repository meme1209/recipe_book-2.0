Thinly Sliced Beef (Fried)

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Thinly sliced beef
  • 2 cups soya sauce
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • 1 cup barbecue seasoning

Instructions

  1. Stir well and let stand half a day
  2. Mix same amount of dry breadcrumbs and flour. Put 7 eggs into meat, stirring well. Then bread the meat and fry on high very quickly, until crisp. Just before serving pour sweet and sour over meat.
