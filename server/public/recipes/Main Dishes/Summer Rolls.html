Summer Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 pork loins
  • 3 packages crab
  • 1 gallon head lettuce, thinly sliced
  • 1 gallon shredded carrots
  • 1 gallon shredded cucumbers
  • 2 packages vermicelli noodle
  • 4 packages rice papers
  • Notes
  • Marinate loins with lean pork seasoning for 48 hours.
  • Give left-over loin to kids.
  • Use 16 cm wraps.
  • Sauce
  • 1 ½ cup thick teriyaki sauce
  • 3 cups soya sauce
  • 1 1/8 cups peanut butter
  • ¾ cup water
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • 3 cups white sugar
  • 1 2/3 cups ketchup
  • ¾ tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 TBSP garlic powder
  • ½ TBSP salt
  • 1 ½ cup vegetable oil
  • ¾ cup vinegar
  • 3 TBSP grated onion
  • 1 ½ TBSP Worcestershire sauce
  • Mix together in blender until smooth.
