Honey Ginger Beef or Pork

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • 1 cup soya sauce
  • 1 TBSP garlic powder or 8 minced cloves
  • 2 TBSP tenderizer
  • 25-30 lbs beef or pork
  • 7 eggs, flour, crumbs. Quick fry meat on 325°.
  • Sauce
  • 10 cups soup
  • 1 ½ cups honey ginger sauce
  • 3 cups soya sauce
  • 6 cups honey
  • 1 ½ cups grated fresh ginger
  • 2 TBSP basil
  • 6 TBSP parsley
  • 1 tsp lemon pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil for 10 minutes. Add 1 ½ cups cornstarch with soup. Then toss the meat with sauce, sprinkle with sesame seeds and bake for a few minutes.
  2. Notes
  3. Half of ginger is enough if using fresh grated.
  4. Couldn’t find honey ginger sauce anywhere.
  5. Served sauce on the side. Doubled recipe.
