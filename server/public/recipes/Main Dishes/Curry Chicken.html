Curry Chicken

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 36 cups soup
  • 2 ¾ cups soya sauce
  • 2 ¾ cups honey
  • ¼ cup curry powder
  • ¼ cup ginger
  • 7 TBSP salt

Instructions

  1. Bread chicken and fry.
  2. Mix together above ingredients and thicken with 2 ½ cups flour.
  3. Pour over chicken.
  4. Let simmer for 1 hour @ 450°.
