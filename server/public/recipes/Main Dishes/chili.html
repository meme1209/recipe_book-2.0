Classic Chili

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained
  • 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, brown the ground beef over medium heat. Drain excess fat.
  2. Add chopped onion and garlic. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. Add beans and diced tomatoes. Stir well.
  5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with toppings like shredded cheese, sour cream, or green onions.
