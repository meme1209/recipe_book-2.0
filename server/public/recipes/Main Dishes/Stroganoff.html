Stroganoff

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 12 lbs ground beef or pork
  • 12 cups chopped onions
  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • ¼ cup salt
  • 4 ½ lbs mushrooms
  • 1 TBSP pepper
  • 4 ½ lbs cream of mushroom or chicken soup
  • 6 cups sour cream
  • 4 cups grated cheese
  • 1 cup minced parsley

Instructions

  1. Sautee onions until tender. Fry meat until no longer pink. Stir in flour, salt, pepper, mushrooms and onions. Add soup, sour cream and grated cheese. Heat thoroughly.
  2. Serve with spaghetti or rice.
