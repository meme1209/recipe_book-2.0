Hamburger

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 gallon ground pork
  • 1 gallon ground beef
  • 15 eggs
  • ½ gallon water
  • ½ gallon dried breadcrumbs
  • ¼ cup seasoning salt
  • ¼ cup salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Stir all ingredients together and form in to patties.
  2. Fry and enjoy.
