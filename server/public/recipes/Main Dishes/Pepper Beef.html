Pepper Beef

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Take a beef roast and cut into ½-inch slabs
  2. Mix together tenderizer and pepper and sprinkle onto beef.
  3. Let stand for about 2 nights.
  4. Fry over quickly on high heat to seal spices in. Lay in layers into pan. Pour some soup over, cover with foil and bake @ 250° from dinner until supper or until tender.
  5. Drain off soup and use to make a thin gravy with oyster sauce.
  6. Pour over meat and serve.
