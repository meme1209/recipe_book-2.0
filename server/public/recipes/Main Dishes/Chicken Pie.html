Chicken Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Potatoes
  • Carrots
  • Parsnips
  • Onions

Instructions

  1. Steam vegetables and mix together. Needs about 1 medium-height steamer tray.
  2. Make a strong gravy.
  3. Mix chicken into vegetables.
  4. Fill pie plates
  5. Pour gravy over, finish pies and bake.
  6. Notes
  7. Made 16 marches 2009. Lots of company. 12 would have been enough.
