Shepherd’s Pie

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef or pork
  • 1 can (10 3/4oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup, undiluted
  • ¼ cup onion, chopped
  • 1 egg
  • ¼ cup dry bread crumbs
  • 1 TBSP parsley
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1/8 tsp pepper
  • 2 cups mashed potatoes
  • 4 bacon strips, crumbled
  • ½ cup cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Combine meat, ½ cup soup, onion, egg, breadcrumbs, parsley, salt and pepper. Mix well. Press onto bottom of plate. Bake @ 350° for 25 minutes. Drain. Combine potatoes and remaining soup. Mix until fluffy (I added margarine, milk, and spices). Spread over meat. Sprinkle with cheese and bacon. Bake until cheese is melted.
