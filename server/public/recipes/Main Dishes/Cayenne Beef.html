Cayenne Beef

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 30 lbs beef thinly sliced
  • 1 ¾ cup corn starch
  • 2 ½ cups soya sauce
  • 7 TBSP sugar
  • 3 TBSP garlic
  • 3 TBSP tenderizer
  • 1 1/3 cup cornstarch
  • 3 ¼ cup soya sauce
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 10 cups soup

Instructions

  1. Put meat through tenderizer if too thick. (mine was about 1/8 inch thick, but was too tough). Mix rest of ingredients together and pour over beef. Marinade for 2 days. Fry over quickly in skillet.
  2. Sauce
  3. Cook until thick and pour over beef.
  4. Notes
  5. Make sure sauce is not too thin
