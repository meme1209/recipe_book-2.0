Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Take a soft tortilla wrap
  2. Spread with mild salsa
  3. Scatter with hard, crumbled tacos
  4. Pile on some ground beef (fried with taco seasoning)
  5. Squeeze a cheese sauce on meat
  6. Top with shredded lettuce
  7. Add tomatoes
  8. Fold edges in 5 times
  9. Fry on skillet until heated through
