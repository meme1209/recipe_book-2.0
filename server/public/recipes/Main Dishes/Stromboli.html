Stromboli

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1/4 recipe bread dough with 3 TBSP thyme and basil (each) added (use about 4 quarts water)
  • Sliced ham
  • Sliced pepperoni
  • Sliced salami
  • Chopped onion
  • Pizza sauce
  • Sliced Swiss cheese
  • Dried oregano (optional)
  • Garlic powder (optional)
  • Pepper (optional)

Instructions

  1. Let dough rise until double, punch down. Roll dough into flat 15” x 12” rectangle. Layer ham and pepperoni on half of the dough (lengthwise). Top with pizza sauce. Layer mozzarella, salami, and Swiss cheese over sauce. Sprinkle with seasonings (very sparingly). Spread pizza sauce on top. Fold other half of dough over filling and seal edges well. Place on bun tray with parchment paper. Bake @ 375° for 30-35 minutes or until golden brown. Brush with melted butter. Heat the remaining pizza sauce and serve with sliced Stromboli
  2. Notes
