Ginger Beef

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Beef, cut into thin strips
  • ½ cup cornstarch
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 TBSP soya sauce
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 ½ tsp finely grated ginger root
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tsp cornstarch
  • ½ cup honey
  • 1 ½ TBSP oyster sauce
  • ½ tsp dried crushed chillies
  • 2 tsp sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Beat everything together by hand. Pour over meat; let stand 30 minutes before frying. Fry meat 1 ½-2 minutes or until golden brown.
  2. Sauce x6
  3. Bring to boil and pour over fried meat. Can also add thin sliced carrots and onions to the meat.
