Prime Rib

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 7 cups beef base
  • 1 cup roast beef rub
  • 1 ½ cups seasoning salt
  • Pepper
  • Montreal steak spice

Instructions

  1. Combine first 3 ingredients. Add enough soup to make a thick paste.
  2. Sprinkle heavily with pepper and lighter with steak spice.
  3. Notes
  4. Easter 2010 – 6 pieces with bone. 110 lbs.
  5. Au Jus from Superstore
