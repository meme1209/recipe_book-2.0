Chicken Burger

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 gallons chicken
  • ¼ cup salt and pepper
  • ¼ cup seasoning salt
  • 1/8 cup onion salt
  • 4 cups crumbs, soaked in
  • 12 cups broth
  • ¼ cup soup base
  • 1 cup chicken fat
  • ½ cup flour

Instructions

  1. Mix everything together. It should be thin. Add more water if needed. A little bit of water on tray helps loosen burgers when ready to fry.
