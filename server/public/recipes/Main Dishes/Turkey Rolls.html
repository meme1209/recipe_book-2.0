Turkey Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 16 ½ lbs ground turkey
  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • 1 ½ cups soya sauce
  • 17 eggs
  • ¾ cup seasoning salt
  • 20 ¼ cup finely chopped cabbage
  • 1 ½ cups vegetable oil
  • ¾ TBSP minced garlic

Instructions

  1. Mix eggs, flour, soya sauce and seasoning salt together.
  2. Add oil and garlic.
  3. Beat with hand beater until smooth.
  4. Add to meat and cabbage.
