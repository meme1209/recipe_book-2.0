Chicken Rice Balls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 15 cups cooked chicken
  • 15 cups cooked rice (approx. 6 cups uncooked)
  • 15 cups shredded cheese
  • 15 cups celery, sliced
  • 15 cups onions
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Seasoning salt

Instructions

  1. Mix chicken and rice. Sautee onions and celery. While still hot, stir into rice so that cheese melts and everything sticks together. Form balls with ice cream scoop; press firmly. Dip into eggs and roll in seasoned breadcrumbs. Refrigerate for a couple of hours. Fry until golden. Serve with sweet and sour.
