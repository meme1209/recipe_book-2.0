Classic Lasagna

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef. Drain excess fat and stir in marinara sauce. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese, egg, Parmesan, salt, and pepper.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a 9x13 baking dish. Layer 3 noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat layers twice more.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake another 15 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  7. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.
