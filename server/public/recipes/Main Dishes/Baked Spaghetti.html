Baked Spaghetti

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 cups onion, chopped
  • 6 cups mushrooms
  • 14 cups pasta sauce
  • 15 cups shredded cheese
  • 12 cups cream of mushroom soup
  • Spaghetti noodle (6 lbs is enough)
  • 6 lbs hamburger meat (I used about 20 lbs)
  • 3 green peppers, cut up
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook noodle and brown hamburger. Fry onions and mushrooms until tender. Fill “tea Tupf” about half full (not quite) with pasta sauce. Add hamburger, onions, mushrooms and peppers. Simmer for 10 minutes. Place half spaghetti noodle in greased pan. Top with half veggie mixture. Sprinkle with half cheese mixture. Repeat the layers. Pour mushroom soup over casserole. Sprinkle with cheese. Bake @ 300° until heated through.
  2. Notes
  3. Put together at dinner, so it’s ready for oven right at 4: 00.
