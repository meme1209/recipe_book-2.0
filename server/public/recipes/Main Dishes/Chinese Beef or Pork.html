Chinese Beef or Pork

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 5 ¼ cup cornstarch
  • 3 ¾ cup soya sauce
  • 2 ¾ cup sugar
  • 10 TBSP minced garlic
  • 10 TBSP tenderizer
  • 4 cups cornstarch
  • 20 cups brown sugar
  • 40 cups strong soup
  • 8 cups soya sauce
  • 4 cups molasses
  • 1 ½ cups garlic

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and pour over meat. Let marinade overnight.
  2. Sauce
  3. Mix all ingredients together and bring to boil. Boil until thickened. Pour over grilled meat.
