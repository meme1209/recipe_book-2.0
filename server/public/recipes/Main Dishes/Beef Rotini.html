Beef Rotini

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 20 cups uncooked rotini pasta
  • 10 lbs ground beef
  • 20 cups sliced mushrooms
  • 10 10-oz cans tomato soup
  • 2 ½ cups water
  • 10 tsp dried oregano
  • 5 tsp garlic powder
  • 10 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions without salt. Drain and set aside. Brown beef, mushrooms and onions. Stir in mixture of soup and water, oregano and garlic powder. Heat to a boil, stirring often. Add cooked pasta. Reduce heat to low. Simmer covered until mixture is heated through, about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Top with cheese and sprinkle with chopped herbs or cracked pepper.
