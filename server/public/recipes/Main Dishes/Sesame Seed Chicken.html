Sesame Seed Chicken

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 gallon strong soup
  • 1 cup soy sauce
  • 2 TBSP curry powder
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 3 cups honey
  • 1 tsp red pepper
  • 1 ¼ cups vinegar
  • 1 ½ cups cornstarch
  • 3 cups sesame seeds
  • Batter for meat x2
  • 5 cups flour
  • 2 ½ cups cornstarch
  • 5 cups cold water
  • 3 TBSP salt
  • 10 eggs

Instructions

  1. Beat until smooth. Do not let pieces touch when frying. Pour sauce over meat just before serving. Serve with spaghetti.
  2. Notes
  3. Do not put chicken in milk and eggs. Just season.
