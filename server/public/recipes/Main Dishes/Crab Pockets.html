Crab Pockets

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ 9 lbs crab meat
  • 2 ¼ 9 lbs cream cheese
  • ¼ 1 TBSP garlic powder
  • ½ 3 TBSP chives
  • ¾ 3 TBSP Hidden Valley Ranch powder

Instructions

  1. Mix cream cheese, garlic powder, chives and ranch powder.
  2. Add crabmeat.
  3. Chill.
  4. Wrap in Won Ton wraps.
  5. Fry.
  6. Notes
  7. Make large recipe for Heartland.
