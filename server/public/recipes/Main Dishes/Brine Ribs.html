Brine Ribs

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups salt
  • 3 cups brown sugar
  • ¼ cup tenderizer
  • 4 tsp prague salt
  • 4 gallons water

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together.
  2. Marinade ribs for 36 hours.
  3. Remove from brine.
  4. Brush lightly with barbeque sauce.
  5. Bake @ 250° for 1 hour or until ribs are tender.
