Wontons With Sweet and Sour

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 lbs ground pork (needs more)
  • 12 cups cabbage, finely chopped
  • 3 small onions, finely chopped
  • 20 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1/8 cup salt
  • 1 TBSP pepper
  • Wonton wraps
  • Sweet and Sour
  • Pineapple juice
  • 6 cups brown sugar, packed
  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • 2 cups vinegar
  • 2 cups water
  • ¾ cup soya sauce
  • 3 cups green peppers, chopped

Instructions

  1. combine brown sugar and cornstarch. Stir in juice, vinegar, water, and soya sauce until smooth. Bring to boil. Cook and stir until thickened. Reduce heat; stir in peppers.
