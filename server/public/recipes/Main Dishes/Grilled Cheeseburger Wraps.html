Grilled Cheeseburger Wraps

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 20 lbs ground beef
  • 1 20 TBSP Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 20 TBSP ketchup
  • 1 20 tsp dried minced onion
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Flour tortillas
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Sliced tomatoes
  • Sliced pickles
  • Mustard

Instructions

  1. Brown ground beef in large skillet and drain. Add Worcestershire sauce, ketchup, dried onion, salt and pepper to the beef and simmer 5 minutes.
  2. Sprinkle shredded cheese down the center of each tortilla. Add a few heaping tablespoons of the beef mixture on top of cheese. Spread some mustard on top of beef along with tomatoes and pickles. Roll up tortilla burrito style and grill.
  3. Grill for about 5 minutes until cheese is melted.
