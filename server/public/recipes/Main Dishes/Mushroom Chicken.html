Mushroom Chicken

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • Enough chicken as for KFC; season as usual
  • Layer in pans, onions underneath. Water as usual.
  • Bake in combi @ 320º for 30 minutes.
  • Pour off liquids and make mushroom gravy
  • 10 quarts of the juices
  • 6 quarts milk
  • 5 cups flour
  • 8 cups mushroom soup base
  • ½ gallon mushrooms
  • Pour gravy over chicken and return to combi for 30 minutes.
