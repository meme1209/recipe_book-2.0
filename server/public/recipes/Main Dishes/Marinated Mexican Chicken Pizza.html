Marinated Mexican Chicken Pizza

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups lemon juice
  • 2 cups canola oil
  • ½ cup minced garlic
  • ¼ cup chilli powder
  • 6 TBSP salt and pepper
  • 2 cups oyster sauce
  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • 18 lbs bacon, fried and crumbled
  • 24 lbs chicken breast
  • 16 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 6 cups salsa
  • 24 cups finely diced green peppers
  • 6 cups finely chopped onions
  • 15 cups shredded lettuce
  • 20 cups diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups sour cream

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients, mixing well. Cut chicken breasts into ½-inch square pieces. Toss with mixture and let marinade in fridge for 2-4 hours. Stir-fry chicken until juice runs clear. Set aside.
  2. Pizza
  3. Arrange tortillas on baking sheet. Sprinkle half of cheese over tortillas. Bake until golden brown- about 6 minutes. Meanwhile, combine chicken, salsa, peppers and onions and mix well. Spoon mixture onto warm tortillas. Top with cheese and bake again until heated through, about 5 minutes. Top with lettuce, tomatoes and a dollop of sour cream. Serve immediately.
  4. Makes 24 pizzas. Needs about 100 small tortillas
