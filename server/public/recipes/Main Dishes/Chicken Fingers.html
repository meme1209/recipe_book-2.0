Chicken Fingers

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cup margarine, melted
  • 4 cups flour
  • 1/8 cup salt (or less)
  • 12 cups milk
  • 24 cups chopped chicken, raw
  • ¾ cup onion, finely chopped
  • ¾ cup chicken soup base

Instructions

  1. Melt margarine. Blend in flour; add salt and milk. Cook over low heat until thick. Cool, then add chicken, onions, and base. Shape into fingers. Dip in eggs and then crumbs. Deep fry.
  2. Notes
  3. Cook milk and flour mixture at breakfast so it cools off.
  4. Make sure chicken is unsalted before adding seasonings.
