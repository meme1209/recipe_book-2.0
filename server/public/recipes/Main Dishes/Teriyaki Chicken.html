Teriyaki Chicken

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 6 quarts pineapple juice
  • 2 4 quarts soya sauce
  • 2 ½ 5 cups brown sugar
  • 1 2 TBSP garlic powder
  • 3 6 TBSP pepper
  • 2 4 TBSP liquid smoke (optional)
  • 1 2 TBSP ginger

Instructions

  1. Soak meat in marinade for 72 hours. Line pans with foil and grease the foil. Place meat into pans, and pour a little bit of marinade over. Don’t cover chicken totally. Bake @250° for about 2- 2 ½ hours or until tender.
  2. Notes
  3. Small recipe for 30 lbs meat
  4. Large recipe for 70 lbs meat
  5. We use large recipe
