Texas Toast

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 TBSP yeast
  • 13 cups water
  • 27 cups flour
  • 1 ½ cups vegetable oil
  • 3 TBSP salt
  • 3 TBSP onion salt
  • 1 TBSP pepper
  • 2 TBSP seasoning salt
  • 3 TBSP parsley flakes
  • 3 TBSP Italian seasoning
  • 1 ½ TBSP sweet basil

Instructions

  1. Let rise 1 hour or until doubled in size. Shape into loafs and place into greased loaf pans. Let rise until doubled. Bake @ 350° for 30-35 minutes. Cool. Cut into slices. Spread with garlic butter and then pizza sauce. Sprinkle with a little bit of mozzarella cheese so that the toppings stick. Then sprinkle with pizza toppings and mozzarella. Bake until heated through.
