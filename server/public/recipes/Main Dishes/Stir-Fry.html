Vegetable Stir Fry

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Optional: sliced chicken, tofu, or beef

Instructions

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add broccoli, bell pepper, snap peas, and carrot. Stir-fry for 5–7 minutes until tender-crisp.
  4. If using meat or tofu, cook separately and add to the vegetables.
  5. Stir in soy sauce and sesame oil. Toss to coat evenly.
  6. Cook for another 1–2 minutes, then remove from heat.
  7. Serve hot over rice or noodles.
