Piggy Wiggies

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 cups half and half cream
  • 10 eggs
  • ½ cup baking powder
  • 1 ½ cups sugar
  • ½ TBSP salt
  • Flour

Instructions

  1. Make dough stiff. Roll out dough and take half a cheese slice and half a wiener. Fold up and fry. Make sure fryer is very hot, about 350°.
  2. Notes
  3. This recipe is big enough for Heartland
