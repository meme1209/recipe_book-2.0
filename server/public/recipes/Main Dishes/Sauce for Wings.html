Sauce for Wings

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 6 cups honey
  • 3 cups BBQ sauce
  • 1 TBSP pepper
  • 1 TBSP chili flakes
  • Minced garlic
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Sautee the garlic in oil 1 minute; do not brown.
  2. Add honey, salt, pepper, chili flakes and BBQ sauce.
  3. Simmer over low heat 10-15 minutes, watching it closely. It can foam up and boil over quickly.
