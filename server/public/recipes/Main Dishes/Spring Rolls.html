Spring Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 1/3 cups uncooked rice (cook)
  • 6 2/3 cups diced celery
  • 12 cups raw chicken, diced as small as possible
  • 4 2/3 cups finely chopped mushrooms
  • 7 cups raw bean sprouts
  • 2 TBSP seasoning salt
  • 2 TBSP onion salt
  • 2 TBSP black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • Notes
  • Make wraps slightly wet when making
  • Needs 6 packages of spring roll wraps with 20/package
  • Fry low
