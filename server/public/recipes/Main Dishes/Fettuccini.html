Fettuccini

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 18 cups heavy cream (or tea Tupf full)
  • 2 cups butter
  • 2 8 oz bricks cream cheese
  • 5 1/3 cups parmesan cheese
  • 7 ½ tsp garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Simmer first 3 ingredients 20 minutes or longer. If it needs thickening, add ½-cup cornstarch.
  2. Notes
  3. Needs stainless steel bucket fettuccini noodle.
