Chicken Turnovers

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups celery, chopped
  • 2 large onions
  • ¾ cup butter
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 TBSP salt
  • Dash pepper
  • 1 ½ tsp ginger
  • 7 ½ cups milk
  • 20 cups cubed chicken
  • 1 cup parsley, chopped
  • 2 lbs bean sprouts (optional)
  • 18 cups flour
  • 6 cups shortening
  • 9 cups sour cream
  • 5 tsp salt
  • 9 cups shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. Sautee celery and onions in ¾ cup butter. Mix flour, salt, pepper, and milk together. Boil until thick. Add vegetables and meat. Add soya sauce to taste.
  2. Dough
  3. Combine salt and flour. Cut in shortening until mixture is crumbly. Stir in cheese and sour cream; mix well. Put in mixer bowl and finish dough, adding flour until manageable. Roll out dough. Cut circle. Place filling in center and fold over. Pinch sides together with fork. Bake @350° for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown. Serve with sweet and sour and/or gravy.
