Honey Garlic Chicken or Ribs

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 ½ cups barbeque sauce
  • 9 cups honey
  • 3 cups brown sugar
  • 3 cups soya sauce
  • 4 ½ TBSP garlic powder
  • 1 TBSP basil
  • 1/8 cup parsley
  • 1 ½ tsp lemon pepper

Instructions

  1. Prepare meat as you usually would. After frying, dip meat in sauce, put in foil lined pans. Bake uncovered @ 350° for about 10-15 minutes.
  2. Notes
  3. Make as is
