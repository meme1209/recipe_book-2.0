Shrimp Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups 30 cups raw shrimp, ground (20 lbs)
  • 1 ½ tsp 10 TBSP corn starch
  • 1 ½ tsp 10 TBSP sugar
  • ¼ tsp 5 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp 7 TBSP salt
  • 1 20 egg yolks
  • ½ tsp 10 tsp oyster sauce
  • ½ tsp 10 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 ½ tsp 10 TBSP vegetable oil
  • 2/3 cup ketchup
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 3-5 TBSP horseradish

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together. Wrap with spring roll wraps or wonton wraps, and deep fry.
  2. Sauce for Shrimp Rolls
