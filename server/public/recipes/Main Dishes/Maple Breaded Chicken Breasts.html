Maple Breaded Chicken Breasts

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup salt
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • ¼ cup prague salt (omit)
  • ½ cup tenderizer (omit)
  • Water – enough to cover meat
  • 4 8 cups minced mushrooms
  • 5 10 cups diced ham
  • 2 4 lbs diced bacon
  • 5 10 cups thinly sliced onions
  • ½ 1 cup minced chives
  • 1 ½ 3 cups melted butter
  • 3 6 cups pancake syrup (use 1/3 portion thick syrup for thickness)
  • 1 2 pinch savory (optional)

Instructions

  1. Stir everything together, pour over meat and let stand overnight
  2. Topping.
  3. Make a short slit thru chicken breasts. Coat breasts with seasoned flour and then pan fry them with butter until they’re nicely browned on both sides, but not well done. Arrange in pans. Next, sauté mushrooms, ham, bacon, onion and chives until slightly crisp; sprinkle over chicken. Drizzle syrup over breasts and add 1 cup water to bottom of pan to create a thin gravy with meat juices. Cover and bake ½ hour or till breasts are done. (160° with probe.)
  4. Notes
  5. I used 40 lbs breasts.
  6. Do not flour.
  7. Used large recipe. Slit breasts if they are too thick. I used bought ones and didn’t slit.
