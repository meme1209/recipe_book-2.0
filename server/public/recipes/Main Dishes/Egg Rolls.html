Egg Rolls

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 quarts cabbage
  • 2 ½ cups onions
  • 5 cups chopped celery
  • 6 TBSP salt
  • 10 cups cooked chicken
  • 1 ½ cups soya sauce
  • 10 TBSP oil
  • ¼ cup pepper

Instructions

  1. Combine cabbage, onions and celery with salt.
  2. Let stand 15 minutes.
  3. Drain off excess liquid.
  4. Add rest of ingredients.
  5. Mix well.
