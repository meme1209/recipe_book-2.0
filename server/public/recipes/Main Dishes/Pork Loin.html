Pork Loin

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • ½ TBSP lemon pepper
  • 1 TBSP garlic pepper
  • ½ cup blended Montreal steak spice (important to blend)
  • ½ cup herb mix
  • 2 TBSP mustard seed
  • 2 TBSP minced garlic
  • Vegetable oil
  • Onions
  • Carrots
  • Potatoes

Instructions

  1. Mix together first 5 ingredients.
  2. Add minced garlic and enough vegetable oil to make a paste, not too thick. Rub pork loin with mixture and marinade for a day.
  3. Cut up vegetables into large chunks; spread over loin.
  4. Preheat oven to 150°. Place loin in oven and cook to 133° with meat probe.
  5. Remove from oven. Remove meat from dish and spoon drippings and veggies into saucepan. Place meat back in pan and cover with foil. Add soup to saucepan and add a little bit of steak spice, garlic, lemon pepper and herb mix. Simmer until ready to pour over meat which has been sliced 1” thick.
  6. Notes
  7. Take spices x3.
  8. Put all meat in combi at 12: 00.
  9. I had about 55 lbs meat. 5 loins. 1 left over.
  10. Marinade large loins 3-4 days
  11. Squirt real lemon over loins to lessen pork taste.
