Steak Lo Mein

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 12 lbs beef, cut into strips
  • 12 cups cabbage, thinly sliced
  • 12 cups carrots, sliced
  • 12 cups celery, sliced
  • 9 cups onions, chopped
  • 9 cups mushrooms
  • Snow peas (optional)
  • 2 lbs spaghetti, cooked and drained
  • 1 ½ TBSP garlic powder
  • 25 cups soup
  • 5/8 cup “each” chicken and beef soup base
  • 5 cups mushroom soup base
  • 5/ 8 cup sugar
  • 7 ½ tsp soya sauce

Instructions

  1. Stir together soya sauce, oyster sauce, salt and pepper. Pour over meat and let marinade at least 10 hours. Put in pan, cover with water, and put in oven from dinner until supper. Tender crisp the vegetables, and toss everything together; pour sauce over.
  2. Sauce
  3. Cook until thick. The mushroom base is the thickener.
