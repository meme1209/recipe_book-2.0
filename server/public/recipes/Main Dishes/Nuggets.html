Nuggets

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 soup dishes ground chicken
  • 16 eggs
  • 5 cups milk
  • ½ cup onion salt
  • 3 TBSP salt and pepper
  • Seasoned bread crumbs

Instructions

  1. Mix together first 5 ingredients.
  2. Roll into balls.
  3. Roll in breadcrumbs and flatten.
  4. Fry @325° until no longer raw in the middle.
