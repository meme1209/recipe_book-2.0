Barbeque and Honey Ribs

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 gallon barbeque sauce
  • 1/3 gallon honey
  • ¼ cup garlic powder
  • 1 cup cornstarch

Instructions

  1. Cook ribs and drain off soup.
  2. Bake ribs @ 350° until nicely browned.
  3. Pour above mixture over ribs 20 minutes before serving.
  4. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  5. Bake for another 20 minutes.
