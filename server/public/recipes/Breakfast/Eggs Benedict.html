Eggs Benedict

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 ½ tsp butter
  • 1 ½ tsp flour
  • ½ cup milk
  • 2 ounces shredded cheese
  • 1/8 tsp pepper
  • 2 English muffins, split

Instructions

  1. Melt butter. Stir in flour and pepper until smooth.
  2. Gradually add milk; bring to boil.
  3. Cook and stir for 1-2 minutes or until thickened.
  4. Remove from heat; stir in cheese until melted.
  5. Notes
  6. X10 for Heartland.
