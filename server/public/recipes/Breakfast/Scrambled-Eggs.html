Scrambled Eggs

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • ¼ cup milk or cream (optional for creaminess)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
  • Optional: chopped herbs, shredded cheese, diced vegetables

Instructions

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl. Add milk, salt, and pepper. Whisk until well combined.
  2. Heat butter or oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Pour in the egg mixture and let it sit for a few seconds.
  4. Gently stir with a spatula, pushing eggs from the edges toward the center.
  5. Continue stirring occasionally until eggs are softly set and slightly glossy.
  6. Remove from heat just before fully cooked — they’ll finish on the plate.
  7. Top with herbs, cheese, or veggies if desired. Serve warm.
