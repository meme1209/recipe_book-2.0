Bacon Potato Pancakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups uncooked potatoes, peeled, and shredded
  • 5 bacon strips, cooked and crumbled
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2 TBSP all-purpose flour
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Dash ground nutmeg
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Rinse and thoroughly drain potatoes. In a bowl combine the potatoes,
  2. bacon, onion, eggs, flour, salt, pepper, and nutmeg. In an electric
  3. skillet, heat 1/8 in. of oil to 375°. Drop batter by 2 heaping
  4. tablespoonfuls into hot oil. Flatten to form patties. Fry until
  5. golden brown; turn and cook the other side. Drain on paper towels.
  6. Yield: 2 dozen
