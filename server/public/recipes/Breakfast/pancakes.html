Pancakes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 40 60 eggs, separated
  • 5 7 ½ cups vegetable oil
  • 3 ¾ 5 2/3 cups sugar
  • 1 1/3 2 cups baking powder (try 1 ½)
  • 6 Tbsp +2 tsp 10 TBSP salt
  • 20 30 cups milk
  • 18 27 cups flour

Instructions

  1. Mix together dry ingredients.
  2. Stir in milk and oil.
  3. Stir in egg yolks just until blended.
  4. The next morning, beat egg whites until stiff and fold into mixture.
  5. Notes
  6. Made large recipe for Heartland March 2012.
