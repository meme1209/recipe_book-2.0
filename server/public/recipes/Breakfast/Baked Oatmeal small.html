Baked Oatmeal small

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups oatmeal
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup margarine, melted
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/3 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together and bake @300° for 30 minutes.
