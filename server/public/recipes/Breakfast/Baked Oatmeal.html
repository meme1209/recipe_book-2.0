Baked Oatmeal

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 7 ½ 11 ¼ 15 18 ¼ cups oatmeal
  • 2 ½ 3 ¾ 5 6 ¼ cups brown sugar
  • 2 ½ 3 ¾ 5 6 ¼ cups milk
  • 1 ¼ 1 7/8 2 ½ 2 ½ +10 TBSP cups margarine, melted
  • 5 7 ½ 10 13 eggs
  • 5 7 ½ 10 12 ½ tsp baking powder
  • 3 4 ½ 6 7 ½ tsp salt
  • 5 7 ½ 10 12 ½ tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix all together and pour into greased baking pan. Small recipe makes 1 steamer tray. Bake @ 350° for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown. Serve with warm milk and warm honey and fruit.
