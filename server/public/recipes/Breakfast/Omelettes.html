Omelettes

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 quarts eggs, beaten
  • ½ cup baking powder
  • 2 cups margarine, melted
  • Salt, pepper, onion salt, seasoning salt
  • Mozzarella
  • Ham
  • Mushrooms
  • Peppers

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs well. Just before using, add margarine, baking powder, and seasonings.
  2. Notes
  3. For supper we need 7-8 quarts eggs.
