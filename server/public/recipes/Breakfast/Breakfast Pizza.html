Breakfast Pizza

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 8 pizza crusts
  • Pizza sauce
  • Cheez Whiz
  • 3 fillers eggs
  • Onions, cut up
  • 8 packages bacon, crumbled
  • Mushrooms, chopped
  • Peppers
  • Mozzarella
  • Tomatoes

Instructions

  1. Make pizza crust half of recipe; about 5 cups water plus the yeast water. Spread crust on pizza plates, and spread with a thin layer of pizza sauce. Bake until edges are golden.
  2. Spread Cheez Whiz on crust.
  3. Fry eggs, leaving very thin. Spread on crust and top with onions, bacon, mushrooms and peppers. Spread mozzarella on top and tomatoes on mozzarella. Bake @ 350° for ½ an hour.
  4. Notes
  5. For kids’ pizza, we only put on eggs, bacon, and cheese.
  6. Kids need at least 3.
  7. Start at 6: 00 a.m.
  8. Needs 16 for supper.
