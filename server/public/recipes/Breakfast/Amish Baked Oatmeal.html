Amish Baked Oatmeal

← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ 12 cups oatmeal
  • ½ 4 cups sugar
  • ½ 4 cups milk
  • ¼ 2 cups butter, melted
  • 1 8 eggs
  • 1 8 tsp baking powder
  • ¾ 3 tsp salt
  • 1 8 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Combine the first 8 ingredients.; mix well.
  2. Spread evenly into a greased 9x13” pan.
  3. Bake @350° for 25-30 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
